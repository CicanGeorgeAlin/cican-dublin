# CICAN DUBLIN ARCHIVE

**Created by CICAN GEORGE ALIN · Dublin, Ireland**

An evidence-first visual archive of Dublin's monuments, memorials, inscriptions, buildings, streets, archaeological remains and overlooked physical details.

## Live website
https://cicangeorgealin.github.io/cican-dublin-archive/

## Current catalogue
The first research batch contains **15 records** across Dublin 1 and Dublin 2, linked to **15 source entries**. Fourteen records are marked VERIFIED against the cited National Inventory of Architectural Heritage records; one record for the individual bronze name plaques around the Famine Memorial is PARTIALLY_VERIFIED because each plaque still requires legible field photography and transcription.

The catalogue is a starting batch, not a complete Dublin inventory. No record is marked photographed, illustrated or published.

## Core mission
Discover, verify and catalogue Dublin's historic physical environment. The founder photographs each location in person. Original photographs are preserved and may be converted into faithful black-and-white A4 line-art illustrations for themed Lulu.com colouring books.

## Production workflow
RESEARCH → VERIFY → REGISTER → PLAN FIELDWORK → FOUNDER PHOTOGRAPHS → ARCHIVE ORIGINAL → CREATE LINE ART → QUALITY CONTROL → WEBSITE → BOOK → PUBLISH.

## Integrity rules
- Every discovery receives one unique archive ID.
- Every historical claim must have a source.
- Research leads and verified records are kept separate.
- Never invent coordinates, object details, images or publication URLs.
- Photography is marked complete only after founder confirmation.
- Preserve original photographs separately from derivative artwork.
- Check duplicates before creating records.
- Respect access, safety, copyright and photography restrictions.

## Repository structure
- `data/objects.json` — master object catalogue.
- `data/districts.json` — 22 geographic research zones.
- `data/categories.json` — discovery taxonomy.
- `data/sources.json` — source registry.
- `routes/` — proposed and completed fieldwork plans.
- `research/` — source-based investigations.
- `photos/originals/` — founder's original photographs.
- `line-art/` — approved illustrations.
- `books/` — publication source files and metadata.
- `documentation/` — project standards and schema.

## Website features
- Responsive black-and-white landing page.
- Search by name, address, district, category and description.
- Category and district filters.
- Individual record detail dialog with source links and photography brief.
- Google Maps search/directions link for each record.
- Local fieldwork list with text export.
- Status counters sourced from the catalogue.

## Geographic and coordinate accuracy
National Grid references are preserved in their own field. Latitude and longitude remain null until reliable conversion or field verification. District lists are research zones, not evidence that those areas have been fully surveyed.
