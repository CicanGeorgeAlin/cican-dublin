# ALWAYS STAY IN THE LIGHT — Permanent CICAN DUBLIN Protocol

**Project:** CICAN DUBLIN  
**Founder:** CICAN GEORGE ALIN  
**Status:** Permanent operating principle  
**Adopted:** 29 October 2026

## Core promise

CICAN DUBLIN exists to bring Dublin's overlooked history into public view: the places, objects, structures, street details, inscriptions, fragments and traces that people may pass every day without knowing their identity or story.

**ALWAYS STAY IN THE LIGHT.**

This is a permanent research and publishing principle, not a temporary campaign or a single-batch instruction.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Discover more.** Do not limit research to the current catalogue, famous landmarks, tourist routes or locations already photographed. Expand systematically across Dublin City and County Dublin.
2. **Evidence before claims.** Do not invent an object, location, date, maker, function, story, coordinate, source or historical significance. Separate verified facts, reasonable interpretations, disputed claims and unknowns.
3. **A missing photograph never blocks a discovery.** If a credible object is documented but no suitable image is available, retain it when identity and evidence requirements are met and prepare it as a founder field-photography target.
4. **Respect image rights.** Inspect the original source and image. Record author, provenance, licence, permission and attribution. Do not import or republish rights-uncleared images. Research leads are not archive assets.
5. **Keep image types separate.** Distinguish founder originals, licensed exact-object references, contextual images, rights-pending leads and no-reference-found records. Never count a third-party image as founder photography.
6. **Verify physical status.** Distinguish objects surviving today from former structures, archaeological discoveries, buried remains, removed features and unverified possible survivals.
7. **Locate responsibly.** Preserve coordinate provenance. A map pin or archival grid reference is not a founder's field GPS fix. Record access, privacy, security and safety restrictions. Never recommend trespass or entry to restricted property.
8. **Protect archive integrity.** Use unique IDs, check duplicates and parent-child relationships, preserve source provenance, validate schemas and references, and never silently overwrite uncertain data.
9. **Expand geography and categories.** Search neighbourhood by neighbourhood and across all relevant Dublin local-authority areas. Include street fabric, architectural micro-details, industrial and transport heritage, archaeology, civic and social history, maritime and rural heritage.
10. **Report honestly.** Publish measurable coverage, unresolved gaps, source limitations and research status. Never claim the archive is complete, the largest or the first unless that is independently demonstrated.

## Autopilot batch workflow

For each user instruction to continue:

1. Read the current repository state and recent batch reports.
2. Identify the next useful research task; do not repeat completed work.
3. Run discovery expansion and visual discovery as parallel objectives.
4. Prioritise new, evidence-supported discoveries as well as image research.
5. Verify identity, historical claims, exact location, current survival status and provenance.
6. Search for exact-object photographs, inspect them, and check reuse rights.
7. If no usable photograph exists, preserve the discovery as a founder fieldwork target with a practical photography brief.
8. Make only evidence-supported repository changes. Keep research leads separate from confirmed catalogue records.
9. Validate affected files, IDs, counts, source links and image-rights fields.
10. Commit a clear batch report and any justified data changes.
11. Report what changed, what remains uncertain, and the next target.

## Interaction rule

The founder may simply reply **“Continue”** to authorise the next research batch under this protocol. Do not ask for repeated approval of the established workflow. Ask a question only when a genuinely essential decision, access permission, rights decision or ambiguous identity cannot responsibly be resolved from available evidence.

Autopilot means continuing the work whenever the founder sends the next instruction. It does not mean background execution between messages or autonomous publishing without an available authorised action.

## Guiding sentence

**Find what is overlooked. Verify what is found. Preserve what is true. Photograph what remains. Keep Dublin's hidden heritage in the light.**
