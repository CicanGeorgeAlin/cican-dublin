## 1. 50010997 — 12 North Great George's Street

**Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010997/north-great-georges-street-dublin-dublin

The NIAH records a terraced three-bay, four-storey house over an exposed basement, completed in 1783. Its fabric includes handmade red brick in Flemish bond, a granite plinth, gauged-brick flat arches, timber sliding sash windows, cast- and wrought-iron balconettes, basement grilles, and a painted stone entrance doorcase with Doric columns and a spoked fanlight. The entrance retains a granite-flagged platform, granite steps, bootscraper and original wrought-iron railings. The appraisal identifies it as a sound late-eighteenth-century Dublin townhouse and notes its association with Sarah Archdall, connected with the Mount Eccles estate.

**Fieldwork priorities:** Full frontage and basement relationship; Doric doorcase and fanlight; balconettes, iron grilles and railings; granite platform, steps and bootscraper; roofline and brick bond. Exterior only from lawful public space; no residential access implied.

## 2. 50010998 — 11 North Great George's Street

**Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010998/north-great-georges-street-dublin-dublin-city

The NIAH describes a terraced three-bay, four-storey house over exposed basement, built in 1774, with a single-storey rear addition. Features include handmade red brick in Flemish bond, a granite plinth, gauged-brick window arches, granite sills, timber sash windows, a Doric doorcase, wrought-iron railings and a basement gate. The record also documents substantial surviving interior plasterwork, including late-Rococo ornament with birds, acanthus leaves and flower baskets, alongside Neo-classical details and a Chinese Chippendale stair. The appraisal records associations with George Whitford and Dr Charles Orpen, and describes careful conservation after a period of decline. A claimed association with Michael Davitt is presented in the NIAH appraisal as a supposition, not a proven fact.

**Fieldwork priorities:** Public-facing façade; doorcase, fanlight and entrance ironwork; sash windows and brickwork; restored public-realm light posts mentioned by the appraisal. Interior details require owner/venue permission and must not be assumed accessible.

## 3. 50011004 — 5 North Great George's Street

**Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50011004/north-great-georges-street-dublin-dublin

The NIAH describes a terraced three-bay, four-storey house over basement, built around 1785 as one of a pair with No. 6. It has a full-height semi-circular rear bay, a concealed slate roof behind a parapet with granite coping, Flemish-bond handmade red brick, gauged-brick window openings, granite sills, sash windows and decorative iron balconettes. The pedimented painted-stone doorcase retains an original leaded spoked fanlight, with granite steps and original wrought-iron railings. The appraisal notes the building was divided into apartments around 1995 and credits the North Great George's Street Preservation Society with helping save it from demolition.

**Fieldwork priorities:** Front elevation and paired relationship with No. 6; doorcase and leaded fanlight; balconettes, railings and basement area; brickwork, granite plinth and roofline. Rear bay should only be photographed from a lawful public or permission-granted viewpoint.

## 4. 50011008 — Dublin and Meath Railway Company, 1 North Great George's Street / Denmark Street Great

**Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50011008/1-north-great-georges-street-denmark-street-great-dublin-1-co-dublin

The NIAH search record identifies a terraced three-bay, four-storey house over exposed basement, built in 1772, with a later three-bay return to the north on Great Denmark Street and a more recent hotel structure to the rear. The record lists Regional rating, Architectural and Artistic interest, original use as a house, historical use as an office and current recorded use as a hotel. The visible description notes a pitched slate roof, rendered parapet with moulded granite coping, shared stepped red-brick chimney stack and ruled-and-lined rendered walls with a moulded granite plinth.

**Fieldwork priorities:** Record the North Great George's Street frontage and Great Denmark Street return as one corner/return composition; parapet and coping; chimney and rainwater goods; plinth and entrance details. Commercial premises: exterior photography from lawful public space; interior access requires permission.

## 5. 50010988 — Bel Canto House, 21 North Great George's Street

**Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010988/bel-canto-house-21-north-great-georges-street-dublin-dublin

The NIAH search record identifies a terraced three-bay, four-storey house dating to approximately 1785, with Regional rating and Architectural and Artistic interest. It describes a natural-slate roof, stepped shared chimney, red brick in Flemish bond, granite ashlar plinth, gauged-brick flat arches, granite sills, timber sash windows, cast-iron balconettes and cast-iron bars between some window reveals. The recorded use is a house.

**Fieldwork priorities:** Full frontage; upper-floor balconettes and iron bars; brickwork and gauged arches; granite plinth and sash windows; roofline and shared chimney where visible. Treat as a private residence; photograph only from lawful public space.
