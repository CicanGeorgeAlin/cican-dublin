# CICAN Dublin Archive — NIAH Discovery Batch 061 Research Review

**Status:** Research-only candidate review. No archive IDs assigned, no catalogue records imported, no visual records created, no map pins enabled, and no production deployment.

## Selection and method

Five unmatched Dublin City Council NIAH registrations were selected from the official national NIAH missing-registration queue generated by Archive quality gate #1844. The group covers the Saint Laurence O'Toole ecclesiastical and educational cluster and two individually registered houses in the Seville Place / Marion Terrace terrace.

Each individual NIAH record was opened or retrieved from the official Buildings of Ireland search result on 21 November 2026. Registration, official title/address, rating, special-interest categories, uses, date range, description and appraisal were checked. The official national CSV row was also consulted for the registration and coordinate evidence.

An initial exact-value/name/address screen was run against the earlier `main` snapshot. It was repeated on 21 November 2026 against the refreshed production snapshot and the open candidate PRs:
- `data/objects.json`: 615 objects
- `data/niah-registered-objects.json`: 495 public NIAH records
- `data/sources.json`: 735 sources
- `data/visual-assets.json`: 615 visual records

No exact NIAH registration, normalized official-title or exact-address collision was found for the five selected candidates in the refreshed four canonical datasets. Cross-PR checks covered PRs #29, #32, #33 and #35. They surfaced related nearby records rather than exact duplicates: PR #33's NIAH 50010033 is a separate detached modernist school built 1936–38, distinct from this batch's attached limestone school group 50010018 (built 1847–48); PR #32 continues the Seville Place terrace with Nos. 45–41; and current-main NIAH 50010020 / DUB-BLD-0194 is the nearby convent in the same ecclesiastical cluster as candidates 50010018 and 50010019. These relationships require footprint/frontage review before import or map placement. Textual checks do not certify spatial non-overlap.

## Candidate records

### 1. Saint Laurence O'Toole's Church — NIAH 50010017
- **Address:** Seville Place / Sherriff Street Lower, Dublin 1
- **Rating / interests:** Regional; Architectural, Artistic, Social
- **Recorded use:** Church/chapel
- **NIAH date range:** 1840–1850
- **Description evidence:** Freestanding cruciform limestone Gothic Revival Roman Catholic church, built 1844–50 to designs of John B. Keane, with the steeple attributed to John Bourke. Five-bay nave, gabled transepts, chancel, four-stage tower and broach spire; pointed-arch openings, geometric tracery and stained glass.
- **Appraisal evidence:** NIAH describes the church as serving the growing dockworker community and as a local landmark/social focal point. The appraisal says the church was begun in 1844 by John B. Keane and completed in 1858 by John Bourke. This conflicts with the page's survey date range and description date range; retain both source statements and do not silently resolve the discrepancy.
- **Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010017/saint-laurence-otooles-church-seville-place-sherriff-street-lower-dublin-1-dublin

### 2. Saint Laurence O'Toole School — NIAH 50010018
- **Address:** Saint Laurence Place East / Sheriff Street Lower, Dublin 1
- **Rating / interests:** Regional; Architectural, Artistic, Social
- **Recorded use:** School
- **NIAH date range:** 1845–1850; description dates the school buildings to 1847–48
- **Description evidence:** Attached multiple-bay, two-storey limestone school range within the grounds of and attached to Saint Laurence O'Toole Church. The recorded composition includes boys' and girls' school ranges, connecting wings, varied pointed-arch and square-headed openings, limestone dressings and decorative roof features.
- **Appraisal evidence:** NIAH identifies the school as a local landmark with an important role in the educational life of the community, and places it in the context of Catholic education after Emancipation.
- **Record-boundary note:** It is a distinct NIAH registration from the church (50010017), although the record explicitly describes it as attached to and within the church grounds. Treat as two separate registered objects with a shared-site/curtilage relationship to be reviewed; do not merge or infer a parent record.
- **Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010018/saint-laurence-otoole-school-saint-laurence-place-east-sheriff-street-lower-dublin-1-dublin

### 3. 49 Seville Place / Saint Laurence Place East — NIAH 50010019
- **Address:** 49 Seville Place, Saint Laurence Place East, Dublin 1
- **Rating / interests:** Regional; Architectural, Artistic, Social
- **Recorded use:** Presbytery/parochial/curate's house
- **NIAH date range:** 1870–1875; built 1872
- **Description evidence:** Corner-sited attached three-bay, three-storey presbytery over raised basement, designed by John Bourke. Mixed garden-wall-bond red brick, granite quoins and dressings, varied sash windows, paired recessed porches and wrought-iron gates.
- **Appraisal evidence:** NIAH calls it a fine 1870s presbytery and highlights its two principal elevations, Lombardic detailing, stonework and relationship to nearby ecclesiastical and educational buildings.
- **Related existing archive record:** The current catalogue already contains DUB-BLD-0194 / NIAH 50010020, The Oasis Centre, the convent on Saint Laurence Place East / Emerald Street. The 50010019 appraisal itself describes a neighbouring red-brick convent. This is a proximity/ensemble relationship, not an exact registration or address duplicate. Before any future import, compare the actual building footprints and address frontages; do not merge the presbytery and convent.
- **Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010019/49-seville-place-saint-laurence-place-east-dublin-1-dublin

### 4. 47 Seville Place / Marion Terrace — NIAH 50010022
- **Address:** 47 Seville Place, Marion Terrace, Dublin 1
- **Rating / interests:** Regional; Architectural
- **Recorded use:** House
- **NIAH date range:** 1860–1880; built c.1870
- **Description evidence:** Terraced two-bay, two-storey brown-brick house in Flemish bond, with M-profile slate roof behind parapet, granite coping and plinth, gauged brick window heads, timber sash windows, moulded entrance surround, panelled timber door and wrought-iron railings.
- **Appraisal evidence:** NIAH describes the house as one of a group of five in a terrace developed in the second half of the nineteenth century, contributing to the architectural heritage of the area.
- **Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010022/seville-place-marion-terrace-dublin-1-dublin-city-3

### 5. 46 Seville Place / Marion Terrace — NIAH 50010023
- **Address:** 46 Seville Place, Marion Terrace, Dublin 1
- **Rating / interests:** Regional; Architectural, Artistic
- **Recorded use:** House
- **NIAH date range:** 1860–1880; built c.1870
- **Description evidence:** Terraced two-bay, two-storey brown-brick house, one of a group of five. M-profile slate roof, Flemish-bond brickwork, granite plinth, gauged-brick voussoirs, rendered reveals, masonry sills, later replacement casement windows and a moulded entrance surround.
- **Appraisal evidence:** NIAH notes the house's contribution to an attractive, well-proportioned terrace associated with nineteenth-century development following the railway's arrival.
- **Record-boundary note:** Separate NIAH registration from 47 Seville Place (50010022). They are neighbouring houses in one terrace, not a single combined record.
- **Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010023/dublin-city

## Coordinate evidence — deliberately not imported

The individual NIAH page coordinate display does not explicitly identify its CRS. For these records, the displayed pairs are:
- 50010017: 317106, 234825
- 50010018: 317070, 234819
- 50010019: 317072, 234880
- 50010022: 317060, 234891
- 50010023: 317057, 234897

The official national NIAH dataset contains separate coordinate fields. Its ITM_E/ITM_N pairs for the same registration numbers are:
- 50010017: 717027, 734852
- 50010018: 716999, 734844
- 50010019: 717000, 734906
- 50010022: 716989, 734919
- 50010023: 716985, 734923

The CSV also supplies latitude/longitude fields, retained in the separate audit JSON. NIAH's dataset documentation states that the ITM fields are the official coordinate values to quote; the dataset provenance also warns that existing centroids were digitised relative to OSI 6-inch mapping and may require revision against larger-scale mapping. The page-displayed pairs are consistent in magnitude with the older Irish Grid/National Grid representation, but the individual pages do not label that CRS. Therefore:
- Do not call the page-displayed pairs ITM.
- Do not copy either coordinate set into the archive map fields at this research stage.
- Do not claim either set is a field-verified building footprint.
- Keep coordinate CRS reconciliation and map accuracy assessment separate from object identity verification.
