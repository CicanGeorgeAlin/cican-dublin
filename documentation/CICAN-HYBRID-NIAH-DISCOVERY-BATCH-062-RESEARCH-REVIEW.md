## Candidate records

### 1. 45 Seville Place / Marion Terrace — NIAH 50010024
- **Rating / interests:** Regional; Architectural
- **Use:** House
- **NIAH date range:** 1860–1880; built c.1870
- **Description:** Terraced two-bay, two-storey brown-brick house, one of a group of five. M-profile slate roof behind parapet with granite coping; Flemish-bond brickwork over granite plinth; gauged-brick window heads; two-over-two timber sliding sash windows; round-headed entrance in moulded render surround; panelled door, engaged pilasters, granite steps and wrought-iron railings.
- **Appraisal:** NIAH links the house to the nineteenth-century development of southern Seville Place following the railway's arrival in the 1830s–1840s. Original fenestration and joinery contribute to significance.
- **Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010024/45-seville-place-marion-terrace-dublin-1-dublin

### 2. 44 Seville Place / Emerald Street — NIAH 50010025
- **Rating / interests:** Regional; Architectural, Artistic
- **Use:** House
- **NIAH date range:** 1860–1880; built c.1870
- **Description:** Corner-sited end-of-terrace three-bay, two-storey brown-brick house, one of a group of five. Double-pile slate roof hipped north; Flemish-bond brickwork over granite plinth; incised stone street-name plaque on north elevation; varied square- and round-headed openings; original timber sash windows and shutters; moulded entrance, panelled door, granite steps and wrought-iron railings.
- **Appraisal:** Its corner position and small round-headed windows distinguish it as a terminating house in a composed terrace of five. Surviving original fenestration and joinery contribute to significance.
- **Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010025/44-seville-place-emerald-street-dublin-1-dublin

### 3. Emerald Dairy, 43 Seville Place / Emerald Street — NIAH 50010026
- **Rating / interests:** Regional; Architectural, Social
- **Previous name:** Emerald Dairy
- **Use:** Original use shop/retail outlet; current recorded use unknown
- **NIAH date range:** 1860–1880; built c.1870
- **Description:** Corner-sited terraced two-bay, two-storey former house over basement, with wraparound ground-floor shopfront and four-bay two-storey rear extension. Double-pile hipped slate roof; Flemish-bond brown brick; bracketed cast-iron gutters; red-brick sawtooth eaves; rendered platband retaining raised lettering reading “Emerald Dairy”. Ground-floor windows are blocked and the shopfront is partly concealed by steel shutters.
- **Appraisal:** NIAH identifies the retained Emerald Dairy lettering as evidence of the former dairy use and local social role. The dairy took its name from adjacent Emerald Street.
- **Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010026/emerald-dairy-seville-place-emerald-street-dublin-1-dublin

### 4. 42 Seville Place — NIAH 50010027
- **Rating / interests:** Regional; Architectural
- **Use:** House
- **NIAH date range:** 1860–1880; built c.1870
- **Description:** Terraced two-bay, two-storey house over raised basement, one of a group of four. Double-pile pitched slate roof with yellow-brick shouldered chimneystacks, yellow clay pots, bracketed cast-iron gutters and red-brick sawtooth eaves. Rendered walls with moulded quoins and plinth; square-headed window openings with rendered reveals; windows blocked and entrance concealed behind recent steel shutters.
- **Appraisal:** NIAH relates the terrace to development after the railway arrived in the 1830s–1840s and notes that retained bracketed eaves and rendered dressings contribute to significance.
- **Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010027/42-seville-place-dublin-1-co-dublin

### 5. 41 Seville Place — NIAH 50010028
- **Rating / interests:** Regional; Architectural, Artistic
- **Use:** House
- **NIAH date range:** 1860–1880; built c.1870
- **Description:** Terraced two-bay, two-storey house over raised basement, one of a group of four. M-profile slate roof; shared yellow-brick chimneystacks; brown Flemish-bond brickwork over granite plinth; original two-over-two timber sliding sash windows; moulded entrance surround and panelled door with engaged pilasters; granite platform and steps; granite plinth wall and wrought-iron railings.
- **Appraisal:** NIAH describes a composed terrace of four houses whose terminating houses have a distinctive single-bay upper storey. Original fenestration, sawtooth eaves and delicate wrought-iron railings contribute to significance.
- **Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010028/41-seville-place-dublin-1-co-dublin

## Record-boundary and coordinate cautions

- 50010024 and 50010025 are separate NIAH registrations within a terrace group of five.
- 50010026 is a separately registered corner property.
- 50010027 and 50010028 are separate registrations in a terrace group of four; 41 Seville Place is a terminating house.
- The individual NIAH pages display coordinate pairs without explicitly labelling their CRS. Raw page values are retained in the JSON evidence packet only; they are not ITM, GPS fixes or authorised map coordinates.
- No building footprint overlap, shared parcel, current occupancy or access permission is inferred from the NIAH descriptions.
