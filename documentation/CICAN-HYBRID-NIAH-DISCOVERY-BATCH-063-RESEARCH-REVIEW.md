## Official records reviewed

### 1. 40 Seville Place — NIAH 50010029
- Regional; Architectural; house; 1860–1880; built c.1870.
- End-of-terrace two-bay, two-storey house over raised basement, one of a terrace of four. Notable recorded fabric includes Flemish-bond brown brick, granite plinth, sawtooth eaves, bracketed gutters, slate roof, granite steps and wrought-iron railings.
- NIAH places the terrace in the nineteenth-century development of southern Seville Place after the railway arrived.
- Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010029/dublin-city

### 2. 6 Oriel Street Upper — NIAH 50010030
- Regional; Architectural and Artistic; house; 1860–1880; built c.1870.
- Terraced two-bay, two-storey brown-brick house, one of a pair. The record describes a slate roof, Flemish-bond brickwork, granite plinth, moulded entrance and panelled timber door.
- NIAH notes its relationship to the Dublin and Drogheda railway and survival of original character.
- Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010030/oriel-street-upper-dublin-1-dublin-city

### 3. 7 Oriel Street Upper — NIAH 50010031
- Regional; Architectural and Artistic; house; 1860–1880; built c.1870.
- End-of-terrace two-bay, two-storey brown-brick house, one of a pair. Recorded details include Flemish-bond brick, granite plinth, slate roof, moulded entrance and surviving historic fabric.
- NIAH identifies the pair's post-railway context and streetscape contribution.
- Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010031/oriel-street-upper-dublin-1-dublin

### 4. Seville Place railway embankment — NIAH 50010032
- Regional; Architectural and Technical; miscellaneous building; 1840–1860; built c.1850.
- Attached railway embankment with red-brick parapet, brick pilasters, snecked rusticated limestone walls, limestone platband and coping, and a segmental-headed blind arcade.
- NIAH describes the contrast between yellow-brick voussoirs and rusticated limestone and the structure's streetscape contribution.
- Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010032/seville-place-dublin-1-dublin

### 5. Saint Laurence O'Toole School, Seville Place — NIAH 50010033
- Regional; Architectural and Social; school; 1935–1940; built 1936–1938.
- Detached three-bay, three-storey rendered national school, dated 1936 and designed by Robinson and Keefe. Recorded features include a flat roof, strong horizontal lintel/sill courses, school crest and date plaque, curved external stair and boundary railings.
- NIAH identifies its modernist design and contribution to the city's architectural variety.
- Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010033/saint-laurence-otoole-school-seville-place-dublin-1-co-dublin

## Coordinate and provenance controls

The individual NIAH pages display coordinate pairs. Their coordinate reference system is not labelled on those pages. The pairs are preserved in the accompanying JSON as raw page-displayed evidence only; none is imported or interpreted as ITM, Irish Grid, GPS or map-ready coordinates.

The national NIAH dataset documentation distinguishes ITM easting/northing from latitude/longitude and states that ITM values should be quoted for official purposes. Any coordinate adoption must come from the correctly identified official dataset fields, with source/version provenance recorded.
