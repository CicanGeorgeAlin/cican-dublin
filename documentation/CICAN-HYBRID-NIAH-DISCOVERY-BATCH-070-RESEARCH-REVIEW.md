## Individual record review

The official NIAH pages were checked individually for registration number, address, rating, categories, use, date range, description and appraisal. Full official record and CSV links are preserved in the JSON evidence packet.

- **87 Amiens Street (50010091):** a c.1832 end-of-terrace bookend of a terrace of seven, notable for its round-headed Ionic doorcase, retained historic rear return and cast-iron coal-hole cover bearing the Hammond Lane Foundry Dublin inscription.
- **76 Amiens Street (50010092):** an end-of-terrace former house now used as a financial institution; NIAH associates its commercial use with No.75 (50010093), while retaining a distinct registration for each property.
- **74 Amiens Street (50010094):** a former townhouse now in office use, with yellow brickwork, Doric entrance and original peacock fanlight.
- **73 Amiens Street (50010095):** the NIAH appraisal calls it the most intact example in its ten-house terrace and records continued residential use at the time of survey.
- **70 Amiens Street (50010098):** a former townhouse now in office use, with an original stained-glass peacock fanlight and shared granite entrance platform.

**Grouping caution:** 50010091 belongs to a terrace of seven. The other selected records are described within a separate terrace of ten paired houses. Do not combine these as one architectural group merely because they share the street name. Nor should the shared platforms, party-wall chimneystacks, doorcases or fanlights be duplicated as independent archive objects without footprint-level evidence.
