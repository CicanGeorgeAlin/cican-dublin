## Selection and source controls

The five registrations below were selected from the refreshed official four-authority missing-registration queue produced by the post-publication audit workflow (run 45319735449, artifact 14886359566). Each registration occurs exactly once in that queue and is assigned to Dublin City Council / County Dublin. Selection is not based on consecutive numbering alone.

Each individual Buildings of Ireland NIAH page was located and reviewed. The national CSV row and individual page text are kept as separate source fields. The official CSV's WGS84 latitude/longitude and ITM coordinates (EPSG:2157) are recorded separately. The raw page coordinate pair is kept separately where explicitly available; its coordinate reference system is not inferred.

## Record-level evidence

- **50010244 — M. & L. Szechuan Chinese Restaurant:** The individual NIAH page describes a late nineteenth-century terraced house with a dormer attic, polychromatic brickwork, sash windows and a shopfront. The national CSV NAME field is blank; the page's previous-name/title is kept separate.
- **50010245 — Brannigan's:** The individual NIAH page describes a late Victorian brick commercial building with a replacement timber pub front, granite cornice and sash windows. The national CSV records the current use as public house.
- **50010246 — M.J. Boylan & Son Ltd:** The individual NIAH page describes a c.1820 five-bay house with an ashlar granite shopfront and visible lettering. The CSV NAME agrees with the page title.
- **50010247 — 8 O'Connell Street Upper:** The individual NIAH page describes a 1917 five-storey corner building at Cathedral Street, designed by P.J. Munden following the 1916 Rising. The national CSV NAME field is blank.
- **50010248 — Irish American Oil Company:** The individual NIAH page describes a 1917 five-storey corner commercial building with a mansard roof and Portland stone detailing, designed by O'Callaghan & Webb. The national CSV NUMBER field is the literal value `01-Feb`, which is inconsistent with the page's address of 1–2 O'Connell Street Upper / North Earl Street. The raw CSV value is preserved; it is not treated as a valid street number.

Full individual-page descriptions, appraisals, raw national CSV fields and coordinate pairs are preserved in `audit/batch-090-five-candidate-evidence.json`.

## Data-quality cautions

1. The national CSV DESCRIPTION and APPRAISAL fields for 50010244, 50010245 and 50010246 contain U+FFFD replacement characters in the word "façade". The clean wording in the evidence packet is transcribed from the corresponding individual official NIAH pages. The raw national CSV value must remain distinguishable from the page transcription.
2. The national CSV NAME fields for 50010244 and 50010247 are blank. The display headings are taken from the official individual pages; the CSV fields are not backfilled.
3. The national CSV NUMBER for 50010248 is `01-Feb`. The official page gives the address as 1–2 O'Connell Street Upper / North Earl Street. Both representations are preserved, and the page-based address is used only as a display heading.
4. The source's WGS84 coordinates are source-derived, with positional accuracy unspecified. They are not founder GPS or field-verified coordinates.
5. Survey-era use and descriptions do not establish current occupancy, condition, access or permission to photograph.
