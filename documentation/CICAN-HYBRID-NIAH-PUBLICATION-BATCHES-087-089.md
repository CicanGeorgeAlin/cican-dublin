- All 14 published registrations were present exactly once in the latest workflow-generated official four-authority missing-registration queue.

- Official national CSV latitude/longitude values are published as SOURCE_DERIVED map positions. Positional accuracy is unspecified; they are not founder GPS or FIELD_VERIFIED.
- Official CSV ITM values (EPSG:2157) and WGS84 latitude/longitude are recorded separately.
- No linked NIAH photographs are reused. All 14 visual records await founder photography and A4 line art.
- The National Model Schools main building (50010222), chapel (50010226) and gate lodge (50010227) remain distinct; the two gate lodges remain separate. The Pro-Cathedral and Pro-Cathedral House also remain separate records.
- Ten nearby archive objects within 20 metres were checked individually: 77 and 82 Marlborough Street buildings and the Edwardian post box are distinct neighbouring assets, not duplicate registrations.
- Present-day occupancy, condition, access and photography permission are not inferred from survey-era information.
