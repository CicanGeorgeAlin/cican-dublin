## Discovery added

**DUB-STR-0092 — 103 Ringsend Park — marble drinking fountain recessed into garden wall**

- Location: garden wall at the junction of Ringsend Park and Cambridge Road, Ringsend, Dublin 4.
- Dublin City Council RPS reference: **7376**.
- Form: marble fountain recessed into a brick garden wall, with decorative granite capping.
- Catalogue coordinates: **53.3436064403, -6.2228018581**.
- Source coordinate: **ITM 718340 E, 734070 N**, transformed from EPSG:2157 to WGS 84 / EPSG:4326.
- Coordinate status: **SOURCE_DERIVED**; founder field GPS remains outstanding.
- Reference image status: no third-party image registered; founder photography required.

The current Dublin City Record of Protected Structures (2022–2028) lists RPS 7376 at 103 Ringsend Park as a drinking fountain in a garden wall. A BusConnects architectural-heritage survey identifies it at the junction of Ringsend Park and Cambridge Road and describes a marble fountain recessed into a brick garden wall with decorative granite capping. An EPA-published environmental assessment appendix includes two photographs of the structure and gives the ITM coordinate used here.

## Date discrepancy preserved

The consulted sources disagree on the construction date:

- The BusConnects CBC16 architectural-heritage appendix describes the fountain as **c.1930**.
- The EPA-published environmental report describes it as erected in **1906**.

Both sources identify the same protected feature, RPS 7376, at 103 Ringsend Park. The archive records the discrepancy explicitly and does not select one date as definitive. Further primary-source research or direct inscription evidence is needed to resolve it.

## Ringsend research decisions

The current 2022–2028 DCC RPS independently confirms the 2018-listed Ringsend features RPS 7378–7381, including the former Reg Armstrong Works entrance, former Irish Glass Bottle Company office, CIE bus depot granite gate piers and former Bovril building. This confirms their continued inclusion in the current statutory register; it does **not** by itself prove that each feature survives physically in situ today. They remain separate survival-verification leads and were not added in this batch.

The current RPS dataset metadata states that its CSV resource was last updated on 19 December 2025 and contains 8,959 records. The resource's preview interface was unavailable during this pass; no claim is made that the entire CSV was downloaded or systematically screened.
