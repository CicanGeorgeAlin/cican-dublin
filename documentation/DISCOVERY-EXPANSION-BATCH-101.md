## Research outcome

This batch separates two buildings that share the Irish Glass Bottle Company name but must not be conflated.

### Former sheet-glass factory — Charlotte Quay

UCD Digital Library's G. & T. Crampton Photograph Archive catalogues a 1935 photograph of the Irish Glass Bottle Company sheet-glass factory at Charlotte Quay, Ringsend. Its catalogue description states:
- originally constructed in 1870–1871;
- architect: Thomas Holbrook;
- works carried out by G. & T. Crampton in 1935–1936;
- the building no longer exists;
- archive location metadata: 53.342089, -6.231755.

The photograph is a valuable historical visual lead, but its stated licence is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and commercial use requires contacting UCD Digital Library. It has **not** been copied into the repository or registered as a publishable reference image. It is not a founder original.

### Separate protected office — Ringsend Road / South Docks Road

The current Dublin City Development Plan 2022–2028, Volume 4, lists RPS 7379 as the former Irish Glass Bottle Company three-storey brick office with glass-block entrance, feature window and clock at Ringsend Road / the corner of South Docks Road.

A 2026 planning record for 50 Ringsend Road identifies an existing office building and states that a portion of its façade is protected under RPS 7379. This supports the continued existence of the office building associated with RPS 7379; it does not prove that every detail in the historic RPS description survives unchanged.

**Critical distinction:** the Charlotte Quay sheet-glass factory is not the RPS 7379 office. The historic factory photograph must never be used to illustrate the protected office, and the office's current existence must not be used to imply that the factory survives.

## Catalogue decision

No new map object was admitted in this batch. The archival photograph depicts a building explicitly described by UCD as no longer existing, while the exact present-day footprint and any surviving archaeological fabric have not been independently established. The source evidence is registered for future historical-site research. A separate catalogue record for the former factory should only be created with a clearly non-visible / former-site status and a defensible site boundary or location methodology.

## Image and rights decision

- Historical image: identified and visually inspected as an archival factory photograph.
- Commercial reuse: not cleared; UCD requires direct contact for commercial use.
- Repository import: **not permitted on the evidence currently available**.
- Founder photography: not applicable to the demolished factory building.
- Current office at RPS 7379: requires its own exact-object field photography; the historic factory image is not a substitute.
