## Research outcome

This batch confirms a new extant architectural heritage object in Irishtown: **Bottlemaker's Hall**, also known as **Dolphin House**, at 1A Irishtown Road, Dublin 4.

### Historical identity

- The Dictionary of Irish Architects records Bottlemakers' Hall on Irishtown Road in 1915 as new club premises for the Irish Glass Bottlemakers' Society, nearly complete at the time.
- The same entry identifies Aubrey Vincent O'Rourke as architect and John Dillon as builder.
- Dublin City Council's Ringsend & Irishtown Walking Trail (2022), stop 5, states the hall was built in 1915 by an amalgamation of bottle blowers from nearby factories. It describes the building's social role and association with Jim Larkin's political rallies.
- The current Dublin City Development Plan 2022–2028, Volume 4, lists Bottlemaker's Hall as protected structure **RPS 4003**.

### Current survival evidence

Dublin City Council planning application **WEB3596/26**, received 22 October 2026, concerns the protected structure at Bottlemaker's Hall, Irishtown Road, D04 HD26. Its description identifies an existing detached 250 m², two-storey and one-storey vacant social club and manager's apartment. It also describes a one-storey dancehall to the rear and proposed renovation and conversion works.

The application was registered and undecided in the planning record consulted on 1 November 2026. The proposed works are not treated as granted permission or as completed works. This current application is the key evidence supporting physical survival.
