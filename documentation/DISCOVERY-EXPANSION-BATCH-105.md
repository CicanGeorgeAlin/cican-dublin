# Discovery Expansion Batch 105 — Pigeon House and Ringsend industrial heritage

**Project:** CICAN DUBLIN  
**Founder:** CICAN GEORGE ALIN  
**Protocol:** ALWAYS STAY IN THE LIGHT.

## Method

This batch uses Dublin Port Company's 3FM Environmental Impact Assessment, Chapter 16 Cultural Heritage, especially Appendix 16-1. The assessment includes site inspections and an illustrated catalogue of individual heritage assets. Published ITM points were converted from EPSG:2157 to WGS84. These are source-derived points, not founder GPS fixes; positional accuracy is unspecified.

## Four new catalogue records

### DUB-BLD-0097 — Former Pigeon House Hotel (Teach Pigeon)
- Dublin City Council RPS 6795.
- Construction 1793–1795; design attributed to Robert Pool.
- Classical dressed-ashlar granite building, three storeys over basement, full-height bows at either end of the south-east elevation; entrance faces the Liffey and its harbour.
- Built to accommodate cross-channel passengers; later incorporated into the Pigeon House precinct.
- EIAR ITM 720343, 733686 → WGS84 **53.33970553746711, -6.192883490566827**.
- The 3FM EIAR records it as standing. Dublin City Film Office describes its form and dates. March 2026 local reporting discusses rooms used by a wastewater operator under a council letting proposal; this is dated context, not proof of final executed lease terms.
- Photograph only from lawful public viewpoints or with permission; do not enter the property.

### DUB-INF-0006 — Concrete boat slip, DCIHR 18-12-118
- Date c.1920–1930, replacing an earlier wooden pier shown on the 1912 OS map.
- Concrete slip with iron posts and railings, concrete mooring blocks, cast-iron lamppost at the north-west corner and limestone quay wall to the east.
- EIAR ITM 717920, 734198 → WGS84 **53.34484995918657, -6.2290583408467**.
- The EIAR describes it as a surviving maritime feature retaining original railings.

### DUB-INF-0007 — Timber-and-metal landing stage, DCIHR 18-12-121
- Date c.1900; later served Hammond Metals from the 1950s.
- Timber-plank deck on a metal frame tied into the stone revetment wall on the eastern bank of the River Dodder.
- EIAR ITM 717909, 734127 → WGS84 **53.34421464294605, -6.2292498858474525**.
- Described as standing, disused and fallen into disrepair at the time of survey. Do not climb onto it or approach unstable edges.

### DUB-INF-0008 — Stone utility house, DCIHR 18-12-151-01
- Date c.1900; associated with the Rathmines and Pembroke Main Drainage Scheme.
- Detached two-bay, single-storey masonry structure with rock-faced granite walls, limestone quoins, pitched slate roof, cast-iron rainwater goods, blocked segmental-headed windows and a square-headed doorway with granite lintel and threshold.
- EIAR ITM 718758, 733985 → WGS84 **53.34274929565924, -6.216559643624238**.
- Described as disused at the time of survey.

## Additional findings retained as research leads

- **Pigeon House Fort Signal Tower, RMP DU019-038001:** the EIAR describes standing and buried remains but explicitly calls the location approximate. No precise pin was created.
- **Blockhouse:** identified as a site, not a separately confirmed standing building. No new building pin was created.
- **Pigeon House Harbour walls and basin:** recorded as heritage assets; exact extents and component phases need further assessment.
- **Macerals Slip, DCIHR 19-09-001:** historic slip shown on Rocque's 1757 map and later OS mapping; the EIAR says it was buried during container-terminal construction. Not a visible-object pin.
- **Lifeboat house:** no authoritative evidence confirming a separate structure was established in this batch. It remains an unverified research query.

## Image rights and fieldwork

Appendix 16-1 contains photographs of several assets, but those embedded report images were not copied because image-specific reuse rights were not independently established. No third-party reference photographs were added for the four new records. All four remain founder-photography targets.

The former hotel is not a public visitor attraction. The landing stage was described as disused and in disrepair. Confirm access, permissions and safe viewing points before visiting. Do not enter restricted port or utility land or unstable structures.
