# Discovery Expansion Batch 107 — Two historic Great South Wall slipways

**Project:** CICAN DUBLIN  
**Founder:** CICAN GEORGE ALIN  
**Protocol:** ALWAYS STAY IN THE LIGHT.  
**Date:** 1 November 2026

## Research basis

This batch is a further extraction from Dublin Port Company's 3FM EIAR Appendix 16-1, *Catalogue of Cultural Heritage Assets*. Both features have individual Dublin City Industrial Heritage Record references and published ITM points. ITM coordinates were transformed from EPSG:2157 to WGS84; they are source-derived, not founder GPS.

## New catalogue objects

### DUB-INF-0010 — Masonry landing slip at eastern entrance to Pigeon House Fort (DCIHR 19-09-012)
- Constructed c.1770 and recorded on the OS First Edition 6-inch and 25-inch maps.
- A substantial masonry boat slip adjoining the Great South Wall beside the eastern entrance to Pigeon House Fort.
- Approximately 25m long and 4.3m wide; slopes west-to-east at approximately 10 degrees.
- Rectangular granite masonry surface, similar to the blocks facing the adjacent Great South Wall.
- The EIAR records it standing up to 1.6m above the foreshore, with four granite courses visible; sheet piles protrude from silty sand on the channel side.
- ITM 720462, 733814 → WGS84 **53.340828224386556, -6.1910488461794735**.
- Port access and foreshore safety must be checked. Do not enter restricted land or approach unstable foreshore.

### DUB-INF-0011 — White Bank Wharf South Wall slipway (DCIHR 19-09-011)
- Believed to date to c.1770; integrated into the Great South Wall and recorded on OS First Edition and later maps.
- Long rectangular masonry blocks, approximately 1m to 1.3m long, laid at right angles to the sea wall.
- The EIAR records erosion and partial collapse at the north-west corner, apparently caused by wash from shipping at low water.
- ITM 721402, 733860 → WGS84 **53.341026658546966, -6.176923248852423**.
- Photograph only from lawful, safe public access; do not approach unstable edges or the waterline.

## Result

The two slipways are recorded separately because they have distinct DCIHR references, locations, fabric descriptions and condition notes. They must not be merged with the earlier Great South Wall boat slip (DCIHR 18-12-118) or the c.1770 slip at the eastern entrance to Pigeon House Fort (DCIHR 19-09-012); this batch's IDs are distinct and each source record is individually reconciled.
