## Research basis

This controlled batch extracts two separately identified industrial-heritage sites from Dublin Port Company's 3FM Environmental Impact Assessment Report, Appendix 16-1, *Catalogue of Cultural Heritage Assets*, compiled by Niall Brady and Dominick Gallagher.

The catalogue states that its entries draw on the National Monuments Service's Register of Monuments and Places, NIAH, Dublin City Council's Record of Protected Structures, the Dublin City Industrial Heritage Record, the National Civil Engineering Database and Dublin Docklands Industrial Archaeology Survey, supplemented by Dublin Port Archives and project field walkover inspection.

Both records have individual DCIHR references and published ITM coordinates. Coordinates were transformed from EPSG:2157 to WGS84. They are source-derived points, not founder GPS fixes; positional accuracy is unspecified.

## New catalogue objects

### DUB-INF-0012 — Former Rope Walk site (DCIHR 18-12-149-01)

- Appendix 16-1, p.30, records a Rope Walk building on the OS historical 6-inch map of 1843.
- The source describes it as a long, narrow building used for manufacturing rope and canvas.
- The catalogue notes that a variety of nineteenth- and twentieth-century structures occupied the site at the time of its survey.
- **Important limitation:** the source does not establish that the original Rope Walk building survives today. The record is for the historic site, not a claim that the original building remains standing.
- ITM 718043, 734112 → WGS84 **53.344050033882695, -6.2272441277899215**.
- Current fabric and lawful access require field verification.

### DUB-INF-0013 — Former Pigeon House Outfall Works (DCIHR 19-09-004)

- Appendix 16-1, pp.59–60, records the Outfall Works as completed in 1906 as part of Dublin's main drainage scheme.
- The works occupied the west side of the former Pigeon House Harbour basin.
- The catalogue describes the site as a major municipal engineering achievement.
- Original settlement tanks were replaced by concrete tanks around 2003.
- **Important limitation:** this is a historic site record. It does not claim that the original 1906 settlement tanks survive.
- ITM 720120, 733795 → WGS84 **53.340735278084885, -6.196188994754772**.
- No public access is assumed. Current operating status and access require verification.
