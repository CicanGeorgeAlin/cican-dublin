## Research and coordinate provenance

The three architectural and monument records use their individual NIAH registrations and published Irish Grid references, transformed from EPSG:29902 to WGS 84 with the project's established coordinate operation 1641.

Knockmaree's archaeological identity and surviving central cist are supported by the Phoenix Park Conservation Management Plan and NMS record DU018-00711. Its navigation point comes from an independent 9 June 2013 field survey that reports 3m GPS accuracy. This is explicitly third-party GPS, not founder GPS, and the source's condition observations are not treated as current.

The bascule pit and pivot stone record is based on Excavations.ie report 2022:467, licence 21E0869. The report gives ITM E 712106, N 734487 and describes surviving subsurface features beneath the former Duke of Dorset Gate arch. The report interprets the original drawbridge as most likely counterweighted; the mechanism remains unconfirmed. The record is linked to the parent Magazine Fort and is not represented as a visitor-visible feature.

## Field and conservation precautions

- Castleknock and Ashtown Gates are public park entrances; photograph from pedestrian paths and keep clear of traffic.
- The Papal Cross is a 35m steel structure; do not climb or interfere with the monument.
- Knockmaree is a protected archaeological monument. Do not touch, clean, move objects or disturb the cist.
- The bascule pit and pivot stone are subsurface archaeology. Do not attempt to expose or access them; photograph only if an authorised tour or interpretation point permits it.
- All coordinates remain source-derived until the founder records a safe, lawful on-site GPS fix.
