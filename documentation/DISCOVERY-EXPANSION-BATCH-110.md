## Research basis

This controlled batch uses eleven individual National Inventory of Architectural Heritage (NIAH) / Buildings of Ireland records for North Wall Quay structures and infrastructure. NIAH entries provide individual registration numbers, Irish Grid coordinates, architectural descriptions and appraisals. Coordinates were transformed from TM65 / Irish Grid (EPSG:29902) to WGS84.

The NIAH site point is not founder GPS and its positional accuracy is not specified. These entries are source-verified, not physically field-verified. Present condition and access must be checked before any founder visit.

- No record is labelled field-verified.
- Survey-era descriptions of dereliction or use are not claims about present-day condition.
- Access is not assumed for any private or operational building.
