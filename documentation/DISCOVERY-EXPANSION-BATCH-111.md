## Research basis

Four additional individually registered National Inventory of Architectural Heritage (NIAH) records were added for the North Wall / Custom House Quay waterfront. The records cover the Point Depot railway goods depot, George's Dock, the CHQ former tobacco warehouse and Custom House Quay.

NIAH coordinates are published in Irish Grid (TM65 / EPSG:29902) and transformed to WGS84. They are source-derived site points, not founder GPS; positional accuracy is unspecified.

## Image and rights record

Four third-party reference photographs were added: two historic Point Depot images (1983 and 2008 reconstruction), one George's Dock image, and one CHQ image. Each is linked to its Commons description page and records the author, date and licence. These are reference images, not CICAN founder originals.

## Verification limitations

- All four coordinates are transformed NIAH points, not field GPS.
- Physical field verification remains outstanding.
- Point Depot images document historical/construction phases and must not be treated as current condition.
- Public access to George's Dock and Custom House Quay may be affected by events or operational arrangements.
- CHQ interior photography is subject to venue rules.
- No founder photographs or line-art files were fabricated.
