### Object distinctions

- The Loop Line Viaduct is a multi-span transport structure with four distinct construction groups; the record includes a photography brief for steel, masonry, Portland stone and the commemorative plaque.
- The George's Dock Arch is catalogued independently from the dock and CHQ. Its 1813 construction and 1998 relocation are both recorded.
- Sheriff Street Lifting Bridge is a 1939–1941 bascule bridge now fixed. The 1873 panels are described as reused fabric commemorating the earlier swivel bridge, not as proof the current bridge dates to 1873.
- George's Dock Lock is catalogued separately from the wet dock and Scherzer Bridges, preserving its own ashlar walls, gates, steps and bollards.

## Image and rights record

Three third-party reference photographs were registered: Loopline Bridge (2013), George's Dock Arch (2006), and Sheriff Street bascule bridge (2023). Author, capture date, source page, attribution and licence are recorded. These are not founder originals. The George's Dock Lock has no photograph attached in this batch because an exact, rights-checked object photograph was not confirmed.

## Coordinate and field-verification policy

All four map points are transformed NIAH Irish Grid points, not founder GPS. No record is marked as field-verified. The Sheriff Street Commons photograph includes camera GPS; this is deliberately not substituted for the NIAH site point because a camera position is not the object's surveyed coordinate.

## Safety and access

Photograph only from public paths and obey railway, dock, event and site restrictions. Do not climb bridges or enter machinery, operational railway or restricted port areas.
