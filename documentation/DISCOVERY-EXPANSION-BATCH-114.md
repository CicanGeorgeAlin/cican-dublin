## Findings

- The Design Tower was built in 1862 for Bewley, Moss & Co. as a sugar refinery, designed by Alfred Derbyshire with engineering advice from Sir William Fairbairn; enlarged in 1890 for distillery use; restored as an enterprise centre from 1978. The 1983 Europa Nostra award is recorded in the NIAH appraisal.
- NIAH 50100331 is a triple-gable former malt house built around 1890.
- NIAH 50100332 is a 21-bay former Guinness malt house built in 1886 and remodelled in 1995.
- NIAH 50100334 is a separate skew-plan former malt house built around 1885, with later glazed stair and attic additions.
- Hanover Quay is an individually registered c.1790 limestone quayside with granite steps and cast-iron mooring posts. It is not merged with the overall Grand Canal Dock basin.
