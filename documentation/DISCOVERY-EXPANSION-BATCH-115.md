## Scope and result

One new physical heritage object is added: **DUB-BLD-0117 — Pumphouse No. 1, Alexandra Basin Heritage Zone** (NIAH 50060587). The building is separately registered by the NIAH and is not a duplicate of the dock, the wider Heritage Zone or other port structures.

The NIAH describes a c.1910 gable-ended brick pumphouse with two construction phases and records its architectural and technical fabric. Dublin Port confirms that it housed the steam engine powering the gates of Graving Dock No. 1 and is now used for cultural and heritage activity. The object is marked VERIFIED for its documented identity and historic function; this does not mean that the founder has inspected it in person.

## Important evidence discrepancy retained

NIAH Registration **50060588**, titled *Dublin Graving Dock*, describes a c.1910 concrete dock and says it remains in use. Dublin Port's official heritage information instead dates Graving Dock No. 2 to 1957, says it closed in 2017 and records its infill in 2021 to create a public plaza. This may reflect a different dock phase, a registration-to-feature mismatch or stale NIAH fields.

Rather than guess, this is added as a separate evidence-graded research note, **RES-DUBLIN-PORT-GRAVING-DOCK-IDENTITY-001**. No additional physical catalogue record or map pin is created until the original port plans and NIAH geometry are cross-referenced.
