## New catalogue object

### DUB-BLD-0123 — Port Centre — Dublin Port Company headquarters
NIAH Registration 50120265. A detached six-storey office building over a podium, designed by Conor Dwyer of Scott Tallon Architects following an architectural competition in 1954 and built in 1980–1981. NIAH describes an external precast-concrete frame, full-height recessed glazing, louvred concrete parapet, glazed entrance composition and rubble-limestone boundary walls with pierced cast-iron gates.

The published NIAH Irish Grid reference 318211, 234802 was transformed from TM65 / Irish Grid (EPSG:29902) to WGS 84: **53.350462469460, -6.225574287817**. This is a source-derived site point, not founder GPS or field-verified. The NIAH record does not specify positional accuracy.

## Infrastructure research — no speculative rail pins

Two Dublin Port updates were added to the source register:

- **Alexandra Road reconstruction:** Dublin Port describes reconstruction of the road and integrated rail line, new rail turnouts, utility ducting and a segregated pedestrian/cycle path. Its project page states a closure from 19 July 2025 until Autumn 2026. That stated period has passed by this report date, but the consulted page does not confirm actual reopening.
- **North Wall freight yard:** Dublin Port's 6 June 2025 release records a €9 million upgrade to the North Wall freight yard, intended to handle rail freight during Alexandra Road works. It anticipated direct rail access into the Port resuming in 2027; this is a forecast, not confirmation of completion.

A research note records these facts and their limits. No historic rail alignment, individual turnout or surviving rail component has been assigned an invented coordinate. The existing NIAH record for embedded railway remains on North Wall Quay remains a separate object and was not duplicated.
