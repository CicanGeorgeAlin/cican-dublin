## Two new micro-detail records

### DUB-DET-0001 — Paired iron mooring hooks set into granite coping
NIAH Registration 50010011 explicitly records several paired iron mooring hooks set into the coping of the c.1800 North Wall Quay wall. The archive now has a dedicated child record for these fittings, linked to parent DUB-INF-0015.

The NIAH description does not give an exact count or individual positions. The child record therefore has **no independent coordinates and no separate map pin**. It must not be treated as a founder field survey.

### DUB-DET-0002 — Three recessed iron access ladders
NIAH Registration 50010011 explicitly describes three shallow recesses containing iron ladders. A second child record documents this feature group under DUB-INF-0015.

The exact current survival and condition of each ladder are unknown. The record has **no independent coordinates and no separate map pin**. It does not invite anyone to climb, descend or access the river.

## Dublin Port Archive drawing lead

The Dublin Port Archive's nineteenth-century engineering-drawings catalogue lists:

- **ED/5074** — Arrangement of rail in connection with travelling cranes, North Wall Goods Terminus; general plan and section, Chris Mulrany, 1881.
- **ED/8853** — Cross-section of North Wall Quay and plan showing positions of old quay walls exposed during reconstruction in 1872–1873; c.1890.

The catalogue listing confirms these records exist, but does not by itself establish the precise location or survival of crane rails or individual quay fittings. No new crane, rail or bollard pin was created from the catalogue title.
