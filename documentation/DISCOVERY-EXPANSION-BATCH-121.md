## New catalogue object

### DUB-BLD-0124 — 2 Sir John Rogerson's Quay — former Tedcastle Steamer Company warehouse

NIAH Registration **50020470**, 2 Sir John Rogerson's Quay, Dublin 2.

The National Inventory of Architectural Heritage records an attached, gable-fronted, three-bay, two-storey former warehouse built in **1913–14**. Its description identifies a pitched slate roof with glazed clerestory, carved granite gable coping, cast-iron rainwater goods, red-brick Flemish-bond walls, granite string courses, brick pilasters and an oculus at the gable apex. A double-height round-headed opening was added around 1980.

NIAH identifies the former name as **Tedcastle Steamer Company**, attributes the design to **James Evans** for ship-owner Tedcastle McCormack, and records later occupation by the British & Irish Steam Packet Company. The official Dublin City Council Record of Protected Structures lists **RPS 7543** as 2 Sir John Rogerson's Quay, **façade only**.

The NIAH Irish Grid reference **316864, 234309** was transformed from TM65 / Irish Grid (EPSG:29902) to WGS 84: **53.346333970375, -6.245973433780**. This is a source-derived locator, not founder GPS and not field-verified.
