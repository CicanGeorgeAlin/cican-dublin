## Evidence notes

- **Drumcondra Road Lower (50130209):** NIAH records a c.1905 cast-iron Edward VII pillar box with crown, Post Office lettering and McDowall Steven & Co. maker's mark.
- **Drumcondra Road Upper (50120002):** NIAH records a c.1935 P&T pillar box with moulded neck and dentillated cap frieze.
- **Church Avenue / All Hallows College (50120012):** NIAH records a wall-mounted box with S.É. insignia, harp and a Postal District 9 stencil; the survey states it is no longer in use.
- **Collins Avenue (50130120):** NIAH records a c.1930 P&T box with Carron Company maker's mark on the rear of the base.
- **Swords Road (50130139):** NIAH records a c.1930 P&T box with Carron Company maker's mark on the front of the base.
- **Collins Avenue (50130251):** NIAH records a P&T box with bilingual “An chéad bailiú eile / Next collection” lettering and Carron Company mark.

All six NIAH registration numbers were checked against the complete existing catalogue before IDs were assigned. No matching NIAH references were present.
