## What the batch uncovers

- **National Botanic Gardens:** the Cactus, Aquatic and Fern Houses; the Teak House; the Curvilinear Range; and a historic corrugated-iron workshop. These are distinct NIAH registrations and individual structures, not duplicate entries for the Great Palm House.
- **Glasnevin's historic boundaries and entrances:** the c.1835 cemetery boundary wall and watchtowers, the south entrance gates, the original east entrance lodge at Prospect Square, and the surviving gates of the former St Vincent de Paul's Male Orphanage.
- **Cemetery architecture and landscape:** the Mortuary Chapel, O'Connell Circle, O'Connell Monument and round tower, and a collection of separately registered family and archbishop mausoleums.
- **Small functional heritage:** a c.1870 cast-iron water hydrant with a lion-head spout, one of a pair documented by NIAH.
- **Surrounding village fabric:** John Kavanagh's historic public house, Farrelly's Cottages and a turn-of-the-century house on Old Finglas Road.

## Evidence and deduplication

All 24 new NIAH registration references were checked against all 400 existing archive records before assigning IDs. No matching registration numbers or identical catalogue names were found. Each new object has a separate official NIAH source entry and a linked visual record. The four Botanic Gardens glasshouse/workshop objects are linked to the existing gardens entrance parent record to express their shared site context; they remain individually registered NIAH buildings.

## Coordinates and fieldwork

Each coordinate is transformed from the published NIAH Irish Grid reference (EPSG:29902) to WGS 84 using the project's established operation 1641. These are source-derived site locators, not founder GPS. They do not establish exact feature positions or current condition. Cemetery and garden records are to be photographed only from permitted visitor routes; no touching memorials, entering restricted spaces or interrupting services.

## Visual integrity

All 24 records are awaiting founder photography. No third-party image was imported, no record was marked photographed, illustrated or published, and A4 line art remains pending the corresponding founder original. The photography briefs distinguish exterior, detail and interior shots and require permission where applicable.
