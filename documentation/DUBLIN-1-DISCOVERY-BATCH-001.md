# Dublin 1 Discovery Batch 001

**Research date:** 2026-10-24  
**Coverage:** Dublin 1 — O'Connell Street Lower, Talbot Street, Gardiner Street Upper  
**Batch status:** Added to the research catalogue; founder field photography remains outstanding.

## Purpose

This batch expands the archive beyond major landmarks into individually identifiable street objects, inscriptions and historic commercial architecture. It follows Dublin City Council's Policy BHA18, which explicitly recognises historic ground surfaces and small public-realm features as heritage assets, including post boxes, coal-hole covers, gratings, boot scrapers, street skylights, lampposts and railings.

The NIAH's Dublin North City inventory recorded more than 2,800 structures in phases between 2011 and 2014, while expressly warning that the inventory is not exhaustive. Its introduction also highlights overlooked domestic and commercial buildings, canal and railway structures, coal-hole covers, post boxes, railings and gates.

### Research notes

- **Jim Larkin Monument:** NIAH describes the bronze figure by Oisín Kelly, erected in 1980, the granite pedestal and inset plaques on three faces. The monument is catalogued as a primary object; the inscriptions are a child record so their wording can be transcribed and photographed independently.
- **Talbot Street post box:** NIAH records a c.1935 cast-iron box with raised P&T insignia and a Carron Company maker's mark.
- **Gardiner Street Upper post box:** NIAH records a c.1880 cast-iron pillar box with Queen Victoria's VR insignia and an A. Handyside & Co. maker's inscription.
- **10 Talbot Street:** NIAH records a c.1840 building retaining historic upper-floor fabric. The indexed NIAH record was available in search results, but direct page retrieval was restricted during this research pass; re-check the live register before public-facing publication.

## Coordinate and fieldwork policy

The NIAH grid references are retained in the catalogue's existing grid-reference field. Decimal latitude/longitude remain null. No decimal GPS has been inferred from a map search or copied from a third-party aggregator. Exact on-foot positions and present condition must be checked during founder fieldwork.

For the two post boxes, the NIAH street-level locations should be reconciled with the physical object on site before treating the point as field-verified. Capture a context photograph that proves which pavement position is the correct object.

## Founder field mission checklist

1. Photograph the Jim Larkin Monument from safe public viewpoints.
2. Photograph all three pedestal plaques square-on; transcribe the complete wording only after checking the original photographs.
3. Photograph the Talbot Street post box, including its P&T insignia and rear maker's mark.
4. Photograph the Gardiner Street Upper pillar box, including the VR insignia and maker's inscription.
5. Photograph the complete façade of 10 Talbot Street and its surviving upper-floor details.
6. Record any access restrictions, temporary works, damage, replacement components or changes from the NIAH description.
7. Upload original photographs to the archive before marking any record PHOTOGRAPHED. Line art is a separate derivative and must not add details absent from the original photograph.

## Evidence and limitations

All five records are based on identified NIAH register entries; the three public-realm/building records whose full NIAH pages were retrieved were checked against their survey descriptions. The 10 Talbot Street entry is retained as VERIFIED at the register-identification level but carries an explicit note that its direct page should be rechecked before publication. The Jim Larkin inscription child record remains PARTIALLY_VERIFIED because complete wording and exact plaque-to-face mapping still require photographic confirmation.

This is a discovery batch, not a claim that Dublin 1 is comprehensively surveyed. The next pass should continue systematically through O'Connell Street, Abbey Street, Talbot Street, Gardiner Street and the Custom House/IFSC approaches, checking for further NIAH records and distinct micro-details without duplicating existing parent objects.
