## Findings

### Transport and street furniture

- **Loop Line viaduct over Talbot Street:** NIAH records the triple-span iron railway viaduct as c.1890, part of the railway link built between 1889 and 1891. Its documented features include riveted steelwork, fluted cast-iron piers, a bow-string parapet, star motifs and maker's insignia. It remains active transport infrastructure; all photography must be from safe public viewpoints, with no access to tracks or railway structures.
- **Marlborough Street post box:** NIAH records a c.1905 cast-iron post box with RE VII POST OFFICE insignia, McDowall Steven & Co maker's lettering and original granite kerbing.

### Historic buildings

- **23 Marlborough Street:** late eighteenth-century townhouse, c.1780, with surviving upper-floor façade composition and sash-window details.
- **77 Marlborough Street:** c.1750 red-brick townhouse with a distinctive chimney-like feature on a limestone pedestal and console. NIAH notes that the feature may relate to an adjoining building demolished in the past; its original function is not asserted here.
- **82 Marlborough Street:** c.1810 townhouse with a raised basement, granite entrance platform, doorcase and wrought-iron railings.
- **Talbot House, 9 Talbot Street:** institutional building begun in 1842, with wings added in 1859, notable for its granite ground floor, decorative frieze, dentils and modillions.
- **National Model Schools, Marlborough Street:** former college building of c.1838 with a seven-bay granite façade, moulded sill courses and dentillated cornice.

## Evidence limitations

The records are based on identified NIAH register entries and their published descriptions. This batch does not establish current condition or field-verified coordinates. Buildings are catalogued as whole primary objects; separately identifiable details may be added as child records after founder photography and evidence review. The batch is not a claim that Talbot Street or Marlborough Street has been exhaustively surveyed.
