# Dublin 1 Discovery Batch 010 — Sherrard Street micro-details

**Research date:** 2026-10-26  
**Scope:** 1 Sherrard Street Upper and 53 Gardiner Street Upper at the Sherrard Street junction, Dublin 1.  
**Method:** Checked the live catalogue for duplicate NIAH registration numbers and street/address matches. Added two host buildings and four separately photographable details from NIAH indexed records. The NIAH pages returned HTTP 403 when opened directly in this research pass; therefore all six records are marked PARTIALLY_VERIFIED rather than promoted to fully verified status.

## Six additions

### DUB-BLD-0078 — 1 Sherrard Street Upper
- **NIAH:** [50010749](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010749/sherrard-street-upper-dublin-dublin-city)
- **Date:** c.1828.
- **Irish Grid:** 315869, 235660.
- **WGS 84:** 53.3586857, -6.2604120.
- **Evidence in indexed NIAH record:** three-bay, three-storey brick townhouse over exposed basement; original decorative leaded fanlight; original cast-iron coal-hole cover set in a granite slab; wrought-iron railings and cast-iron corner posts. The appraisal also notes a rare original benchmark in the streetscape.
- **Fieldwork:** multiple-occupancy residence. Photograph only from the public footpath; do not enter the property or basement area.

#### DUB-STR-0046 — original cast-iron coal-hole cover
The indexed NIAH record explicitly describes an original cast-iron coal-hole cover set in a granite slab to the pavement. The exact foundry, pattern and date are not stated. Photograph the cover, granite setting and any visible maker's marks from the public footpath.

#### DUB-STR-0047 — original decorative leaded fanlight
The indexed NIAH record describes an original decorative leaded fanlight above the entrance. Photograph the full lead pattern and its relationship to the doorcase, only if visible from the public footpath.

#### DUB-STR-0048 — benchmark noted in the streetscape appraisal
The NIAH appraisal mentions a rare original benchmark. The indexed text does not identify its exact position, appearance or inscription. This is a fieldwork search lead linked to the host property, not a precisely located object. Do not create a precise pin or transcribe a mark until it is physically identified and matched.

### DUB-BLD-0079 — 53 Gardiner Street Upper / Sherrard Street junction
- **NIAH:** [50010751](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010751/gardiner-street-upper-sherrard-street-dublin-1-dublin)
- **Date:** c.1830; NIAH record range 1820–1840.
- **Irish Grid:** 315850, 235647.
- **WGS 84:** 53.3585731, -6.2607020.
- **Evidence in indexed NIAH record:** corner-sited late-Georgian townhouse with a Portland limestone date plaque on its south side elevation reading “Sherrard Street 1825”.
- **Fieldwork:** photograph the side elevation and plaque from the public footpath only; respect residents' privacy.

#### DUB-INS-0006 — “Sherrard Street 1825” limestone plaque
The indexed NIAH record transcribes the Portland limestone plaque as “Sherrard Street 1825” and locates it on the south side elevation. The exact plaque position inherits the host-building point and is not separately georeferenced. Photograph the full inscription square-on and preserve its exact wording.
