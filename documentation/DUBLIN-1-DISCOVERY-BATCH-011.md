## 6 Seville Place — DUB-BLD-0086

NIAH 50010046 describes a terraced two-bay, two-storey house over raised basement, built circa 1850. The register records yellow Flemish-bond brickwork, original timber sash windows, a round-headed entrance with original petal fanlight, a shared granite platform and steps, a cast-iron bootscraper, original cast-iron railings and decorative garden railings.

## 47 Hardwicke Street — DUB-BLD-0088

NIAH 50010889 describes a terraced three-bay, three-storey house over raised basement, built circa 1810. It records an encaustic-tiled entrance platform, wrought-iron bootscraper and granite steps. The appraisal notes that Hardwicke Street was laid out by Francis Johnston and that the house is among the modest Georgian townhouses framing St George's Church to the northeast.

Source: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010889/47-hardwicke-street-dublin-dublin

Micro-detail DUB-STR-0077 separately records the wrought-iron bootscraper. Its precise form, maker and condition require fieldwork.

## 6 Sherrard Street Upper — DUB-BLD-0089

NIAH 50010744 describes a terraced two-bay, three-storey house over exposed basement, built circa 1828. It retains a three-centred arched doorway with Ionic doorcase, original flat-panelled timber door and petal fanlight, decorative wrought-iron balconettes, granite platform and a cast-iron bootscraper. The appraisal identifies Thomas Sherrard, Surveyor to the Wide Street Commissioners, with the street's layout in the 1820s.

Source: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010744/6-sherrard-street-upper-dublin-1-co-dublin
