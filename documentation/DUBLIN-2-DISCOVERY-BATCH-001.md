## Main building — DUB-BLD-0085

NIAH registration 50100511 identifies No. 46 Merrion Square East as an attached three-bay, four-storey former house over basement, built circa 1790 as one of Nos. 46–48. The NIAH dates it 1785–1795 and records the group as built by James McMahon.

The register describes Flemish-bond red-brick walls on a moulded granite plinth, timber sliding sash windows, an ornate tripartite elliptical-headed Adamesque Ionic doorcase, decorative batwing fanlight, cast-iron balconettes, wrought-iron window guards and basement grilles, granite entrance platform and steps, basement railings with remnants of a wrought-iron lamp standard, and a cast-iron coal-hole cover in the pavement.

## Evidence and fieldwork

The NIAH register content was directly inspected and supports the existence and broad description of both details. Their exact present condition and positions remain fieldwork questions. Do not claim the lamp standard is complete, or infer the coal-hole cover's maker or date.

Fieldwork should record the façade, doorcase, fanlight, balconettes, basement railings, surviving lamp-standard remnant and coal-hole cover. Photograph only from lawful public viewpoints, respect the office occupants, and do not enter the entrance platform or handle the ironwork.
