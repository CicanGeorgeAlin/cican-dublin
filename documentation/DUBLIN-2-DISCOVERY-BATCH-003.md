Research date: 26 October 2026

## 1 Pembroke Street Upper — DUB-BLD-0031

NIAH registration 50930231 describes a terraced three-bay, four-storey former townhouse, built circa 1825 and now in use as an embassy. The detailed register record explicitly identifies a granite entrance platform approached by five granite steps, a cast-iron bootscraper on the platform, and a coal-hole cover in the pavement.

Official source: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50930231/embassy-of-greece-1-pembroke-street-upper-dublin-2-dublin

Separately catalogued details:
- DUB-STR-0079 — cast-iron entrance bootscraper.
- DUB-STR-0080 — cast-iron pavement coal-hole cover.

## 37 Harcourt Street — DUB-BLD-0014

NIAH registration 50920231 records a cast-iron coal-hole cover to the front footpath of 37 Harcourt Street. The appraisal specifically notes the retention of setting features such as railings, granite platform and coal-hole cover as enriching the streetscape.

Official source: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50920231/37-harcourt-street-dublin-2-dublin

Separately catalogued detail:
- DUB-STR-0081 — cast-iron pavement coal-hole cover.

## Evidence and fieldwork

The official NIAH register contents were directly reviewed. The records support the presence of these three features, but not their exact makers, installation dates or present condition. Each micro-detail is linked to its host building and has no independent map pin. Walking directions resolve to the host address.
