## 18 Earlsfort Terrace — DUB-BLD-0090

NIAH 50110449 describes a c.1880 terraced two-bay, three-storey former house over basement, now in office use. The register records a cast-iron bootscrape on the granite entrance platform, a cast-iron balustrade flanking the steps, a matching gate, basement panel railings and a coal-hole cover set in a granite slab to the front pavement.

Source: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50110449/18-earlsfort-terrace-dublin-2-dublin

## 1 Harcourt Terrace — DUB-BLD-0091

NIAH 50110463 describes a corner-sited former house built circa 1830. The front setting includes an elliptical-plan granite entrance step, cast-iron coal-hole cover set in granite pavior, granite plinth walls, wrought-iron railings with cast-iron collars and a wrought-iron gate.

Source: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50110463/1-harcourt-terrace-dublin-2-co-dublin

## 2 Pembroke Street Upper — DUB-BLD-0092

NIAH 50930232 describes a c.1825 terraced three-bay, four-storey former townhouse over basement, with an integral carriage-arch to Fitzwilliam Court. The register records a cast-iron bootscraper on the granite entrance platform, decorative cast-iron railings and corner posts, and a coal-hole cover in the pavement.
