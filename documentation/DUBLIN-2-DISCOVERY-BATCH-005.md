## DUB-BLD-0093 — 84 Harcourt Street

NIAH Registration 50920237 describes a terraced two-bay, four-storey former townhouse over basement, built circa 1830 and now used as a hotel. The register records red Flemish-bond brickwork, channelled granite at ground-floor level, a round-headed doorway with cast-iron Scamozzian Ionic columns, a panelled frieze and cornice, and a studded timber door.

The shared granite entrance platform has a cast-iron bootscraper and nosed granite steps. Cast-iron railings topped with anthemion finials stand over a carved granite plinth and enclose the basement well. A cast-iron coal-hole cover is recorded in the street to the west.
