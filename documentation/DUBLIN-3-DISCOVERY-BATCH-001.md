# Dublin 3 Discovery Batch 001

**Date:** 2026-10-25  
**Project:** CICAN DUBLIN ARCHIVE  
**Research status:** Three records added as VERIFIED against individual National Inventory of Architectural Heritage (NIAH) entries.  
**Field status:** All three await founder photography and on-site verification.

## Records added

### DUB-BLD-0038 — 7 Marino Crescent
- **NIAH:** [50120103](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50120103/marino-crescent-dublin-3-dublin)
- **Period:** Built 1792
- **Coordinates:** NIAH Irish Grid 317970, 236444; transformed WGS 84 53.3652625, -6.2285798.
- **Documented details:** Carved stone doorcase, panelled pilasters with foliate brackets, leaded petal fanlight, granite steps and platform, wrought-iron and cast-iron railings, decorative gate and carved limestone plinth.
- **Field note:** Residential frontage. Photograph only from the public footpath. Exact details are not individually georeferenced.
- **Interpretive caution:** NIAH notes the local “Spite Crescent” story as reputed; it is not treated as proven motive.

### DUB-BLD-0039 — Marino College
- **NIAH:** [50120089](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50120089/marino-college-14-20-marino-mart-dublin-3-co-dublin)
- **Period:** Built 1935–36; central block raised circa 1975.
- **Coordinates:** NIAH Irish Grid 317782, 236341; transformed WGS 84 53.3643794, -6.2314408.
- **Documented details:** Herringbone brickwork, raised ashlar granite plinth, carved granite mullions, granite sill courses, carved limestone plaques by L.T. Campbell depicting Industry and Commerce, and carved granite entrance surround.
- **Field note:** Educational and public-library setting. Check current access and photography rules before entering the grounds. The published point locates the building, not the plaques.

### DUB-BLD-0040 — Vernon Lodge, Clontarf
- **NIAH:** [50030164](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030164/vernon-lodge-41a-castle-avenue-clontarf-dublin-3-co-dublin)
- **Period:** Dated 1885.
- **Coordinates:** NIAH Irish Grid 319351, 236272; transformed WGS 84 53.3634082, -6.2079102.
- **Documented details:** Carved ashlar limestone entrance, Tudor arch, crenellated gable, date stone, Vernon family coat of arms, crest and motto “Vernon Semper Viret”, octagonal piers and carved capitals.
- **Field note:** NIAH records the entrance as disused. Photograph from the public footpath; do not enter private grounds.

## Coordinate method

The original NIAH Irish Grid coordinates are preserved in each record. Decimal WGS 84 points were transformed from TM65 / Irish Grid (EPSG:29902) using the EPSG transformation operation 1641, with published transformation accuracy of 1 metre. This does **not** establish the accuracy of the source NIAH point centroid. All three remain SOURCE_DERIVED and NOT_FIELD_VERIFIED.
