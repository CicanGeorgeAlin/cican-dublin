# Dublin 3 Discovery Batch 002

**Date:** 2026-10-25  
**Scope:** Five additional NIAH records in Marino and Clontarf, Dublin 3.  
**Method:** Individual National Inventory of Architectural Heritage records; source-derived Irish Grid coordinates transformed to WGS 84.

## Added records

### DUB-BLD-0041 — 24 Marino Crescent — Georgian house and entrance details
- **NIAH:** [50120120](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50120120/marino-crescent-dublin-3-dublin)
- **Address:** 24 Marino Crescent, Dublin 3
- **Period:** 1792
- **Irish Grid:** 318063, 236457
- **WGS 84:** 53.3653585, -6.2271786
- **Fieldwork:** Photograph the exterior from the public footpath only. Do not enter the front garden or private property.
- **Photography:** Full frontage and its position within the crescent; Round-headed doorway and carved timber doorcase; Pilasters, frieze and remaining architrave; Granite steps, plinth and entrance platform; Window surrounds, parapet and roofline
- **Research note:** NIAH Registration 50120120. NIAH describes a 1792 house and records alterations including replacement fanlight and windows. The published point locates the building, not each architectural detail.

### DUB-BLD-0042 — Marino Institute of Education — former St Patrick’s Monastery
- **NIAH:** [50130265](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50130265/marino-institute-of-education-griffith-avenue-marino-dublin-3-dublin)
- **Address:** Griffith Avenue, Marino, Dublin 3
- **Period:** 1928–1929
- **Irish Grid:** 317766, 237162
- **WGS 84:** 53.3717561, -6.2313755
- **Fieldwork:** The building is used as a library/archive associated with Marino Institute of Education. Photograph from public viewpoints; check current campus access and photography rules before entering grounds.
- **Photography:** Full Griffith Avenue elevation and overall U-plan composition; Doric portico, pediment, dentils and copper cross finial; Christian Brothers badge and rendered detailing; Timber sash windows and quoins; Former chapel round-headed windows and stained glass where visible; Entrance surround, steps and garden terrace
- **Research note:** NIAH Registration 50130265. The record dates the former monastery to 1928–29 and identifies Ralph Byrne as its architect. Current access and the visibility of individual features require field confirmation.

### DUB-BLD-0043 — Clontarf Stationmaster’s House — polychrome brick railway architecture
- **NIAH:** [50030283](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030283/clontarf-railway-station-96-howth-road-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin)
- **Address:** 96 Howth Road, Clontarf, Dublin 3
- **Period:** Circa 1880
- **Irish Grid:** 318604, 236647
- **WGS 84:** 53.3669438, -6.2189847
- **Fieldwork:** Private house adjoining the former station building. Photograph only from the public footpath or other lawful public viewpoint; do not enter the grounds or obstruct the railway environment.
- **Photography:** Overall house frontage and relationship to former station; Red brickwork and English-bond pattern; Yellow-brick quoins, stringcourses and voussoirs; Segmental-headed windows and granite sills; Half-glazed entrance door and overlight; Roofline, bargeboards and cast-iron rainwater goods
- **Research note:** NIAH Registration 50030283. This record describes the former stationmaster's house, now a private dwelling, not the station platform or active railway infrastructure. NIAH states the station closed in 1956.

### DUB-BLD-0044 — Clontarf and Scots Presbyterian Church — Gothic Revival church
- **NIAH:** [50120135](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50120135/clontarf-and-scots-presbyterian-church-howth-road-clontarf-road-dublin-3-dublin)
- **Address:** Howth Road / Clontarf Road, Clontarf, Dublin 3
- **Period:** 1888–1890; tower 1897; west additions 1931
- **Irish Grid:** 318092, 236348
- **WGS 84:** 53.3643731, -6.2267838
- **Fieldwork:** Church and grounds may have managed access. Photograph exterior from public footpaths; obtain permission before entering or photographing interiors.
- **Photography:** Full church and tower from public viewpoints; Four-stage tower, crenellated parapet and sandstone finials; Rusticated granite walling and red sandstone quoins; Traceried pointed-arch windows and carved surrounds; Pointed-arch entrances, doors and iron furniture; Boundary wall, piers and wrought-iron gates
- **Research note:** NIAH Registration 50120135. NIAH dates the church to 1888–90, tower to 1897 and west-end vestry/porch works to 1931. The Harry Clarke window is recorded as a 1919 memorial.

### DUB-BLD-0045 — Church of St John the Baptist — seventeenth-century church ruins
- **NIAH:** [50030311](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030311/church-of-st-john-the-baptist-castle-avenue-clontarf-dublin-3-co-dublin)
- **Address:** Castle Avenue, Clontarf, Dublin 3
- **Period:** Circa 1610, incorporating earlier fabric
- **Irish Grid:** 319490, 236479
- **WGS 84:** 53.3652358, -6.2057452
- **Fieldwork:** The ruins stand within a graveyard. Photograph only from publicly accessible areas and observe any cemetery opening hours, signage and access restrictions. Do not climb on masonry or disturb graves.
- **Photography:** Overall ruin and relationship to Castle Avenue; West gable and surviving bell-cote; Rubble-stone wall construction and surviving wall heights; Round-headed window openings and stone/brick reveals; Pointed-arch door openings and porch; Gravestones and surviving interior features from paths only
- **Research note:** NIAH Registration 50030311. NIAH dates the surviving church to circa 1610, incorporating earlier fabric. The Bram Stoker baptism detail is an NIAH appraisal statement; seek the underlying parish-register entry before treating it as independently verified.


## Coordinate method and limitations

Original Irish Grid references are retained verbatim. Coordinates were transformed from TM65 / Irish Grid (EPSG:29902) to WGS 84 using EPSG transformation operation 1641. The transformation operation has a published accuracy of 1 m; this does not establish the positional accuracy of the original NIAH point. All five records remain SOURCE_DERIVED and NOT_FIELD_VERIFIED. The founder must confirm the physical location and practical access on site.

## Batch outcome

Five new records were added to the master catalogue, source registry and visual workflow. No photographs, illustrations or publication assets were fabricated. Each record awaits the founder's original photography. Dublin 3 now contains eight records in total, but this is not a comprehensive survey.
