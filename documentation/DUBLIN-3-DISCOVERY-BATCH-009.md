# Dublin 3 Discovery Batch 009 — Clontarf micro-details

**Research date:** 2026-10-26  
**Scope:** Castle Avenue, Kincora Avenue and the Clontarf Road seafront, Clontarf, Dublin 3.  
**Method:** Checked the current catalogue before adding any detail. Each new feature is explicitly described in an existing NIAH registration and is linked to an existing host-building record. Coordinates are inherited from the NIAH host point, not represented as precise micro-feature positions.

## Five new independently photographable details

### DUB-INS-0004 — Vernon Lodge 1885 date stone
- **Parent:** DUB-BLD-0040 — Vernon Lodge, 41A Castle Avenue.
- **NIAH:** [50030164](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030164/vernon-lodge-41a-castle-avenue-clontarf-dublin-3-co-dublin)
- **Evidence:** NIAH explicitly records a carved limestone date stone and dates the freestanding entrance to 1885.
- **Fieldwork:** Photograph from the public footpath at Castle Avenue / Kincora Avenue. Record all visible characters and the stone's position in the gable. Do not enter the private grounds.

### DUB-INS-0005 — Vernon family motto, coat of arms and crest
- **Parent:** DUB-BLD-0040 — Vernon Lodge.
- **NIAH:** [50030164](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030164/vernon-lodge-41a-castle-avenue-clontarf-dublin-3-co-dublin)
- **Evidence:** NIAH identifies the carved limestone coat of arms, crest and motto as those of the Vernon family, which owned Clontarf Castle for around 300 years. The motto is transcribed as “Vernon Semper Viret”.
- **Fieldwork:** Photograph the complete heraldic composition, the motto lettering and visible carving from the public footpath. Do not infer details that cannot be seen clearly.

### DUB-STR-0040 — Warrenpoint paired masonry sphinxes
- **Parent:** DUB-BLD-0049 — Warrenpoint gate lodge, 84 Clontarf Road.
- **NIAH:** [50030185](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030185/warrenpoint-84-clontarf-road-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin)
- **Evidence:** NIAH describes the entrance gate with masonry sphinxes on its piers.
- **Fieldwork:** Photograph each visible sphinx and the gate context from the public footpath only. This is a private residence; do not enter or approach through the gate.

### DUB-STR-0041 — 86 Clontarf Road petal-motif fanlight
- **Parent:** DUB-BLD-0050 — 86 Clontarf Road.
- **NIAH:** [50030182](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030182/clontarf-road-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin)
- **Evidence:** NIAH describes a round-headed entrance with a petal-motif fanlight, cut-granite entrance steps and platform, and cast-iron balustrade.
- **Fieldwork:** Photograph the fanlight and its doorway context only if visible from the public footpath. Respect resident privacy.

### DUB-STR-0042 — Clontarf Castle gate lodge carved shield
- **Parent:** DUB-BLD-0047 — Clontarf Castle gate lodge.
- **NIAH:** [50030163](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030163/clontarf-castle-castle-avenue-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin)
- **Evidence:** NIAH explicitly records a shield over the former carriage arch. This is distinct from the sculpted animal-head corbels already catalogued as DUB-STR-0039.
- **Fieldwork:** Photograph the shield and its relationship to the arch from a safe, lawful public viewpoint. Do not enter private grounds or stand on the carriageway or roundabout.

## Coordinate and evidence limits

- Each new micro-detail is linked to its already catalogued host building.
- The map coordinate is the host building's NIAH point, transformed from TM65 / Irish Grid (EPSG:29902) using the project's established EPSG operation 1641.
- These points do **not** claim to locate the individual carving, inscription, fanlight or sculpture precisely.
- Exact feature position, current condition and visibility remain for founder field verification.
- The features are documented by the official NIAH register; this does not mean they have been photographed by CICAN.
- No original photographs, line art or publication assets have been fabricated.
