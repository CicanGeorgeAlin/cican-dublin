# Dublin 3 Discovery Batch 011 — six additional Clontarf details

**Research date:** 26 October 2026  
**Scope:** Kincora Grove, Clontarf Road, Mount Prospect Avenue, Dunluce Road and Malahide Road, Dublin 3.  
**Method:** Search-indexed National Inventory of Architectural Heritage (NIAH) records checked against the existing catalogue for duplicate registration numbers; published Irish Grid points transformed using the established EPSG:29902 to WGS 84 operation 1641.

## Six new discoveries

### DUB-BLD-0080 — Beech Lodge, 118 Kincora Grove
- **NIAH:** [50030335](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030335/beech-lodge-118-kincora-grove-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin)
- **Date:** circa 1820
- **Irish Grid:** 319301, 236703
- **WGS 84:** 53.36729006981998, -6.208498381377299
- **Documented fabric:** Former gate lodge, previously named Clontarf Castle; attached double-pile, three-bay single-storey house with half-dormer attic, gabled breakfront, canted bays, carved timber ridge cresting and bargeboards, decorative rendered chimneystacks, pointed-arch entrance, granite threshold and flagstones, and rear outbuildings.
- **Significance:** Predates Kincora Grove as a street. NIAH notes the earliest Ordnance Survey map shows a yard and possible lodge on a driveway leading to Clontarf Castle.
- **Fieldwork:** Private residence; photograph only from the public footpath or with permission.

### DUB-BLD-0081 — 89 Clontarf Road
- **NIAH:** [50030357](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030357/89-clontarf-road-clontarf-dublin-3-co-dublin)
- **Date:** 1847
- **Irish Grid:** 319059, 236016
- **WGS 84:** 53.36117491333796, -6.212390354217271
- **Documented fabric:** Two-bay, two-storey terrace house over raised basement, with Flemish-bond brick front, granite platband, cornice and stringcourse, timber sash windows, petal-motif fanlight, corniced and friezed door surround, granite entrance steps and platform, cast-iron balustrade, railings and pedestrian gate.
- **Significance:** Part of a terrace built in 1847 by Isaac Warren on Vernon estate land. Its entrance features are individually photographable details.
- **Fieldwork:** Private residence; photograph only from the public footpath and respect resident privacy.

## Evidence and limitations

The NIAH indexed records provide the object descriptions, dates, appraisals and Irish Grid references. Full direct inspection of the individual NIAH pages remains outstanding. All six entries are therefore **PARTIALLY_VERIFIED** and their source records explicitly preserve that limitation.

The WGS 84 points are transformed from published NIAH Irish Grid references, not founder-recorded GPS fixes. Fieldwork must confirm current presence, condition, exact position and access. No founder photographs, line art or publication images have been created.
