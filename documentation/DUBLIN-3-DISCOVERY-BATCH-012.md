# Dublin 3 Discovery Batch 012 — North Strand, Clonliffe Road and Fairview Park

Research date: 26 October 2026  
Status: PARTIALLY VERIFIED — official NIAH indexed content reviewed; full-page inspection and fieldwork pending  
Area: Dublin 3

## Purpose

This batch adds seven individually identifiable physical discoveries from three National Inventory of Architectural Heritage (NIAH) records. The indexed NIAH content provides descriptions and Irish Grid references. Direct opening of the individual NIAH pages was unavailable during this research pass, so none of these additions is promoted to VERIFIED.

## DUB-STR-0057 — North Strand Road / Bayview Avenue milestone

Source: NIAH 50120248  
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50120248/north-strand-road-bayview-avenue-dublin-3-dublin

The indexed record describes a freestanding cut-granite milestone, circa 1820, with pedimented cap and chamfered plinth. A cast-iron plaque on the southeast face bears raised characters reading “P.O Dublin 1 Howth 8” and an incised mason's mark. It is located at the northwest side of North Strand Road at the Bayview Avenue junction.

Irish Grid: 317065, 235594.  
WGS 84: 53.35782984018193, -6.24248202034123, transformed from EPSG:29902 using the project's established EPSG operation 1641.

Unknowns: current condition, plaque legibility, exact mason's mark, precise on-site position and any later repairs.

## DUB-BLD-0082 — 238 Clonliffe Road Victorian corner house

Source: NIAH 50120194  
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50120194/238-clonliffe-road-dublin-3-dublin

The indexed record describes a c.1860 three-bay, two-storey former house over basement. Details include an M-profile hipped slate roof, cut-granite parapet and stringcourse, Flemish-bond brickwork, decorative terracotta chimney pots, timber sash windows, moulded doorcase, inscribed granite street-name plaque, wrought-iron railings, cast-iron bootscrape and cast-iron gate.

Irish Grid: 316055, 236201.  
WGS 84: 53.363503596363906, -6.257421474217028.

### Separately catalogued micro-details attached to DUB-BLD-0082

- DUB-INS-0007 — inscribed granite street-name plaque. The indexed record confirms its presence but does not transcribe the wording. The inscription must be read from a founder photograph before transcription is published.
- DUB-STR-0058 — cast-iron bootscrape on the granite entrance platform. Its exact form, maker and condition are not established.
- DUB-STR-0059 — cast-iron gate and historic railings with pointed finials on a cut-granite plinth wall.

These are separate physical features, not separate map points. Their navigation resolves to the host building. Exact feature positions and lawful viewpoints must be established during fieldwork.

## DUB-BLD-0083 — Fairview Park former public convenience

Source: NIAH 50120133  
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50120133/fairview-park-annesley-bridge-road-dublin-3-co-dublin

The indexed record describes a detached two-bay, single-storey former public convenience constructed circa 1930, inside Fairview Park. Details include a bell-cast terracotta roof with gambrels, louvered timber sections, terracotta ridge tiles, timber eaves brackets, a central panelled rendered chimney stack, pebbledash walls, wire-cut brick plinth, steel-framed barred windows, blocked round-headed doorways and wrought- and cast-iron railings.

Irish Grid: 317513, 236163.  
WGS 84: 53.36284059388305, -6.235545605328876.

### DUB-STR-0060 — Art Deco chimney stack and roofscape

This separately catalogued micro-detail records the NIAH appraisal's description of the central chimney as Art Deco in character and the roofscape as eccentric. Its exact form and silhouette require founder photography from public park paths. It has no independent map point; navigation resolves to the host building.
