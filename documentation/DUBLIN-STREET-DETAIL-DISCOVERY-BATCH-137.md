## Evidence and limitations

The NIAH surveys document the physical features and provide Irish Grid references. Coordinate conversions use EPSG:29902 to WGS 84 transformation operation 1641. The NIAH point for 29 Fitzwilliam Street Upper locates the host townhouse, not the exact boot scraper or coal-hole cover. These points are therefore source-derived approximate discovery locators, not founder GPS or exact micro-feature positions.

Dublin City Council Development Plan Policy BHA18 identifies post boxes, boot scrapers and coal-hole covers among historic public-realm features for conservation. This is policy context, not proof of present survival at each address.

All five records are marked **PARTIALLY_VERIFIED** and **TO_PHOTOGRAPH**. No founder photograph or third-party reference image has been added. Present condition, visibility, exact feature position and safe access remain to be checked on site. Photograph only from lawful public viewpoints; do not enter private property or obstruct pedestrians.
