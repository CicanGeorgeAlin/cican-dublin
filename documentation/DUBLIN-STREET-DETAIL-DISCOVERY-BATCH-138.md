## Coordinate and verification limits

Published NIAH Irish Grid references were transformed from EPSG:29902 to WGS 84 using transformation operation 1641. The transformed coordinates identify the host buildings only; they do not locate the exact boot scraper or coal-hole cover. All six micro-detail records are PARTIALLY_VERIFIED and await founder GPS and field photography. The three parent building records are verified against the official NIAH survey descriptions, not field visits.

The two coal-hole covers at No. 4 Mountjoy Square North are provisionally grouped in one record. Verify that both survive and record their separate GPS positions before splitting the record.

Photograph only from lawful public viewpoints. Do not enter private property, cross railings or obstruct pedestrians.
