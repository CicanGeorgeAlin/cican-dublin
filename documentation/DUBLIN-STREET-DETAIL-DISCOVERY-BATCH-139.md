## Evidence and feature distinctions

- **25 Hatch Street Lower (NIAH 50110519):** NIAH describes a c.1810 townhouse and records a wrought-iron boot-scrape on the quarry-tile/granite entrance platform and a cast-iron coal-hole cover in the footpath.
- **26 Fitzwilliam Square (NIAH 50930219):** NIAH describes a c.1825 townhouse and records a cast-iron boot scraper, pavement coal-hole cover and a metal grille affixed to a basement-window sill. The grille is recorded as *metal*; its material is not inferred.
- **35 Harcourt Street (NIAH 50920229):** NIAH describes a c.1800 townhouse and records a cast-iron coal-hole cover to the footpath, alongside historic railings and a granite entrance platform.

The features are registered as separate child objects. No claim is made that any feature has been recently inspected or photographed.
