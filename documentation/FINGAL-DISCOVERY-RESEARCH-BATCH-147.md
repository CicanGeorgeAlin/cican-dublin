## Candidate 1 — Saint Columb's Well and associated pump, Well Road, Swords

- **NIAH:** 11343013 — https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11343013/
- **National Monuments Service / RMP:** DU011-034013- (ritual site — holy well).
- **Location evidence:** Fingal archaeological impact assessment for Woodside, Swords (24E0374), Appendix 1, lists the RMP site at ITM 718035, 746569 and describes the enclosed spring well off Well Road, within view of St Columba's Church. The same passage records a cast-iron water pump immediately north of the well.
- **Historical evidence:** The assessment summarises historic map annotations and descriptions by Ó Danachair (1958) and Henry A. Wheeler (1975). It records local traditions associating the spring with St Colmcille and Swords, and historical accounts of its use for sore eyes. These are recorded traditions, not verified medical claims or proof of a sixth-century structure.
- **Feature distinction:** Treat the holy well enclosure and the immediately northward pump as potentially separate physical objects only after reviewing NIAH 11343013 and field imagery. Do not assume the pump survives today.
- **Coordinate caution:** The ITM point is a source-derived site locator for the RMP feature, not founder GPS and not the exact position of the pump. Preserve ITM 718035, 746569. Do not label it field-verified.
- **Fieldwork:** Check lawful public access and current condition. Do not enter the well enclosure unless access is clearly permitted; do not touch, clean or alter the structure, pump, ironwork or water.

## Candidate 2 — c.1780 Newbridge House demesne bridge, Donabate

- **NIAH:** 11336004 — https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11336004/newbridge-house-newbridge-demesne-donabate-dublin
- **Location:** Newbridge Demesne, Donabate, County Dublin.
- **NIAH description:** Single-arch humpback road bridge, c.1780, with carved limestone archivolt and keystones, random-rubble stone parapets, wrought-iron railings and ashlar piers.
- **NIAH Irish Grid:** 322039, 249413 (source-derived locator; transform and retain provenance if a map point is later added).
- **Duplicate distinction:** This is not NIAH 11336005, the separately registered c.1800 single-arch bridge already catalogued in Batch 146 (DUB-INF-0151). Keep the two registrations separate.
- **Fieldwork:** Verify visitor access and current condition within Newbridge Demesne. Photograph only from permitted routes; do not climb or stand on the bridge parapets or enter the watercourse.
