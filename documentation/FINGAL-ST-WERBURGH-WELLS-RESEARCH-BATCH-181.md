# CICAN Dublin Archive — Fingal Holy-Well Research Batch 181

**Date:** 17 November 2026  
**Scope:** Resolve the multiple Fingal records and local traditions using the name St Werburgh's Well; identify distinct archaeological sites, surviving fabric, lost sites and access constraints.  
**Status:** Evidence-backed research note only. No permanent catalogue IDs, source-registry entries, visual records or map pins created because the complete canonical production catalogue is not currently available for full duplicate reconciliation.

## 1. Candidate A — St Werburgh's Well, Crowscastle (DU011-045)

- **SMR:** DU011-045---- — ritual site / holy well.
- **Townland:** Crowscastle, Fingal.
- **Historic grid reference:** National Grid 31849/24582, as published by Swords Tidy Towns. Retain in original form; do not treat as founder GPS or convert without confirming the grid datum and transformation.
- **Associated local name:** St Werburgh's Well.
- **Location lead:** Airside Business Park / Lakeshore Drive, Swords, based on the historic protected-structure schedule and local heritage account. Exact surviving footprint must be checked against the National Monuments Service Historic Environment Viewer and field evidence.
- **Physical description:** Swords Tidy Towns describes a natural spring holy well predating the 1700s. The older Fingal protected-structure schedule describes a small well enclosed by a low stone wall in a wet drain. This supports a distinct physical target, but does not prove the present wall, spring or watercourse remains unchanged.
- **Access:** Swords Tidy Towns reports that a proper public right of way had not been put in place when its page was published. Do not direct visitors through the business park or imply public access. Photograph only from a lawful public viewpoint or with written landowner permission.
- **Current condition:** NOT FIELD VERIFIED. The available local account discusses restoration and access arrangements but does not establish the present condition. No restoration completion is assumed.
- **Disposition:** HIGH-PRIORITY FIELD-VERIFICATION LEAD. Do not publish as an accessible visitor stop until current access and survival are confirmed.

## 2. Candidate B — St Werburgh's Well, Commons East (DU012-022)

- **SMR:** DU012-022---- — ritual site / holy well.
- **Townland:** Commons East.
- **ITM locator:** 718911, 746786, published in the 2024 Woodside Archaeological Impact Assessment.
- **Historic identification:** Named “St. Werburgh's Well” on Duncan's 1821 map.
- **Survival:** The 2024 archaeological assessment explicitly says this is a different St Werburgh's well from DU011-045 to the southwest, and that DU012-022 has been built over with no visible remains.
- **Disposition:** LOST / BURIED SITE RESEARCH RECORD. It is a distinct historical site, not a second name for the Crowscastle well. Do not create a visitor pin or imply a visible well survives.

## 3. Important exclusion — Lady Well, Feltrim (DU012-026)

DU012-026 is a separate holy-well record in Feltrim, historically called Lady Well. The archaeological assessment for the Greater Dublin Drainage project states that it was formerly on the north-facing slope of Feltrim Hill and was removed during quarrying. It must not be merged with either St Werburgh's well above, and it must not be represented as a surviving feature.

Source: Greater Dublin Drainage cultural heritage assessment, Appendix 6: https://www.gddapplication.ie/sites/default/files/docs/greater-drainage-dublin/alternative-sites-assessment-phase2/Appendix-6_Cultural-Heritage-Assessment.pdf

## 4. Identity and duplicate controls

- DU011-045 (Crowscastle) and DU012-022 (Commons East) are two separate St Werburgh's well sites.
- DU012-026 (Feltrim) is Lady Well, a third and separate site.
- The names alone are not sufficient for matching; use the SMR identifiers, townlands and published locators.
- Repository search for DU011-045, DU012-022, DU012-026 and “St Werburgh” did not return an existing matching CICAN object record in the searchable repository index. This is not a substitute for a full master-catalogue duplicate check.
- No CICAN object IDs are allocated in this note.
- No map pins are created. DU011-045 retains only its published historic grid reference; DU012-022 retains its published ITM locator with the source and condition caveats above.
- The two named sites require separate parent records if promoted after full catalogue recovery. The associated folklore must remain attributed tradition, not established history or medical fact.
