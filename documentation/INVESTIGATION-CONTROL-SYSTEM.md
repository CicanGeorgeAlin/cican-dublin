# CICAN Dublin Archive — Investigation Control System

**Status:** LOCKED METHODOLOGY · version 1.0  
**Adopted:** 26 October 2026  
**Scope:** Historical research, source evaluation, claim verification and reconstruction of Clontarf's Sheds. The method may be reused across the archive.

## Purpose

This control system makes the investigation reproducible and prevents catalogue growth from being mistaken for historical certainty. It does not change the website design, physical catalogue, map data or protected map-marker implementation.

The unit of verification is the **individual historical claim**, not the source record and not the research note as a whole. A source can support one part of a claim while leaving its date, interpretation, location or geometry unproven.

## Evidence grades

Grades describe the directness of the evidence currently inspected. They are not a measure of how important or plausible a claim is.

| Grade | Definition | Permitted wording |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Original inspected** | The original archival document, manuscript, map, photograph or physical record has been directly examined and its relevant content recorded. | “The original record/map shows…” |
| **B — Contemporary source inspected** | A contemporary published primary source has been directly inspected, but the original underlying record or artefact is not the item examined. | “The contemporary published account states…” |
| **C — Catalogue or abstract** | An archival catalogue, finding aid, published abstract or faithful transcription identifies a record, but the underlying original has not been inspected. | “The catalogue/abstract records…” |
| **D — Secondary interpretation** | A later historian, local-history account, report or scholarly interpretation makes the claim. | “The secondary source reports/ interprets…” |
| **E — Unverified lead** | A search result, unattributed statement, inference or candidate source has not yet been adequately checked. | “A lead suggests…” |

### Mandatory grading rules

1. Assign grades claim by claim. Never transfer a grade from one claim to another merely because they appear in the same research note.
2. Where several sources support a claim, record each source and its role. Do not hide a weaker source behind a stronger unrelated source.
3. A catalogue entry proves that a record is catalogued with the supplied metadata; it does not prove what an unseen image depicts.
4. A published abstract proves what the abstract reports; it does not mean the original deed, will or memorial has been inspected.
5. A secondary transcription of an archival document remains secondary until checked against the original or a reliable image of it.
6. An official modern report may be authoritative for its own findings, but its historical interpretation is still not a directly inspected historic primary source.
7. If evidence conflicts, retain both readings, record the conflict and do not silently choose one.
8. If a date, attribution or interpretation changes, add a dated correction entry explaining the former value, new value, reason and affected records.
9. “Verified” must always state **what** is verified. A place name may be verified while its footprint remains unknown.
10. No claim is promoted to a mapped physical feature until its geographic precision and source support are independently adequate.

## Investigation backlog — ordered work

Tasks are not complete merely because a source URL or catalogue entry has been registered. Completion requires the stated evidence output.

### INV-001 — Inspect the 1785 Malton print
- **Priority:** P1 · **Status:** READY FOR SOURCE ACCESS
- **Source:** British Library Maps K.Top.53.25; BLL01004821630.
- **Action:** Obtain the best available scan or catalogue image; inspect the entire print and details at sufficient resolution.
- **Record:** Exact title and imprint; visible buildings and shoreline; foreground/background landmarks; inscriptions; image orientation; any stated viewpoint; catalogue metadata; reproduction terms.
- **Complete when:** Image inspected and observations are documented separately from interpretation. If no adequate scan is accessible, record the access barrier and next contact/request.
- **Do not:** Infer modern coordinates or shed footprints from the title alone.

### INV-002 — Verify the candidate Giles King view (1744–1745)
- **Priority:** P1 · **Status:** CANDIDATE NOT YET REGISTERED
- **Action:** Locate the exact British Library catalogue record, confirm title, date, shelfmark and the numbered key; register the source only after those details are checked; obtain and inspect the image.
- **Record:** Verbatim key wording and number, image features, view direction if demonstrable, catalogue URL and rights.
- **Complete when:** Exact catalogue record and image are independently checked. Until then, the claim remains Grade E.
- **Do not:** Present the candidate as a confirmed source or conflate it with the 1785 Malton print.

### INV-003 — Retrieve the original 1836 County Dublin OS Name Book entry
- **Priority:** P1 · **Status:** ACCESS TARGET IDENTIFIED
- **Source:** NLI County Dublin Name Books, Volume I, BB6442; Clontarf entry.
- **Action:** Search Ask About Ireland's Name Books collection and/or request consultation through the NLI Main Reading Room.
- **Record:** Full original entry, page/folio, date, surveyor, all annotations, associated map and exact transcription.
- **Complete when:** Original page or authoritative image is inspected and compared word-for-word with the secondary quotation.
- **Do not:** Treat the secondary transcription as primary verification.

### INV-004 — Inspect the 1728 Burgh/Perry harbour chart
- **Priority:** P2 · **Status:** IMAGE REGISTERED; LABEL UNVERIFIED
- **Action:** Obtain a sufficiently high-resolution image around Clontarf; independently transcribe the label; compare with the scholarly attribution and Cosgrave's wording.
- **Record:** Exact label including ambiguous letters, location relative to coast road and shoreline, map title/edition, source image and rights.
- **Complete when:** Label and map location are directly visible and documented. If illegible, preserve alternative readings and mark unresolved.
- **Do not:** Georeference an ambiguous label or reuse the image without permission from the rights holder.

### INV-005 — Inspect Taylor's 1816 map at Clontarf
- **Priority:** P2 · **Status:** ACCESS ROUTES IDENTIFIED
- **Action:** Use NLS, Virtual Treasury item LBC-GJ-13-2 and/or the SDCC historic map viewer to inspect the actual Clontarf section.
- **Record:** Exact sheet/extent, road names, route geometry, landmarks, map scale and image source.
- **Complete when:** The reported Green Lane label and its alignment are directly inspected or explicitly rejected as unsupported.
- **Do not:** Treat the secondary transcription as a map reading.

### INV-006 — Reconcile Rocque editions and map coverage
- **Priority:** P2 · **Status:** SOURCE RECONCILIATION REQUIRED
- **Action:** Identify separately the 1756 four-sheet city survey, 1757 harbour/environs publication, 1757 reduced city/suburbs map and 1760 county survey. Determine exactly which sheet/report reproduction is being cited.
- **Record:** Full title, publication date, sheet, repository, image link, Clontarf coverage and direct inspection status for each.
- **Complete when:** No source is described merely as “Rocque's map” where edition identity matters.

### INV-007 — Trace Patrick Toole's 1792 property
- **Priority:** P3 · **Status:** PUBLISHED ABSTRACT LOCATED
- **Action:** Extract the full abstract and identify memorial/deed registration details, grantor/grantee, tenure, adjoining lands and chain of title; seek the original memorial.
- **Record:** Exact text/page, deed or memorial identifier, names, boundary calls and any map/estate references.
- **Complete when:** The underlying memorial is inspected or the access barrier is documented. Plot location requires independent boundary evidence.
- **Correction history:** An earlier project entry incorrectly gave 1789. The corrected date is 11 September 1792. Preserve the correction history.

### INV-008 — Trace the 1786 Christmas Weekes water-supply record
- **Priority:** P3 · **Status:** TRANSCRIPTION LOCATED
- **Action:** Identify the original County Dublin Grand Jury minute/presentment or related record and search for plans, route descriptions, contractors or water-source references.
- **Record:** Original date and wording, record series, page/folio, named places, technical description and any plan.
- **Complete when:** Original record is inspected or the record's absence/access limits are documented.
- **Do not:** infer a pipe route or locate a water source from the letter alone.

### INV-009 — Inspect the 1704 Donnycarney survey and 1792 NLI maps
- **Priority:** P3 · **Status:** CATALOGUED; COVERAGE UNKNOWN
- **Action:** Inspect DCLA C1 S1/130, Brownrigg's Slip Field (MS 21 F.51/(081)) and Bay View & Mount Pleasant (MS 21 F.51/(079)).
- **Record:** Full sheet extent, scale, named features, boundaries, north/orientation if present, and whether the Sheds corridor is actually depicted.
- **Complete when:** Each map's relevance is classified as direct, regional context or irrelevant to the Sheds. No relevance is inferred from title alone.

### INV-010 — Build a defensible historical geography
- **Priority:** P4 · **Status:** BLOCKED BY SOURCE INSPECTION
- **Action:** Compare only inspected maps and images against stable historic and modern control features. Record map distortion, scale, datum/registration method and uncertainty.
- **Record:** Feature-by-feature evidence, candidate geometry, confidence and uncertainty bounds.
- **Complete when:** Every proposed mapped feature has traceable visual or documentary support and reproducible georeferencing.
- **Do not:** create an exact footprint, boundary, oyster-bed polygon or map pin from general descriptions or unverified secondary maps.

## Source inspection record template

For each inspected source, record:
- Source ID and full bibliographic/catalogue identity.
- Repository, collection, shelfmark or stable identifier.
- Exact page, folio, plate, sheet or image region.
- Whether the original, facsimile, transcription, catalogue or search excerpt was examined.
- Date inspected and by whom.
- Verbatim transcription for critical labels/wording, preserving uncertainty.
- Description of visible content separated from interpretation.
- Relevant claims supported and claims not supported.
- Image resolution, map scale, orientation and geographic limitations where applicable.
- Copyright, access and reproduction conditions.
- Next action and outstanding questions.

## Publication and map promotion gate

A research note may be published with an explicit evidence grade and limitations. A historic feature may be added to the physical catalogue or map only after all of the following are satisfied:

1. The relevant source is registered and its exact item/page/sheet is identified.
2. The specific claim is recorded in the claim register.
3. The source content relevant to the claim has been directly inspected, or the note explicitly remains a lead rather than an established fact.
4. Independent corroboration is sought for important location and identity claims.
5. Geographic precision is supported by the evidence and the georeferencing method is documented.
6. Copyright, access and reproduction conditions are respected.
7. The quality validator passes and the deployed website is checked.

**A source count is not an evidence score. A catalogue title is not a view of the map. A historical place name is not a surveyed boundary.**

## Protected project boundaries

- Do not redesign the website as part of this protocol.
- Do not alter the protected map-marker baseline or its CSS/JavaScript.
- Do not add unverified Sheds footprints, pins or object records.
- Keep research notes separate from the physical object catalogue.
- Preserve the archive's source-based, privacy-conscious and evidence-first principles.
