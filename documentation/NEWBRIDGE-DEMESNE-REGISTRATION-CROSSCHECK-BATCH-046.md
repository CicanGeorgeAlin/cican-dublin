# CICAN Dublin — Batch 046: Independent NIAH Search Index Cross-Check for Newbridge Demesne Registrations

**Date:** 20 November 2026  
**Scope:** Cross-check individual NIAH register identities for Mack's Bridge and neighbouring Newbridge Demesne registrations using the official Buildings of Ireland search index and independent Fingal records.  
**Publication state:** Research only; no production changes.

## 1. Official Buildings of Ireland search index cross-check

The official Buildings of Ireland search page for County Dublin (page 4) returns individual entries for the four neighbouring registrations:
- **11336002** — Newbridge House, Hearse Road, Kilcrea, Donabate; vermiculated granite entrance gate piers, c.1770, with crown finials and pedestrian entrance piers.
- **11336003** — Newbridge House, Hearse Road, Newbridge Demesne, Donabate; rusticated granite entrance gate piers, c.1790, reproduction cast-iron gates and coursed limestone rubble wall with granite coping; entrance rebuilt 1986.
- **11336004** — Newbridge House, Newbridge Demesne, Donabate; single-arch humpback road bridge c.1780, carved limestone archivolt and keystones, random rubble stone parapet walls, wrought-iron railings and ashlar piers.
- **11336005** — Newbridge House, Newbridge Demesne, Donabate; separate single-arch road bridge c.1800 with random rubble parapets, cut limestone coping and square piers on vermiculated plinths.

Official search page:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/?county=DU&date_from=&date_to=&group=&location_type=building&query=&search_page=4&town=&townland=&type=

This search index supports the distinct identities and broad descriptions. It is not a substitute for opening and capturing every individual record page, and the search index does not expose the full appraisal field in the returned text.

- Newbridge Demesne walls/gates/railings, 11336002, coordinates 322049,249367.
- Newbridge Demesne bridge, 11336003, coordinates 321031,249527.

Source:
https://www.fingal.ie/sites/default/files/2023-03/Donabate%20Portrane%20Heritage%20Audit%20Report%20FINAL%2019.09.2022.pdf

The appendix labels these as separate registration numbers and gives different coordinate pairs. This is direct evidence against merging the four assets into one Newbridge Demesne record.

## 4. Important distinction: official search-index evidence versus live feature-row evidence

The indexed Buildings of Ireland search page is an official NIAH-facing source and confirms the exact registration identities and descriptions, but it is not the national FeatureServer feature row. The FeatureServer schema contains APPRAISAL and official coordinate fields, but the exact REG_NO 11336004 row remains unretrieved. No national dataset row or appraisal is claimed in this batch.
