## 1. Corduff House — NIAH 11329018

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11329018/corduff-house-corduff-ba-e-by-dublin

- Rating: Regional.
- Categories of special interest: Architectural, Artistic.
- Original use: Farm house.
- NIAH date range: 1740–1760; description dates the house c.1750.
- Raw coordinate pair displayed by NIAH: 320271, 252747.
- Date recorded: 30 August 2000.
- Description: Detached five-bay, two-storey over semi-basement farmhouse with dormer attic, reached by a flight of steps. The NIAH describes a gabled central bay with Diocletian window, a rear return, and an eastward extension of c.1908 adjoining a three-bay range of c.1750 that may originally have been detached. A group of detached and attached single- and two-storey outbuildings is arranged around a courtyard; a detached three-bay single-storey gate lodge is also recorded on the site.
- Architectural details include a double-pitched slate roof, terracotta ridge tiles, rendered and rubble-limestone walls, red-brick cornice, timber sash windows and a round-headed rusticated granite door surround with a timber fanlight.

**Important group relationship:** Fingal's Record of Protected Structures describes RPS 313 as Corduff House, including its outbuildings, entrance gates and piers, and a cast-iron wall-mounted post box in a gate pier. NIAH 11328003 is the separately registered post box. Preserve Corduff House and the post box as separate CICAN objects if the catalogue audit confirms no existing records; link them as a documented site/component relationship rather than merging them.

The NIAH coordinate pair is transcribed as displayed; the individual page does not label its CRS beside the coordinate value. Do not convert or pin it in this batch.

## 2. Corduffhal thatched dwelling — NIAH 11328001

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11328001/corduffhal-co-dublin

The official NIAH search index describes a terraced four-bay single-storey thatched house, c.1850, with a projecting entrance porch, rear extension, hipped thatched roof and two red-brick chimney stacks.

**RPS status caution:** Fingal's proposed-deletions schedule identifies a Corduff Hall thatched dwelling on Drogheda Road, Lusk, as RPS 312 and cites NIAH 11328001. The Council's appraisal says that the thatch was replaced with slate and the building significantly altered, and recommends deleting it from the RPS. This is a recommendation to delete from the local RPS, not proof that the NIAH survey record itself has been withdrawn. Do not describe it as currently thatched or as currently protected without checking the current statutory schedule and the present building condition.

**Identity caution:** Fingal's RPS documents distinguish several thatched dwellings in the Corduff area (RPS 311, 312, 314 and 315). The NIAH registration number must be used to keep each candidate separate. Do not merge this record with other Corduff thatched dwellings or with the distinct Corduff (Hackett) records 11323013, 11323014 or 11323025.

No defensible coordinate is published in the retrieved source extracts for this record. No map pin is approved.

## 3. Gracedieu bridge — NIAH 11328002

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11328002/gracedieu-co-dublin

- Rating: Regional.
- Categories of special interest: Architectural, Technical.
- Original and current use: Bridge.
- NIAH date range: 1840–1880; description dates the bridge c.1860.
- Raw coordinate pair displayed by NIAH: 317081, 252490.
- Date recorded: 18 August 2000.
- Description: Single-arch rubble-stone road bridge over a river, with rock-faced limestone to the parapet wall and rounded capping.

**Independent corroboration:** The Greater Dublin Drainage Alternative Sites Assessment, Phase 2, Appendix 6.5.2, lists NIAH 11328002 at Gracedieu/Roscall as a bridge, gives NGR 317081, 252490 and describes the same single-arch rubble-stone bridge, c.1860, with rock-faced limestone parapet and rounded capping. The report notes it is c.240 metres east of the Baldurgan land parcel and c.800 metres east of the Baldurgan 20-hectare site.

The NIAH page and specialist report reproduce the same numeric pair, but this research note does not assume the coordinate reference system from the values alone. Retain each source's coordinate label and CRS metadata before any transformation or pin.

## 4. Corduff Common wall-mounted post box — NIAH 11328003

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11328003/corduff-common-co-dublin

The official NIAH search index describes a wall-mounted cast-iron post box, c.1905, with an 'ER VII' monogram, set into a gate pier of Corduff House.

**Independent corroboration:** The Greater Dublin Drainage cultural-heritage assessment identifies NIAH 11328003, RPS 313, at Drogheda Road, Corduff, as a post box. It gives NGR 319994, 252760 and describes a wall-mounted cast-iron post box, c.1905, with 'ER VII' monogram, set into the gate pier of Corduff House.

**Object modelling:** This is a distinct small-scale street/architectural object from the Corduff House parent building. The RPS description explicitly includes the post box in the Corduff House protected-structure group, but NIAH gives it its own registration. Keep it as a separate archive object and link to Corduff House; do not merge it into the house record.

The coordinate pair above comes from the specialist report's NGR field. The coordinate reference system and precision should be confirmed from the report's mapping metadata or the official NIAH source data before conversion. No pin is approved.

This is a project-specific cultural-heritage assessment, not a substitute for the NIAH survey record or current statutory RPS schedule. Its distances are measured relative to the GDD assessment land parcels and should not be repurposed as object dimensions or public-access directions.
