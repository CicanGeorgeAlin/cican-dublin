## 1. Four NIAH records

### 1.1 Saint Ita's Hospital gate lodge — NIAH 11329013

**Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11329013/saint-itas-hospital-portrane-road-portraine-demesne-donabate-co-dublin

- Rating: Regional.
- Categories of special interest: Architectural, Artistic.
- Previous name: Portrane Mental Hospital, originally Portrane Asylum.
- Original use: Gate lodge.
- NIAH date range: 1850–1890.
- Raw coordinate pair as displayed by NIAH: 323934, 250739.
- Date recorded: 1 August 2000.
- Description: Detached three-bay, single-storey gate lodge, c.1870, with a canted central bay and a single-bay extension to the left. The NIAH describes a hipped slate roof, central red-brick chimney, terracotta ridge tiles and finial, rendered and ruled walls, original 1/1 sash windows to the façade, replacement uPVC frames to the side, and a replacement door.

**Research controls:** Keep this as a separate gate-lodge asset from the main Saint Ita's Hospital complex (11330001), the memorial folly (11330002) and the nurses' home (11330003). The shared institutional place-name does not make these one structure. The individual NIAH page gives no explicit CRS label for its coordinate pair; do not transform or pin it in this batch. Current access and ownership were not independently established here.

- Rating: Regional.
- Categories of special interest: Architectural, Social.
- Original use: School.
- Current use: Apartment/flat (converted).
- NIAH date range: 1870–1890.
- Raw coordinate pair as displayed by NIAH: 323438, 250463.

**Research controls:** Preserve the current NIAH use field as apartment/flat (converted); do not imply it remains a school. The source coordinate pair is not explicitly CRS-labelled on the individual page. The building is in residential use; no interior access or photography is assumed.

### 1.4 Rogerstown House — NIAH 11329017

**Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11329017/rogerstown-house-rogerstown-co-dublin

- Rating: Regional.
- Categories of special interest: Architectural.
- Original and current use: Farm house.
- NIAH date range: 1770–1790.
- Raw coordinate pair as displayed by NIAH: 323343, 252961.
- Date recorded: 9 August 2000.
- Description: Detached five-bay, two-storey house, c.1780, with a projecting central entrance added c.1850. ## 2. Fifth specialist source — Fingal Thatch Project 2022

**Source:** Fingal County Council, “Fingal Thatch Project and Report”  
https://www.fingal.ie/fingal-thatch-project-and-report

The Council describes historic thatched buildings as an important part of Fingal's vernacular heritage and explains that the 2022 project sought to document surviving and lost thatched dwellings, their residents, building traditions, materials, skills, memories and photographs. It provides a project report and StoryMap.

**Use in CICAN:** This is regional/specialist context for the existing production record DUB-BLD-0180, The Thatch Cottage, Ballisk (NIAH 11329015). It is not a substitute for the NIAH record and does not establish the present survival, condition, exact coordinates, ownership or access status of any individual cottage. No new catalogue object is created from this source alone.
