## Four NIAH records checked

1. **Lucan House cemetery and medieval remains — NIAH 11201140.** Official record confirms an enclosed cemetery, remains of a church rebuilt c.1730, ruins of a medieval tower house and early eighteenth-century carved headstones. ITM E 303172, N 235187. The record describes a heritage group; do not split out individual headstones or ruins without separate evidence and identity checks.
   Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11201140/lucan-house-lucan-demesne-lucan-dublin

2. **Rathfarnham weir, River Dodder — NIAH 11211029.** Official record describes a two-stage weir, c.1800, originally supplying a mill stream, with cobbled/slab construction, sluice and overflow context. ITM E 314761, N 229659. Do not infer a surviving mill building from the weir record.
   Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11211029/rathfarnham-rathfarnham-dublin

3. **Saint Matthias' Church, Killiney-Ballybrack — NIAH 60260255.** Official record confirms design in 1834, construction in 1835 and substantial “improvement” in 1879–80. It documents granite construction, cruciform plan, tower, stained glass, interior joinery and the adjoining ecclesiastical group. ITM E 324923, N 224732. Preserve the distinction between original work and later alteration.
   Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/60260255/saint-matthias-church-killiney-ballybrack-church-road-loughlinstown-dublin

4. **Pastorville, Seatown Road, Swords — NIAH 11335003.** Official record names Pastorville and describes a red-brick parochial house, c.1895, designed by J. Kelly Freeman. ITM E 318548, N 246989. Do not infer current ownership or continuing religious use.
   Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11335003/swords-parochial-house-swords-demesne-fingal

**Held candidate:** Coolevin House, NIAH 11205035, was also located in the official NIAH search and remains a valid candidate for a subsequent NIAH batch. It was not counted among the four NIAH checks in this batch.
Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11205035/coolevin-house-lucan-newlands-road-neillstown-dublin

## Specialist source check

The National Monuments Service's *Archaeological Record of Monuments and Places: Dublin Manual* (1998) lists a Lucan historic-town group with separate entries for a possible tower house (DU017-01901), church (DU017-01902), graveyard (DU017-01903) and cross site (DU017-01904), with grid references in the Main Street, Lucan area.

Source PDF: https://www.archaeology.ie/app/uploads/2025/03/Archaeology-RMP-Dublin-Manual-1998-0013.pdf

**Evidence limitation:** this is an older RMP manual transcription. It is a specialist archaeological research lead and corroborative context, not proof that each listed monument corresponds exactly to a particular feature within the NIAH Lucan House cemetery polygon or that it survives in the same condition today. Check the current Historic Environment Viewer and individual monument records before creating separate catalogue objects.
