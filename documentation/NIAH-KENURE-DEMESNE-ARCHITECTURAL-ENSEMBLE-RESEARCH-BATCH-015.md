## 1. Kenure Park portico — NIAH 11324028

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11324028/kenure-park-portico-rush-demesne-rush

- Rating: Regional.
- Categories of special interest: Architectural, Artistic.
- NIAH original use: Country house.
- NIAH date range: 1835–1855; described c.1845.
- Raw NIAH coordinate pair: 326182, 255533.
- Date recorded: 9 August 2000.
- Description: Hexastyle Corinthian granite portico with pediment above, c.1845. Formerly adjoined a large three-storey house, c.1750, demolished in 1978.

**Independent context:** Fingal County Council's local history page states that Kenure House was demolished in 1978 and the free-standing portico is what remains of the mansion. Ask About Ireland's local heritage resource also describes the portico as the surviving entrance feature of the former mansion. The former mansion and surviving portico are not interchangeable objects: the NIAH record in this batch identifies the surviving portico.

**Research controls:** Do not describe the entire house as extant. Do not infer that the portico's NIAH point precisely describes its full curtilage or all former mansion remains. Preserve the NIAH registration as the portico object.

## 2. Kenure Park gate lodge, Skerries Road — NIAH 11324027

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11324027/kenure-park-skerries-road-rush-demesne-rush-dublin

- Rating: Regional.
- Category of special interest: Architectural.
- Original use: Gate lodge.
- NIAH date range: 1860–1870; described c.1865.
- Raw NIAH coordinate pair: 326595, 254916.
- Date recorded: 9 August 2000.
- Description: Detached three-bay single-storey gate lodge with advanced gable-fronted left bay and veranda to central entrance. Built at the same time as the parsonage of Kenure Church of Ireland church. The NIAH describes an irregular-plan roof with central valley, half-hipped front gable, terracotta ridge tiles, cement-rendered chimney stacks with brick, rock-faced limestone walls, tooled limestone quoins, red-brick dressings to openings, granite cills, timber sash and casement windows, timber veranda and slate roof.

**Relationship context:** The NIAH explicitly links its construction period to the parsonage, but this gate lodge is a separate registration from the Old Rectory (NIAH 11324025) and the Kenure Church of Ireland church (NIAH 11324026). Do not merge the gate lodge with the parsonage or church.

**RPS caution:** The Fingal RPS lists several Kenure structures, including RPS 273 for a gate lodge on Whitestown Road. Do not automatically map RPS 273 to this Skerries Road lodge; street and location differ. Verify the current RPS schedule and location before assigning any RPS ID.

## 3. The Old Rectory / Kenure Parsonage — NIAH 11324025

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11324025/the-old-rectory-skerries-road-rush-rush-co-dublin

- Rating: Regional.
- Categories of special interest: Architectural, Artistic.
- Previous name: Kenure Parsonage.
- Original use: Rectory/glebe/vicarage/curate's house.
- NIAH current use at survey: House.
- Date range: 1865–1870; built 1866.
- Raw NIAH coordinate pair: 326573, 254848.
- Date recorded: 10 August 2000.
- Description: Detached three-bay single-storey Church of Ireland rectory with half-dormer attic, T-shaped plan, projecting porch and full-height gabled projecting end bay. Designed by James Edward Rogers (1838–1896), who also designed nearby Kenure Church (NIAH 11324026). The NIAH describes pitched slate roofs, snecked limestone chimney stacks with red-brick stringcourses, red-brick-banded snecked rock-faced limestone walls, pointed segmental-headed window openings, and one-over-one timber sash windows. The interior is described as intact in the NIAH survey.

**Relationship context:** The Old Rectory and Kenure Church form a historically associated Church of Ireland group, but have separate NIAH registrations. The nearby gate lodge 11324027 is another distinct record. The shared architect James Edward Rogers is a source-supported relationship, not a reason to combine objects.

## 4. Kenure Park entrance gates and quadrant walls, Whitestown Road — NIAH 11324008

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11324008/kenure-park-rush-demesne-rush

- Rating: Regional.
- Categories of special interest: Architectural, Artistic.
- Original use: Demesne walls/gates/railings.
- Date range: 1720–1760; described c.1740.
- Raw NIAH coordinate pair: 326054, 254238.
- Date recorded: 3 September 2000.
- Description: Curved quadrant walls containing centrally placed wrought-iron entrance gates, c.1740, with robust granite piers having bands of vermiculated granite. Moulded granite corniced coping to piers is surmounted by spherical granite finials.

**Research controls:** This is a distinct entrance composition from the portico and from the separate Kenure Park gate lodges. Preserve its source label “Kenure Park, Whitestown Road” and its NIAH registration. Do not assume it is the same entrance as the Skerries Road gate lodge or the Kenure Park Whitestown Road gate lodge (NIAH 11324009).

### Kenure Park Whitestown Road gate lodge — NIAH 11324009
