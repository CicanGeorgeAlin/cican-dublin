## 1. New NIAH candidate — 11318003, Kenure Park gate lodge

The official NIAH individual record identifies **11318003**, Kenure Park, Rush Demesne, as a **detached three-bay, single-storey gate lodge**, dated **1820–1840**, with an approximate construction date of **c.1830**. The NIAH description records an advanced pedimented central bay, a rear extension c.1880, and a derelict condition at the time of the survey. It describes a partly remaining hipped slate roof, exposed roof construction where slates are lost, rendered chimney stack, nap-rendered walls, plinth/string courses, vermiculated quoins, recessed side-elevation panels, rendered moulded window surrounds and granite sills. The record is rated Regional, with architectural interest.

**NIAH coordinates as published:** 326427, 256074. The individual record page does not label the CRS beside this coordinate pair. Do not silently label it ITM or transform it.
**Survey date recorded:** 11 August 2000.
**Condition caution:** “Now derelict” is the NIAH survey description, not a current 2026 condition inspection.
**Identity caution:** This is a distinct NIAH registration from the two other gate lodges previously studied:
- 11324009 — Kenure Park, Whitestown Road, c.1845.
- 11324027 — Kenure Park, Skerries Road, c.1865.
It is also distinct from NIAH 11324008, the entrance gates and quadrant walls. Do not merge these records without evidence.

**Next:** Establish the exact entrance/estate location from official mapping and inspect whether the lodge survives, from lawful public access. Seek current photographs and current condition evidence.

## 4. Rush Harbour breakwater — preserve historic survey details and source coordinate

Fingal’s Coastal Architecture Study (Site 15) records **Rush Harbour breakwater**, map sheet DU008 / 2791, Irish National Grid coordinate **327302, 254330**, date range **1830–1850**, condition **Good** at the 22 September 2010 survey, and a mid-19th-century breakwater protecting the northern side of Rush Harbour. It describes a battered profile, rounded top and rock-face ashlar construction.

Fingal’s later proposed-addition assessment IH0386 describes the structure as two sections northwest of the harbour, built of squared random-coursed limestone walls with half-round coping and a central gap containing remains of wooden posts. It recommends addition to the RPS. The adopted Fingal Development Plan 2023–2029 RPS subsequently lists Rush Harbour Breakwater as **RPS 0891**.

**Important source distinction:** The 2010 survey’s “Good” condition is historical and does not establish present condition. The coordinate is explicitly labelled Irish National Grid in that study; retain it as a source coordinate, not founder GPS. Do not use it to place separate sections individually.
