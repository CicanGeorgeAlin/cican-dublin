## 1. Kenure Park, Whitestown Road gate lodge — NIAH 11324009

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11324009/kenure-park-whitestown-road-rush-demesne-rush-dublin

- Rating: Regional.
- Special interest: Architectural.
- Original use: Gate lodge.
- Date range: 1830–1860; described c.1845.
- Raw NIAH coordinate pair: 326049, 254261.
- Date recorded: 3 September 2000.
- Description: Detached three-bay single-storey former gate lodge with advanced central pedimented bay. Single-storey rear extension c.1990. The record states it is now in use as offices. The roof is double-pitched slate, hipped, with terracotta ridge tiles and rendered chimney stack with terracotta pots. Walls are coursed limestone, with a nap-rendered pediment, red-brick quoined dressed openings and granite plinth course. Openings include square-headed openings with red-brick dressings, granite cills and timber casements recorded c.1990, segmental-headed brick recess panels, and a pointed nap-rendered door surround with timber panelled door and granite steps.

**Critical distinction:** This is NIAH 11324009, Whitestown Road gate lodge. It is not NIAH 11324027, the separate Skerries Road gate lodge, and it is not NIAH 11324008, the quadrant walls and entrance gates. Do not merge the three records based on shared Kenure Park association.

**Current-use caution:** “Now in use as offices” is the NIAH survey statement. It is not a live occupancy check.

## 3. Sion House, Lower Main Street — NIAH 11324010

- Current NIAH survey use: House.
- Date range: 1840–1850; described c.1845.

## 6. Source and data quality notes

The NIAH Dublin data download page states that its CSV dataset contains registration number, location, classification, rating, description and appraisal fields. Location information is available in ITM, latitude/longitude and National Grid. The data is refreshed biannually as surveys are published.

The NIAH FAQ clarifies that inclusion in the NIAH is not itself equivalent to formal inclusion on a local authority Record of Protected Structures. Protected status must be verified from the current adopted local-authority RPS.
