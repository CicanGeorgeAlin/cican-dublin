## Conflicts, condition and access

The NIAH page describes the rear roof pitch as corrugated iron. Fingal's 2022 survey gives further detail: wheaten-straw thatch with a hipped and gabled form, bamboo rodding and lattice band, while noting the rear slope was covered with galvanised sheeting at the time of survey. These are not treated as a contradiction; the more detailed report records the survey-era roof condition. Neither source establishes the building's condition in November 2026.

The property is a private residence. Photography must be from a lawful public viewpoint or with owner/occupier permission. No report photograph was copied into the archive because a separate reuse licence was not confirmed.
