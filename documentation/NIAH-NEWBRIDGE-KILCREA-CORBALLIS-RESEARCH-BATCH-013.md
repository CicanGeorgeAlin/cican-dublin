### DUB-BLD-0190 — Kilcrea House (NIAH 11336025)

Official NIAH record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11336025/kilcrea-house-fingal

- Rating: Regional; special interest: Architectural, Artistic.
- Original/current use: Farmhouse.
- NIAH date range: 1780–1820; described c.1800.
- Recorded: 1 August 2000.
- Description: detached five-bay, two-storey farmhouse with a parallel range to the southeast and a return to the northeast. - The heritage audit notes the Kilcrea townland is documented from the fourteenth century and that the house and mill appear on Rocque's 1760 map. This earlier estate/house-site reference does not change the c.1800 date assigned to the present farmhouse.

## 3. Coordinate-system and transformation policy

The 2019 EIAR explicitly labels the coordinates for NIAH 11336004, 11336025 and 11336027 as ITM. These values were transformed from EPSG:2157 to WGS 84 using PROJ's “IRENET95 to WGS 84 (1)” transformation, with a stated transformation accuracy of 1 m.

The individual NIAH page coordinate for 11336028 is an Irish Grid / National Grid pair, treated as TM65 / EPSG:29902 and transformed using EPSG operation 1641. Do not relabel it as ITM.

Transformation accuracy does not establish the accuracy of the original source point. All four are source-derived structure locators, not founder GPS. Future official NIAH ITM data should be retained as ITM rather than replaced by a converted page-level Irish Grid pair.

## 4. Specialist archaeological review — Kilcrea

**Source:** Fingal County Council, *Donabate–Portrane Heritage Assets Audit Report* (2022), with site observations recorded in September 2021:
https://www.fingal.ie/sites/default/files/2023-03/Donabate%20Portrane%20Heritage%20Audit%20Report%20FINAL%2019.09.2022.pdf

The audit identifies five separate archaeological records in the Kilcrea landscape:

| SMR reference | Record | ITM Easting | ITM Northing | 2021 survey/access note |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| DU012-016001 | Kilcrea Church | 721676 | 748839 | Partially accessible; standing east gable and side walls, very overgrown |
| DU012-016002 | Kilcrea Graveyard | 721679 | 748831 | Partially accessible; disused and extremely overgrown |
| DU012-017 | Enclosure | 721855 | 749059 | Cropmark evidence; not visible at ground level |
| DU012-018 | Tidal mill | 722197 | 748671 | Inaccessible; millrace evidence and stone collapse noted |
| DU012-072 | Ring-ditch | 722093 | 748600 | Aerial-photograph evidence; no standing feature described |

Key distinctions:
- DU012-016001 church and DU012-016002 graveyard are separate but related records. The 2021 audit describes a plain rectangular church ruin, approximately 5.40 m wide, 8.75 m long and up to 3.50 m high, with east gable and side walls standing. The graveyard is no longer in use and contains partial church remains.
- DU012-017 is a cropmark enclosure, roughly 30 m across with an irregular annexe, not a visible ringfort.
- DU012-018 is a tidal-mill site identified through historic mapping and millrace evidence. The audit marks it inaccessible. Its attribution to Baltray Corn Mill / Rocque's 1756 map is a probable identification, not a proven surviving mill building.
- DU012-072 is retained as a separate ring-ditch record; the reviewed source does not support a more specific date or interpretation.

No new SMR object IDs or map pins were created. The audit's access and condition notes date from 2021; current survival, geometry and access must be checked through the NMS Historic Environment Viewer and safe lawful field observation before any separate archaeological catalogue records are promoted.
