## 1. 16 Lower Main Street / Jack the Bachelor's — NIAH 11324022

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11324022/main-street-rush-rush

- Rating: Regional.
- Special interest: Architectural, Social.
- Original use: House.
- NIAH date range: 1720–1800; description dates it c.1760.
- NIAH coordinate pair: 326732, 254103.
- Date recorded: 9 August 2000.
- NIAH description: Terraced five-bay two-storey thatched house with projecting entrance porch, c.1920. The roof is double-pitched thatch with a central rendered chimney stack. The walls are roughcast-rendered with rendered coping to the gables, a nap plinth course and porch. The openings include square-headed windows with rendered reveals and granite cills, probably original two-over-two timber sash windows, and a narrow small first-floor window. The projecting porch has a square-headed opening and timber door, c.1980.
- NIAH associates the house with “Jack the Bachelor”, described as a notorious eighteenth-century smuggler.

**Fingal Thatch Project:** FCC-TS-19; RPS 266; report name “Jack the Bachelors”; address “16 Main Street Lower”; townland Rush. The report labels the coordinates ITM east 726655.5238 and ITM north 754124.8784. Its survey description calls the building a south-facing terraced lobby-entry five-bay two-storey thatched former house, then used as a restaurant, with a windbreak and rear restaurant extension. It describes a wheaten-straw roof, central painted smooth-rendered chimney stack, concrete verge coping, painted roughcast front elevation, and surviving interior details including a lugged-and-kneed architrave and raised-and-fielded panelled door, c.1740.

**Identity and date caution:** The survey's 2022 description reports use as a restaurant at that time; do not assert present business use without current verification. The Jack the Bachelor association is attributed to NIAH and the Fingal survey; this batch does not independently prove the historical biography. Preserve source wording rather than expanding the story.

## 2. 14 Upper Main Street — NIAH 11324021

**Official NIAH survey search entry:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/search-vernacular-thatch-building-surveys/?county=&query=&search_page=12&thatch=truthy

**Fingal Thatch Project report:** FCC-TS-20; NIAH 11324021; RPS 267; address “14 Main Street Upper”; townland Rush.

- NIAH registration: 11324021.
- NIAH vernacular/thatch index description: Terraced four-bay single-storey thatched house, c.1780. The search entry describes a steeply thatched double-pitched roof, concrete coping to gables, overhanging thatched eaves and roughcast render to the front elevation.
- Fingal Thatch Project survey description: North-facing semidetached direct-entry four-bay single-storey with attic house, fronting directly onto the street. The report describes a steeply pitched wheaten-straw roof with flush bobbin ridge, lattice band and rows of bamboo; bamboo rodding to eaves and horizontal bamboo rows to verges; painted pebbledash render to clay walls with smooth-rendered plinth; and a partially exposed mud-wall construction to the east party wall. It records timber casement windows and a glazed timber door dated to the 1940s.
- The 2022 report states that the building was to be given to the community for community use under planning reference F21A/0445. This is a statement about the report's survey/planning context, not proof that the transfer or community use has since occurred.
- Fingal report coordinate fields: ITM east 726496.5575; ITM north 754132.8777.
- A separate GDD cultural heritage assessment associates NIAH 11324021 with 14 Main Street, Rush, NGR 326573, 254111, and RPS 267.

**Address caution:** “14 Upper Main Street” in the NIAH index and “14 Main Street Upper” in the Fingal survey align with the GDD assessment's “14 Main Street”. Retain all variants as source-specific labels; do not silently standardize street naming without the canonical NIAH source row. The Fingal report's ITM pair and GDD's NGR pair are different coordinate representations; preserve both with their source CRS labels and do not substitute one for the other.

## 3. Shamrock Cottage, Sandy Road — NIAH 11324019

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11324019/shamrock-cottage-sandy-road-rush-rush-dublin

- Rating: Regional.
- Special interest: Architectural, Social.
- Original use: House.
- NIAH date range: 1830–1870; description dates it c.1850.
- NIAH coordinate pair: 326304, 254202.
- Date recorded: 10 September 2001.
- Description: End-of-terrace four-bay single-storey thatched house, c.1850, with projecting entrance porch. The double-pitched thatch roof has a nap-rendered chimney stack; the porch roof includes corrugated iron and thatch. Walls are nap-rendered, ruled and lined with rendered dressings. Openings include square-headed windows with rendered reveals and stone cills, probably original two-over-two timber sash windows, small timber windows with margin panes, and a tongue-and-groove timber door with overlight.

**Fingal Thatch Project:** FCC-TS-22; RPS 268; “Shamrock Cottage, Sandy Road, Rush”. The GDD cultural heritage assessment independently lists NIAH 11324019 at Sandy Road and gives the same NGR pair 326304, 254202, describing it as a thatched cottage.

**Relationship control:** Keep separate from Tigín (11324018), the adjacent former thatched shop (FCC-TS-23 / RPS 270 in Fingal's schedule), and the separate Sandy Road house NIAH 11324017. Proximity does not make these one building or one NIAH object.

## 4. Sandy Road thatched house — NIAH 11324017

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11324017/sandy-road-rush-rush

- Rating: Regional.
- Special interest: Architectural, Social.
- Original use: House.
- NIAH date range: 1750–1850; description dates it c.1800.
- NIAH coordinate pair: 326247, 254162.
- Date recorded: 10 August 2000.
- Description: End-of-terrace three-bay single-storey rendered mud-walled thatched house, c.1800, with a single-storey rear extension, c.1960. The roof is double-pitched thatch with a small rendered chimney stack. The front elevation is nap-rendered with rendered dressings and granite coping to the parapet wall; the right side elevation has a nap-rendered plinth and rendered coping on the gable wall, with a section of mud wall exposed. The square-headed openings have rendered reveals and stone cills; fixed panes were recorded as boarded up in the NIAH survey description. The timber-panelled door is dated c.1980.

**Independent assessment:** The Greater Dublin Drainage Alternative Sites Assessment, Appendix 6 Cultural Heritage Assessment, identifies NIAH 11324017 as a thatched cottage at Sandy Road, Rush, with NGR 326247, 254162 and repeats the description of the mud-walled thatched house. It notes the property was within the area of the proposed northern outfall in that assessment. That historical infrastructure-planning statement is not evidence that any project has been built or that the property is currently affected.

**RPS caution:** Do not automatically assign RPS 270 to NIAH 11324017. Fingal's current RPS schedule describes RPS 270 as a separate three-bay former thatched shop on Sandy Road, adjacent to Tigín and Shamrock Cottage. The GDD assessment's table maps RPS 268 to NIAH 11324019 and RPS 269 to NIAH 11324018; it does not establish RPS 270 as the identifier for NIAH 11324017. Keep the distinction explicit.

## 5. Specialist source — Fingal Thatch Project 2022

**Fingal County Council project page:** https://www.fingal.ie/fingal-thatch-project-and-report  
**Full 2022 report:** https://www.fingal.ie/sites/default/files/2023-02/Fingal%20Thatch%20Project%202022_Report.pdf

The Council's project was designed to document surviving and lost thatched dwellings, architectural features, residents, traditional skills, materials, memories and photographs. Its report lists 62 structures surveyed in 2022, of which 51 were thatched; it reports 29 historic thatched roofs as extant in the county at the time of the survey.

Rush records relevant to this batch:
- FCC-TS-19 — Jack the Bachelor's, 16 Main Street Lower — NIAH 11324022; RPS 266.
- FCC-TS-20 — 14 Main Street Upper — NIAH 11324021; RPS 267.
- FCC-TS-22 — Shamrock Cottage, Sandy Road — NIAH 11324019; RPS 268.
- FCC-TS-23 — Sandy Road — listed as a separate survey location; the report map/gazetteer identity must be checked before linking it to any specific NIAH record.

The project survey is a valuable independent source, especially for roof materials, construction details and survey-era use. It does not prove present condition or present occupancy. Use the FCC-TS identifier as a separate source reference, not as a replacement for the NIAH registration.
