## 1. Rush Martello Tower — NIAH 11324023

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11324023/rush-martello-tower-rush-rush-fingal

- Rating: National.
- Categories of special interest: Architectural, Historical, Social, Technical.
- Original use: Martello tower.
- NIAH date range: 1800–1810; description dates it c.1805.
- Raw coordinate pair displayed by NIAH: 325763, 254030.
- Date recorded: 9 August 2000.
- Description: Circular-plan Martello tower with tapered profile and machicolation above the former entrance. The NIAH describes a thick parapet concealing the roof, a ruinous red-brick wall built on the roof, nap-rendered walls, and square-headed openings with rendered reveals. A tongue-and-groove timber-panelled door is set high in the wall.

**Fingal corroboration:** The Fingal County Council Record of Protected Structures lists the Martello Tower at Tower Street, Rush, as RPS 265a and describes it as an early nineteenth-century circular military stone tower. A separate Fingal constraints study describes the tower as c.1805 and notes that it is in a private garden, with the access overgrown at the time of that study. That is a dated access observation, not confirmation of present access conditions.

**Research controls:** Keep this Martello tower distinct from Drumanagh Martello Tower and the other coastal towers in Fingal. Do not infer public access or permission to enter the garden. The NIAH coordinate pair is transcribed as displayed; the individual page does not label its CRS beside the coordinate value. No transformation or public map pin is approved.

## 2. The Thatched Cottage, Skerries Road, Rush — NIAH 11324024

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11324024/skerries-road-rush-rush

- Rating: Regional.
- Categories of special interest: Architectural, Social.
- Original use: House.
- NIAH date range: 1740–1820; description dates the cottage c.1780.
- Raw coordinate pair displayed by NIAH: 326643, 254655.
- Date recorded: 10 August 2000.
- Description: Detached four-bay single-storey thatched house with projecting entrance porch and a single-storey extension to the left, c.1950. The NIAH describes a double-pitched thatched roof, raised rendered gable ends, three small rendered chimney stacks, a clay-tiled roof to the extension and a slate porch roof. The walls are nap-rendered to the thatched house and roughcast-rendered to the extension. The openings include single-pane sash windows, a small timber casement to the gable indicating a loft, and a tongue-and-groove door.

**Fingal Thatch Project corroboration:** The 2022 Fingal Thatch Project report lists “The Thatch Cottage, Skerries Road, Rush” as survey location FCC-TS-18. This independently confirms that the building was included in the Council's Rush thatch-survey programme; it does not prove that the roof remains thatched today.

**Research controls:** Keep distinct from the Sandy Road cottages and the other Rush thatched buildings. The NIAH description is a survey-era description; do not state current roof condition without a recent survey or field observation. The coordinate pair is raw source data and is not transformed in this batch.

## 3. Tigín, Sandy Road, Rush — NIAH 11324018

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11324018/tigin-sandy-road-rush-rush-dublin

- Rating: Regional.
- Categories of special interest: Architectural, Social.
- Original use: House.
- Current NIAH use: House.
- NIAH date range: 1750–1810; description dates it c.1780.
- Raw coordinate pair displayed by NIAH: 326262, 254166.
- Date recorded: 10 September 2001.
- Description: Terraced two-bay single-storey thatched house with a single-bay rear extension, c.1930, and a rubble-stone shed to the rear, c.1880. The NIAH describes a double-pitched thatched roof, nap-rendered walls with plinth, a low square-headed door with rendered reveals and tongue-and-groove door leaf, and square-headed window with rendered reveals and no cills.

**Fingal Thatch Project corroboration:** The 2022 report lists “Tigin, Sandy Road, Rush” as FCC-TS-21. Fingal's RPS schedule also lists Tigin, Sandy Road, as RPS 269 and describes it as a two-bay single-storey thatched dwelling near the junction with Main Street.

**Research controls:** Do not confuse Tigín with Shamrock Cottage (NIAH 11324019 / FCC-TS-22), the nearby Sandy Road thatched shop (FCC-TS-23), or other Sandy Road dwellings. The Fingal survey labels corroborate the locality and thatch-project inclusion, but exact one-to-one identity should be checked against the report's map and detailed address before assigning an RPS cross-reference in the production record.

## 4. Kilbush Lane, Rush / Old Kilbush House — NIAH 11324032

**Official NIAH record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/11324032/old-kilbush-house-kilbush-lane-rush-rush

- Rating: Regional.
- Categories of special interest: Architectural, Social.
- Original use: House.
- Current NIAH use: House.
- NIAH date range: 1820–1880; description dates it c.1850.
- Raw coordinate pair displayed by NIAH: 327077, 254322.
- Date recorded: 10 August 2000.
- Description: Detached seven-bay single-storey thatched house with attic storey, c.1850. A timber projecting entrance porch was added c.1980, with a flat-roofed single-storey rear extension c.1970. The NIAH describes a double-pitched thatched roof with overhanging eaves, concrete coping to the gables, a small rendered chimney stack, roughcast-rendered walls and timber casement windows.

**Fingal Thatch Project corroboration:** The 2022 Rush thatch-location map lists “Kilbush House, Kilbush Lane, Rush” as FCC-TS-27. Fingal's earlier RPS schedule identifies Old Kilbush House at Kilbush Lane, Rush, as RPS 264 and describes a seven-bay single-storey thatched dwelling with attic storey.

**Research controls:** Preserve the NIAH registration and both source labels (Kilbush Lane / Old Kilbush House) in the source reconciliation. Do not assume the current thatch condition from the historic survey. A 2025 planning application on Kilbush Lane concerns a development site; its proximity alone does not establish that it relates to this NIAH property. No connection is asserted in this batch.

## 5. Specialist source — Fingal Thatch Project 2022

**Primary local-authority source:** Fingal County Council, “Fingal Thatch Project and Report”  
https://www.fingal.ie/fingal-thatch-project-and-report

The Council describes a project to document surviving and lost thatched dwellings, their residents, building traditions, materials, skills, memories and photographs. The 2022 report's Rush map labels:
- FCC-TS-18 — The Thatch Cottage, Skerries Road, Rush.
- FCC-TS-21 — Tigin, Sandy Road, Rush.
- FCC-TS-27 — Kilbush House, Kilbush Lane, Rush.

This specialist survey provides useful corroboration for the locality and inclusion of these named structures in a Fingal thatch-survey project. It does not by itself prove present survival, present roof material, condition, exact NIAH registration mapping or access. Use the individual NIAH registrations as the primary identifiers and retain the Council survey IDs as separate source references.

- RPS 264: Old Kilbush House, Kilbush Lane, Rush.
- RPS 268: Shamrock Cottage, Sandy Road, Rush.

The 2022 Fingal Thatch Project's Rush map includes a wider set of thatched locations, including FCC-TS-18 through FCC-TS-27. This demonstrates that the four records in this batch sit within a wider local vernacular-heritage cluster. It is not permission to merge the records into one site or to infer the identity of unreviewed buildings.
