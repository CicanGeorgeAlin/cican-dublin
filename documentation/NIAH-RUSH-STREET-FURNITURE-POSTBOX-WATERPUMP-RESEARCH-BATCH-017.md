## 1. Whitestown Road cast-iron post box — NIAH 11324007

**Official NIAH search result:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/?county=&date_from=&date_to=&group=&location_type=building&query=Post+box&search_page=24&type=

- Registration number: 11324007.
- Location label: Whitestown Road, Rush, County Dublin.
- Description: Wall-mounted cast-iron post box, c.1905, with “ER VII” monogram.
- The search result does not expose the complete individual record, full appraisal, exact coordinates, rating or precise mounting location.

**Interpretation limits:** The “ER VII” cipher indicates the Edward VII period in the NIAH description, but do not infer an exact installation year from c.1905. Do not infer that the box remains in situ, is still operational, or is accessible. It is a separate object from the Corduff Common wall-mounted cast-iron post box NIAH 11328003, which belongs to a different site and has its own archive relationship.

**Fieldwork plan:** First retrieve the individual NIAH page and GIS location. Then verify the box from a lawful public viewpoint. Record the wall or pier into which it is set, its orientation, visible maker's marks, condition and any postal markings. Take a context photograph and a close-up only where safe and lawful. Founder GPS should be recorded separately from any NIAH point.

## 6. Official source context

The NIAH data-download page states that its Dublin CSV contains published Ministerial Recommendations indexed by registration number and includes location, classification, rating, description, appraisal and links to records and images. The dataset is refreshed biannually as surveys are published.

The NIAH FAQ notes that the inventory includes small functional structures such as post boxes and water pumps, but NIAH inclusion alone does not make a structure a protected structure. Formal protected status must be checked against the relevant local authority's adopted Record of Protected Structures.
