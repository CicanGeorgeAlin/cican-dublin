# Rush North Beach EWIP Follow-up and NMS Archive Route — Research Batch 217

**Project:** CICAN Dublin Archive  
**Research status:** Documentation-only; no production catalogue or map changes  
**Focus:** Separate the Rush North Beach second-season monitoring record (licence 11E0090) from the 2011C4 flint group and identify the formal route to the original excavation and museum records  
**Research date:** 20 November 2026

## Executive finding

The 2024 Park Road, Kenure archaeological assessment identifies a second-season East–West Interconnector monitoring project at **Rush North Beach**, under licence **11E0090**, Excavations Bulletin reference **2012:245**. It says no archaeological deposits or features were identified during those works. The assessment cross-references Excavations 2011, no. 230, for the first season.

This is a separate licence and coastal location from the 2010 general cable monitoring report **10E0154**, which records flint artefacts in fields immediately north of Rogerstown Estuary. No source reviewed here links licence 11E0090 to the NMI 2011C4 flint accessions. It should not be treated as a source for those artefacts.

The National Monuments Service (NMS) now publishes a clear archive request route: request excavation reports by their exact licence numbers from **nmarchive@housing.gov.ie**. Its guidance states that the Archive Unit holds submitted digital and hard-copy reports from licensed and unlicensed archaeological investigations, and that researchers should use the Excavations.ie database to identify the correct licence number before requesting reports.

## 1. Rush North Beach second-season monitoring

The 2024 Park Road, Kenure assessment, Appendix 2, lists:
- Licence: **11E0090**
- Location: **Rush North Beach, Rush, Dublin**
- Excavations Bulletin reference: **2012:245**
- Author: **T. Bolger**
- Description: monitoring connected with the EirGrid East–West Interconnector cable; second season of project work; no archaeological deposits or features identified.

Source: https://consult.fingal.ie/en/system/files/materials/35680/Park%20Road%2C%20Kenure%2C%20Rush%2C%20Co.%20Dublin%20AIA%2024E0330.pdf

The same appendix identifies the earlier season by reference to Excavations 2011, no. 230. The 2024 assessment's summary does not give object finds, contexts or a connection to the 2011C4 registrations.

## 2. Distinguish the related EWIP licences

| Licence | Published context | What can be said |
|---|---|---|
| **10E0154** | 2010 general East–West Interconnector monitoring across multiple Dublin townlands | Records flint artefacts (arrowhead, possible blades) from topsoil stripping north of Rogerstown Estuary, east of the railway. |
| **11E0090** | Rush North Beach, second season of EWIP monitoring; Excavations 2012:245 | 2024 assessment says no archaeological deposits or features were identified. |
| **11E0235** | Rogerstown burial-ground testing | Separate investigation of human remains and an early medieval cemetery discovered during cable works. |
| **11E0283** | Identified in the GDD archaeological review as a separate EWIP landfall licence, likely associated with investigation of an early medieval site south of the northern outfall boundary | The cited GDD report describes this as a likelihood, not a confirmed relationship to 2011C4. |

**Interpretation:** These licences belong to related infrastructure works in the same wider coastal district, but they are not interchangeable. The fact that the 2011C4 registration prefix contains “2011” does not establish that the items came from 11E0090, 11E0283, 10E0154 or any other licence.

## 3. Formal route to the original excavation reports

The current NMS page **Access to and Deposit of Collections** states:
- The NMS Archive holds reports from archaeological excavations and development-led investigations for 1990–2024, including licensed and unlicensed work.
- The annual Finding Aid is an Excel index of reports held by the Archive Unit.
- Excavations.ie can be used to identify excavation licence numbers.
- Requests should be emailed to **nmarchive@housing.gov.ie**.
- Licence numbers must be quoted in the accepted format, for example `04E1254`; requests with incorrect licence numbers may be returned for correction.
- Copyright rests with the report author; researchers must agree to the relevant copyright terms.

Official guidance: https://www.archaeology.ie/collections-and-publications/collections/access-and-deposit-of-collections/

This gives a practical route to the full report for **10E0154**, the Rush North Beach report **11E0090**, and the separate landfall investigation **11E0283**. The request should ask the Archive Unit to confirm the report title, author and whether the relevant finds register and appendices are held with each report.

## 4. Recommended targeted archive request

Request the following, in numerical order:
1. **10E0154** — full East–West Interconnector monitoring report, including finds register, context sheets, field plans, artefact catalogue, photographs and correspondence relating to flint recovered north of Rogerstown Estuary.
2. **11E0090** — Rush North Beach second-season monitoring report (Excavations 2012:245), including any finds register confirming whether any stray finds were recovered despite the summary statement that no archaeological deposits or features were identified.
3. **11E0283** — report and appendices for the EWIP landfall investigation, to confirm its actual location, scope and relationship (if any) to the Rogerstown cemetery discovery.
4. **11E0235** — only if required to cross-check the cemetery investigation separately; do not merge it with the flint provenance inquiry.

Separately, request from the NMI/NMS collections archive the original Topographical File registration records for:
- `2011C4:1614–1618`
- `2011C4:1848–1920`
- `2011C4:1933–2003`

Ask whether the museum can provide the registration ledger, findspot and finder fields, dates of receipt, collection history, object photographs and any linked excavation licence or report number.

## 6. Archive treatment and limits

- Keep 11E0090 separate from the 2011C4 flint group unless original records establish a connection.
- Keep 11E0235 cemetery testing separate from the flint provenance investigation.
- Treat the GDD report's possible link between 11E0283 and the early medieval site as a qualified statement, not a confirmed identification.
- Do not interpret “no archaeological deposits or features” in the 11E0090 summary as proof that no stray object was ever recovered; the full report and finds register would be needed to answer that narrower question.
- Do not add map pins for the 2011C4 entries or assign them to Rush North Beach.
