## Verification and rights policy

A photograph appearing in a search result, Commons category, institutional gallery or Flickr page is not automatically reusable. Before adding it, inspect the individual file/page for creator, date, licence and any restrictions; record the original page and direct image URL. If reuse permission is unclear, preserve the link as a research lead, not as an archive image asset.

Do not mistake an image of the larger property for a close-up record of an inscription or sculptural detail. One contextual photograph can be useful, but it must be labelled as context and must not imply that every separately catalogued feature is visibly documented.
