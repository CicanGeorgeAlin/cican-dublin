## Significant photographic discoveries

### 1. North Strand Bombing Photographic Collection — 1941

The dedicated collection page identifies 57 photographs held by Dublin City Library and Archive. The images document individual addresses and streets affected by the May 1941 bombing, including North Strand Road, Summerhill Parade, Shamrock Place, Aldborough Place, North William Street, Buckingham Place, North Clarence Street, Newcomen Court, Charleville Mall and other streets.

The page explains that Dublin Corporation commissioned photographer H. McCrae to document damage for insurance assessment. This is a high-value historical source for catalogue objects that can be matched to the same surviving building or exact address, but images of destroyed or damaged buildings must be described as historical views, not present-day photographs.

Rights: originals are held by Dublin City Library and Archive. The collection page does not grant a blanket reuse licence. Seek permission or confirm terms for each image before republication.

Source: https://northstrandbombing.ie/photographic-collection/

### 2. Daly Bros. shop, 7 Marino Mart, Fairview — 1926

UCD Digital Library's G. & T. Crampton Photograph Archive provides a catalogue record for a black-and-white photograph of Daly Bros. butcher shop, 7 Marino Mart, Fairview. It identifies the creator as G. & T. Crampton (Firm), date 1926, the building address and coordinates 53.36412945, -6.232651342.

The rights statement is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and says commercial use requires contacting UCD Digital Library. Because CICAN's archive may be used in a commercial publication or colouring-book project, do not assume this licence permits the intended reuse. Contact UCD for permission and clarify whether non-commercial display is permitted for the intended website and print use.

This photograph is a strong Fairview historical streetscape/building lead, but it is not automatically a match to a separate catalogue record unless the catalogue contains the same building/address.

Source: https://digital.ucd.ie/c0097/items/c0097_o0063.html

### 3. Marino Local History Society — local photographic appeal

The Marino Local History Society describes its “Our History in One Thousand Images” appeal and a local photographic collection covering Fairview, Clontarf, Ballybough, North Strand and Drumcondra. It explicitly states that contributors retain copyright and donated photographs are published with the owner's permission.

This is a particularly relevant source for photographs of vanished shops, historic streetscapes and local buildings. Contact the society and ask for image-by-image permission, creator attribution, dates and precise location identifications.

Source: https://marinolocalhistorysociety.wordpress.com/

### 4. Dublin City Council visual archive

Search results report that Dublin City Libraries and Archive made a large historic image and document collection searchable online, including North Strand Bombing photographs, old street scenes and Fairview traffic. The archive is a discovery route; the original catalogue entry and rights conditions must be inspected for each candidate.

Source: https://digital.libraries.dublincity.ie/vital/access/manager/Index

### 5. National Library of Ireland aerial photograph

The NLI catalogue has a 1954 December aerial photograph record titled “Flooding at North Strand, Co. Dublin showing a damaged railway bridge”, by Alexander Campbell Morgan. The catalogue states that reproduction rights are owned by the National Library of Ireland. This is a historical area-context lead, not a verified match to an individual catalogue object.

Source: https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000732823

## Catalogue relevance and matching cautions

The live missing-record list includes DUB-STR-0056, “North Strand Road — George V McDowall Steven pillar postbox”, and DUB-BLD-0086, “6 Seville Place — Victorian townhouse with historic entrance fabric”. The 1941 collection's street-addressed photographs may provide historical context for nearby buildings, but no exact match to either object was established in this cycle.

The Fairview/Marino records in the catalogue should be checked against the UCD Daly Bros. photograph by address and coordinates before any association is made. The Daly Bros. photograph must not be attached to another Marino/Fairview property merely because it is nearby.

## Quality-control rule

A collection containing many relevant photographs does not mean every catalogue object is covered. Only add a photograph after matching the depicted object/address and recording its source, creator, date, direct image reference and permission/licence basis. Never count a research lead as a photograph in the visual inventory.
