## North Strand bombing collection — address-level audit

Dublin City Council confirms that the 57 photographs were commissioned after the bombing of 31 May 1941, taken by H. McCrae from June through October 1941, and are held by Dublin City Library and Archive. The council's image rights statement reserves copyright; personal non-commercial use is permitted under stated conditions, while commercial reproduction requires written permission and may incur a fee.

Key source pages:
- Collection index: https://northstrandbombing.ie/photographic-collection/
- Dublin City Council collection and rights statement: https://www.dublincity.ie/library/blog/north-strand-bombing-1941
- Flickr album: https://www.flickr.com/photos/dublincitypubliclibraries/albums/72157606884699486/

Two individually described images were inspected as representative records:
1. Flickr image 25/57 identifies Clarence Street North nos. 61–64 and Synnot Street; taken 9 June 1941: https://www.flickr.com/photos/dublincitypubliclibraries/2786096287
2. Flickr image 57/57 identifies North Strand nos. 12–14, named shopkeepers and visible tram tracks; taken 31 October 1941: https://www.flickr.com/photos/dublincitypubliclibraries/2786098335

The 57-image index also names addresses including North Strand Road nos. 11–16, 17–22, 23–29, 30–35, 153–164 and 165–170; Summerhill Parade nos. 17 and 43–45; 5 Shamrock Place; 4–6 Aldborough Place; 5–10 and 1–5 Aldborough Avenue; 2 and 4 Dunne Street; 47–48 and 2–3 North William Street; 6–7 Bailey's Row; 3–5 Charleville Mall; 16 Buckingham Place; 39 Strandville Avenue; 5–5a Newcomen Court; 4–5 Empress Place; 23 Rutland Road; and 582 North Circular Road.

### Catalogue matching outcome

These address descriptions are useful historical evidence and a targeted search index. However, the current missing catalogue records do not contain an exact street-number match for the two inspected Flickr examples (Clarence Street North 61–64; North Strand 12–14). Nearby or same-street proximity is not sufficient to associate a historical image with a specific catalogue object. No North Strand bombing image was added to the visual inventory.

Important distinction: these photographs depict the aftermath of the 1941 bombing. They may serve as historical views of a site only when the surviving object/site is positively matched; they cannot substitute for a current photograph of a surviving postbox, doorway, plaque or other street detail.

## Further Clontarf and Marino leads

### Vernon Lodge — DUB-BLD-0040, DUB-INS-0004 and DUB-INS-0005

NIAH entry 50030164 has a gallery of images and identifies the 1885 carved limestone pedestrian entrance, its date stone, coat of arms, crest and motto. This is an exact-object image lead for the building and its inscription records. The NIAH page is authoritative for identification and description, but the page does not establish an open reuse licence for its photographs. Seek permission/terms before importing images.

Source: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030164/vernon-lodge-41a-castle-avenue-clontarf-dublin-3-co-dublin

Laurie O'Connor Architects also publishes a Vernon Lodge conservation project with photography credited to Myles Shelly. This may contain useful context imagery, but no reuse permission was established.

Source: https://www.laurieoconnorarchitects.com/work-1/project-four-jffkl

### Clontarf Stationmaster's House — DUB-BLD-0043

NIAH record 50030283 confirms the address 96 Howth Road and describes the c.1880 polychrome brick stationmaster's house. This provides a reliable exact identity/address cross-check. The retrieved page did not establish a reusable photograph; no image added.

Source: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030283/clontarf-railway-station-96-howth-road-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin

### Marino Institute of Education / former St Patrick's Monastery — DUB-BLD-0042

NIAH record 50130265 confirms the former Christian Brothers monastery, built 1928–29, at Griffith Avenue, Marino. The architectural description provides a detailed visual checklist for future image matching. The page's retrieved content did not establish an open image licence.

Source: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50130265/marino-institute-of-education-griffith-avenue-marino-dublin-3-dublin

### 238 Clonliffe Road — DUB-BLD-0082 and associated detail records

NIAH record 50120194 confirms the c.1860 house and specifically describes the inscribed granite street-name plaque, fanlight/doorcase, bootscrape, railings and gate. This is an excellent authoritative identification source for the catalogue's building and detail records. The retrieved page does not establish an open reuse licence for its images.

Source: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50120194/238-clonliffe-road-dublin-3-dublin
