## 1. Clontarf Road Victorian pillar postbox — DUB-STR-0043

The catalogue's existing photograph is Tdr112's 8 September 2022 image:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dublin_-_Clontarf_Road_pillar_postbox_-_20220908183803.jpg

Its Commons page identifies the subject as the Clontarf Road pillar postbox, gives camera coordinates 53.358772, -6.189932, and records author Tdr112 and CC BY-SA 4.0. The archive record's source-derived point is 53.3587049403907, -6.189826723248944, approximately 12 metres away. The image is a strong direct match and already appears in the visual inventory.

A second image was found:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dublin_-_Clontarf_Road_pillar_postbox_-_20230827175714.jpg

Commons identifies the image as Clontarf Road pillar postbox, taken 27 August 2023 by Dvcireland, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. It provides another independently authored view of the same already-covered object. It was not added because the record already has a suitable direct photograph and the purpose of this phase is to cover missing catalogue locations, not inflate the number of image entries for already-covered records.

## 2. Clontarf Baths — DUB-BLD-0060

NIAH register 50030148 has an image gallery and describes the historic swimming pool built in 1881, its surviving outer walls, north-shore structures, railings and gates. The official NIAH entry is a strong exact-object identification source:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030148/clontarf-baths-clontarf-road-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin

A Flickr photograph titled “Clontarf Baths” by LukeFromDublin was also identified. It was taken 11 August 2011 and uploaded 9 August 2011:
https://www.flickr.com/photos/27864448@N08/6027285150

The description confirms it depicts the former privately owned open-air seawater swimming pool, closed in 1996. The image is a direct historical view of the target. However, the retrieved Flickr page did not establish a reuse licence or permission. It is therefore a research lead only and has not been imported.

## 3. Clontarf Promenade bandstand — DUB-BLD-0055

NIAH register 50030149 has a gallery with images and describes the round-plan reinforced-concrete bandstand built circa 1955, attached to the promenade retaining wall west of the baths:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030149/clontarf-road-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin

This is an exact-object image lead for a currently uncovered catalogue record. NIAH is authoritative for identification and description, but no open image reuse licence was established. No image added.
