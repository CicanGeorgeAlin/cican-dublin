## 1. New reference added — DUB-STR-0022, Merrion Square

A Wikimedia Commons photograph was identified:
- File: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Merrion_Square_South,_Dublin_(507052)_(30505692474).jpg
- Photographer: Robert Linsdell
- Photograph date: 12 June 2015
- Camera coordinates: 53.339620, -6.251859
- Licence: CC BY 2.0
- Attribution: Robert Linsdell, “Merrion Square South, Dublin (507052)”, 12 June 2015, via Wikimedia Commons (CC BY 2.0).

The catalogue record DUB-STR-0022 is the Edward VII cast-iron pillar postbox at Merrion Square near Merrion Street Upper. Its NIAH-derived point is 53.3396091, -6.2518799. The photo's camera coordinates are approximately 2 metres from that point.

The file is listed in Commons' “Post boxes in Dublin” category. The location match and category support treating it as a strong third-party visual reference. The record's founder-original photo status remains unchanged; the image is not represented as a founder photograph and should not be used to claim a close-up of the Edward VII cipher or maker's mark without visual inspection.

The reference was added to data/visual-assets.json and its source was registered in data/sources.json:
- Visual source ID: WMC-DUB2-MERRION-SQUARE-POSTBOX-2015-01
- Licence URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/

## 3. Additional discovery source

Dublin Historic Maps maintains a “Dublin Post Boxes” map compiled by Brian and Aidan Hollinshead:
https://www.dublinhistoricmaps.ie/miscellany/post_boxes/index.html

A separate Mailbox Map page reports 589 postboxes in Dublin and describes its data as sourced from An Post and OpenStreetMap contributors:
https://dennisgavrilenko.com/projects/mailbox/ie/dublin/

These are discovery/indexing aids, not photographic licences or proof that an individual historic box matches a catalogue object. The Dublin Historic Maps page was accessible but its interactive map data was not exposed in the text extraction during this cycle.
