## 3. North Strand bombing archive — historical context, not yet a postbox match

Dublin City Libraries' 1941 North Strand bombing collection includes:
https://www.flickr.com/photos/dublincitypubliclibraries/2786021559

The caption identifies North Strand Road nos. 30–35 and a postbox blown onto its side in the foreground. The photograph was taken 4 June 1941 by H. McCrae and belongs to the Dublin City Libraries and Archive collection. The page states © Dublin City Council.

This is an important historical image, but it is not yet a confirmed image of catalogue object DUB-STR-0056. The 1941 address block and the current catalogue pin must be checked against historical street numbering and the former cinema location. Copyright permission is not confirmed for republication; the page identifies Dublin City Council rights. No reference added.
