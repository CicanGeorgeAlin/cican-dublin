## 1. Fitzwilliam Place 1993 image — investigated, not attached

Commons file:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Post_box,_Fitzwilliam_Place,_Dublin_-_geograph.org.uk_-_3301013.jpg

The Commons file page and full-resolution image were inspected:
- Photographer: Christopher Hilton
- Date: 26 July 1993
- Coordinates recorded for both camera and depicted object: 53.334292, -6.251482
- Reuse licence: CC BY-SA 2.0
- Image: 1,066 × 1,600 pixels
- Attribution: Christopher Hilton, “Post box, Fitzwilliam Place, Dublin”, via Geograph / Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 2.0).

Potential catalogue object:
- DUB-STR-0023
- NIAH registration 50930153
- Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50930153/fitzwilliam-place-dublin-2-co-dublin
- NIAH description: freestanding cast-iron pillar box erected c.1910, with ER VII / Post Office lettering and crown; located at the south-east end of Fitzwilliam Place.
- NIAH Irish Grid coordinates: 316436, 232786.
- Catalogue point: 53.3327505, -6.2529561.

The image itself is a close-up of a postbox and its collection panel, but it does not establish that it is the NIAH Edward VII box. The Commons object coordinate is materially displaced from the catalogue point (roughly 200 metres), and the photograph's visible upper-body lettering/cipher is not sufficiently legible to prove the ER VII identity. The NIAH location description and image location therefore remain unresolved against each other. The image is rights-cleared for reuse, but object identity is not established. **Do not attach it to DUB-STR-0023.**

This resolves the previous batch's lead as an unconfirmed candidate, not a newly covered location.

## 2. Targeted Dublin postbox image searches

Searched exact street-name and postbox combinations for:
- Haddon Road — DUB-STR-0045
- Mount Prospect Avenue — DUB-STR-0049
- Kincora Grove — DUB-STR-0050
- Dunluce Road — DUB-STR-0051
- Malahide Road — DUB-STR-0052
- Grand Canal Quay — DUB-STR-0055

Results:
- Haddon Road search returned an Edward VIII postbox at Shire Lane / Haddon Road, but its coordinates are in Buckinghamshire, England (approximately 51.652, -0.526), not Dublin. Rejected as a geographic false positive.
- Grand Canal Quay searches returned general quay/building photographs, including a CC BY 2.0 streetscape, but no verified image of the McDowall Steven postbox at DUB-STR-0055. Not attached.
- Malahide searches surfaced a general “Malahide, green Irish post box” Commons file, but the available result did not establish that it depicts the Malahide Road object DUB-STR-0052. Not attached.
- No exact, geographically verified and rights-cleared matches were established for Mount Prospect Avenue, Kincora Grove or Dunluce Road in this search pass.

A broad Commons category for green postboxes in the Republic of Ireland was also reviewed. It contains multiple Dublin-region postbox photographs, but a category-level Dublin label is not sufficient to assign an image to one of these exact catalogue objects.

## 3. North Strand bombing photograph — still unresolved

Previously identified historical image:
https://www.flickr.com/photos/dublincitypubliclibraries/2786021559

The available catalogue caption identifies North Strand Road nos. 30–35 and a postbox blown onto its side in the foreground, photographed by H. McCrae on 4 June 1941. The image is credited to Dublin City Libraries and Archive / Dublin City Council.

The evidence available in this cycle still does not connect the overturned wartime postbox to the surviving George V McDowall Steven box catalogued as DUB-STR-0056 (NIAH 50120259). The exact historic position and continuity of the object have not been established. Copyright permission for republication is also not confirmed. Keep this as a historical research lead only; do not attach it as a photograph of the surviving object.
