## 2. NIAH rights determination — important distinction

Official terms:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/terms-and-conditions/

The indexed terms state:
- Website content is copyright of the Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage.
- Educational or private use is permitted on condition that NIAH is credited.
- Commercial use is permitted only with prior written agreement from NIAH.
- Social-media content must be credited to NIAH using its official handles.

The separate NIAH National Dataset is listed on data.gov.ie under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
https://data.gov.ie/dataset/national-inventory-of-architectural-heritage-niah-national-dataset

**Critical distinction:** CC BY 4.0 for the structured national dataset does not itself grant CC BY rights to the photographs hosted in NIAH galleries. The website terms explicitly retain copyright in website content and require prior written agreement for commercial use. Do not copy NIAH gallery photographs into the archive or eventual commercial colouring-book workflow without written permission covering the specific images and intended uses.
