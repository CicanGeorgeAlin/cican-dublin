## 7. Malahide Road — two separate NIAH postboxes must not be conflated

Current catalogue target DUB-STR-0052:
- NIAH 50030291
- c.1940 Jessop Davis, Enniscorthy, P&T pillar box.
- Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030291/malahide-road-clontarf-donnycarney-dublin-3-co-dublin

A separate NIAH entry also exists:
- NIAH 50130246, Malahide Road, Artane, Dublin 3.
- c.1905 Edward VII McDowall Steven & Co. pillar box.
- Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50130246/malahide-road-dublin-3-co-dublin

These are distinct postboxes, separated by a substantial distance and different historic design. The Artane Edward VII box must not be mistaken for the catalogue's Clontarf/Donnycarney Jessop Davis box. The current catalogue target remains the NIAH 50030291 object. The second record is a possible scope-expansion candidate, not an image match.

A planning observation also mentions an additional unrecorded postbox north of Collins Avenue that was proposed for relocation:
https://www.pleanala.ie/publicaccess/Observation/313182/ABP-313182-22%20-%20Observation%20-%20%20Dublin%20City%20Council.pdf
This is another separate object and not the catalogue target.
