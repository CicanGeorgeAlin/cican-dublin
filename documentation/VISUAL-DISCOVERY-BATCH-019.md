## 6. Historic identification reference

Ask About Ireland / Dublin City Public Libraries explains how royal cyphers, maker names and post-independence P&T symbols help identify and date Irish postboxes. Its Dublin 6 examples include VR wall boxes, Edward VII, George V and Free State-era examples. The page credits the Rathmines, Ranelagh and Rathgar Historical Society for contributed photographs. This is useful for identification, but its credited images are not automatically reusable.
