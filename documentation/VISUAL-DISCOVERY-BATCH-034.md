### 1. Famine Memorial — DUB-MEM-0001
- Photographer: David Dixon; taken 1 September 2022.
- Page: https://www.geograph.ie/photo/7292957
- Original JPEG: https://s0.geograph.org.uk/geophotos/07/29/29/7292957_325323d5.jpg
- The image shows the complete six-figure bronze group on Custom House Quay. The page identifies the Rowan Gillespie memorial. Its subject point (53.34802, -6.25017) is approximately 2 m from the archive navigation point.
- Attribution: David Dixon, “Custom House Quay, The Famine Memorial”, 1 September 2022, Geograph Ireland photo 7292957 (CC BY-SA 2.0).

### 2. George Salmon Statue — DUB-MON-0004
- Photographer: N Chadwick; taken 3 August 2018.
- Page: https://www.geograph.ie/photo/6040098
- Original JPEG: https://s0.geograph.org.uk/geophotos/06/04/00/6040098_091f9391.jpg
- The image shows the seated marble figure and plinth lettering “GEORGE SALMON / PROVOST / 1883–1904”. Subject point (53.34462, -6.25737) is within the page's stated 100 m precision.
- Attribution: N Chadwick, “Trinity College Dublin - George Salmon statue”, 3 August 2018, Geograph Ireland photo 6040098 (CC BY-SA 2.0).
