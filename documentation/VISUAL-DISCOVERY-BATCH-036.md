## 2. Old Detective Office, 1 Exchange Court — DUB-BLD-0004 / NIAH 50910280

A fresh inspection opportunity was found in the Royal Irish Constabulary Forum thread:
https://irishconstabulary.com/viewtopic.php?f=36&t=1419

Post #4 includes an attachment titled “Now2 mine.jpg” and introduces it as the view “as it is today” in a section about the Exchange Court Detective Office. The attachment was opened and visually inspected. It shows a historic brick façade with a granite memorial plaque above a doorway, consistent with the NIAH description of the target's brick façade and commemorative granite plaque above its entrance.

The forum page identifies the poster as Peter Mc. The attachment filename includes “mine”, but neither this nor the forum post establishes the photographer's full identity, the date, exclusive ownership, or permission for commercial/print reuse. The attached image is delivered through Tapatalk; no licence was displayed.

NIAH independently describes the Old Detective Office as a c.1830 four-storey building at the south end of Exchange Court, with a granite commemorative plaque above the entrance:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50910280/old-detective-office-1-exchange-court-dublin-2-dublin

**Decision: potential exact-façade photograph / rights-contact lead only. Do not add it to the archive.** Contact Peter Mc to confirm the photographed building, photographer, date, original-resolution file and written permission covering online and commercial print use. A closer visual match to the NIAH façade description is promising but does not replace rights clearance.

## 3. 53 Gardiner Street Upper plaque — DUB-INS-0006 / NIAH 50010751

The official NIAH record explicitly describes a Portland limestone date plaque on the south side elevation reading “Sherrard Street 1825”:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010751/53-gardiner-street-upper-sherrard-street-dublin-1-dublin

The official record page contains a multi-image survey gallery. Three linked survey-image URLs were tested through the browser retrieval path; all returned HTTP 403, so the image files could not be visually inspected or tested at original resolution in this pass. No reuse licence for these NIAH gallery images was established.

**Decision: official survey-gallery lead only. No image added.** Request access and written reuse terms from the Department/NIAH, or photograph the plaque directly as a founder original. The field photograph should include a close-up of the wording and a wider frame locating it on the south elevation.

## 4. Harrington Hall, 69 Harcourt Street — DUB-BLD-0012 / NIAH 50920250

The official Harrington Hall hotel gallery surfaced a façade image:
https://www.harringtonhall.com/gallery.html

The image was visually inspected. Its entrance door is numbered “70”, and the same official gallery page gives the property address as “70 Harcourt St, Saint Peter's, Dublin 2”. This is the neighbouring No. 70, not the archive's target Harrington Hall at No. 69.

NIAH 50920250 confirms that No. 69 has granite entrance steps, cast-iron boot scrapers and railings, and a pavement coal-hole cover:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50920250/harrington-hall-69-harcourt-street-dublin-2-dublin

**Decision: rejected as the wrong address/building.** Do not use it for No. 69 and do not add it to the archive.
