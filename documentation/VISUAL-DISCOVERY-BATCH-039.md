## 2. 84 Harcourt Street — DUB-BLD-0093 / NIAH 50920237

### New exact-address exterior photograph visually inspected

The official Albany House guesthouse website has a Gallery page:
https://albanyhousedublin.com/gallery/

Its “Guesthouse” gallery includes an exterior photograph titled by its image filename “outside-sign-2-albany-house-dublin”. The image was opened and visually inspected:
https://albanyhousedublin.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/outside-sign-2-albany-house-dublin-1352x900.jpg

The photograph shows the Albany House sign attached to a granite-faced façade, the Harcourt Street streetscape, railings and entrance area. It is consistent with the hotel's stated address at 84–86 Harcourt Street and the NIAH record for No. 84. It is useful as an exact-address exterior/context candidate, but the image is framed around the sign and street frontage; it does not provide a sufficiently clear close-up of the individual No. 84 bootscraper, coal-hole cover or anthemion finials to verify those details.

The hotel's official website states that Albany House is at 84–86 Harcourt Street:
https://albanyhousedublin.com/

The NIAH record for 84 Harcourt Street (Reg. 50920237) describes a c.1830 former townhouse now used as a hotel, with Scamozzian Ionic entrance, shared granite platform, cast-iron bootscraper, nosed granite steps, railings with anthemion finials and a cast-iron coal-hole cover in the street:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50920237/84-harcourt-street-dublin-2-dublin

The hotel gallery is controlled by the property/website operator, but no explicit licence or written permission for archive, print or commercial reuse was located. The photographer and capture date for this specific image were not confirmed.

**Decision: exact-address exterior/context candidate; not added.** Request written permission and photographer/credit details from Albany House/Byrne Hotel Group. Founder should photograph the exact bootscraper, coal-hole cover and railings independently.

## 3. 1 Pembroke Street Upper — DUB-STR-0079 / DUB-STR-0080 / NIAH 50930231

The official NIAH page was opened successfully. It confirms the c.1825 former townhouse, now Embassy of Greece, and describes the red-brick façade, Doric doorcase, ornate fanlight, granite entrance platform, cast-iron bootscraper, five granite steps, cast-iron railings and pavement coal-hole cover:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50930231/embassy-of-greece-1-pembroke-street-upper-dublin-2-dublin

The page exposes two survey image links. Both were clicked; both image requests returned HTTP 403. Consequently, the photographs were not visually inspected, and their individual details, photographer, and reuse rights could not be confirmed.

**Decision: official image-gallery lead only; no image added.** Contact NIAH/Department for image access and reuse terms; do not cite the blocked files as visually verified.

## 4. Gardiner Street Upper Victorian postbox — DUB-STR-0003 / NIAH 50010760

The official NIAH record confirms a freestanding cylindrical cast-iron pillar box erected c.1880, with flat-domed top, fluted collar, Queen Victoria VR insignia and maker's inscription “A. HANDYSIDE & CO. LTD. DERBY & LONDON”:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010760/gardiner-street-upper-dublin-1-dublin

A search of Wikimedia Commons' Dublin postbox category found many Dublin postbox images, but the files inspected/identified were at other addresses, including Arran Quay and Dame Street. These are different objects and were rejected as substitutes:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Post_boxes_in_Dublin

The Arran Quay Edward VII pillar box photograph is by Christine Matthews, dated 20 July 2011 and licensed CC BY-SA 2.0; its object coordinates are 53.345991, -6.277282. It is not the Gardiner Street Upper Victorian box and cannot be used for DUB-STR-0003:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Edward_VII_Pillar_Box,_Arran_Quay,_Dublin,_Ireland_-_geograph.org.uk_-_2566676.jpg

A historical National Folklore Collection record documents a July 1980 streetscape photograph by Gerard Brady at the junction of Gardiner Street and Talbot Street:
https://www.duchas.ie/en/cbeg/50864

That location is not demonstrated to be the NIAH postbox position (NIAH coordinates 315856, 235627). The photograph itself could not be inspected in this pass because the archive page redirected to a security verification page. It is a possible historical streetscape research lead only, not an exact-object photograph.

**Decision: no qualifying photograph for the target postbox; no image added.** Continue searching for an image at the exact NIAH coordinates or make a founder-original photograph.

## 5. Old Detective Office, 1 Exchange Court — DUB-BLD-0004 / NIAH 50910280

The official NIAH record confirms the c.1830 four-storey building, Flemish-bond brickwork, granite coping, commemorative granite plaque above the entrance and its historical connection with the Constabulary Office:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50910280/old-detective-office-1-exchange-court-dublin-2-dublin

The NIAH record is a reliable descriptive target, not a substitute for a photograph. Earlier Batch 036 identified a forum attachment introduced as a current image, but photographer identity and permission remained unresolved. No new rights-cleared photograph was established in this batch.

**Decision: no new image added.** Continue with the original photographer/rightsholder contact route and founder photography.
