## 2. Charleville Mall — target group

This batch concentrated on the unillustrated terrace targets:
- DUB-BLD-0016 — The Moorings, 13 Charleville Mall (NIAH 50010072)
- DUB-BLD-0017 — 11 Charleville Mall (NIAH 50010070)
- DUB-BLD-0018 — 12 Charleville Mall (NIAH 50010071)
- DUB-BLD-0019 — 14 Charleville Mall (NIAH 50010073)
- DUB-BLD-0020 — 15 Charleville Mall (NIAH 50010074)

The NIAH records describe the buildings as part of a c.1830 Georgian terrace facing the Royal Canal. They identify features relevant to the archive's photographic briefs: brick façades, granite platforms and steps, cast-iron bootscrapers, wrought-iron railings, urn-finial posts and cast-iron coal-hole covers. No photograph was accepted solely because it depicted the general terrace.

## 3. Historic North Strand bombing photographs — exactness check

The North Strand Bombing photographic collection includes images explicitly titled “3–5 Charleville Mall” and “3–5 Charleville Mall, rear”. These document different house numbers from the target set (Nos. 11–15). They cannot verify the present condition or details of the five target houses.

Collection:
https://northstrandbombing.ie/photographic-collection/

**Decision: rejected for DUB-BLD-0016 through DUB-BLD-0020 because the photographed addresses do not match.** They remain useful historical context for the wider terrace and the 1941 bombing, not object photographs for these records.

## 4. Charleville Mall Library image leads — out of current catalogue scope

The search identified a substantial image trail for Charleville Mall Library, NIAH 50010075. The library is adjacent to the terrace, but the current 195-object catalogue contains no separate Charleville Mall Library record. To preserve the fixed catalogue and avoid attaching an image to the wrong object, no library image was added to any house record.

### 4.1 Wikimedia Commons — 2020 exterior photograph

The Wiki Loves Monuments 2020 Ireland category lists:
“County Dublin - Charleville Mall Library - 20200907143550.jpg” (3024 × 4032).

Category:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Images_from_Wiki_Loves_Monuments_2020_in_Ireland

Wikidata identifies this file as the image associated with Charleville Mall Library (Q55049794):
https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q55049794

The file's exact image page, photographer/author and licence could not be independently retrieved in this pass. It is therefore an unverified rights/attribution lead, not an accepted asset. The fact that it appears in a Wiki Loves Monuments category does not by itself establish the licence details for the specific file.

- LBH010 — Charleville Mall Library, 1987
- LBH011 — Charleville Mall Library, 1990

Album:

Individual inspected pages:

The 1987 and 1990 captions describe the library's fire damage and refurbishment. All three individual pages display “All rights reserved”. They are historically valuable library photographs, but not images of Nos. 11–15 and not reusable under an open licence based on the inspected page.

### 4.3 Dublin City Libraries Flickr — 2007 exterior photograph

A separate Dublin City Libraries Flickr image titled “Charleville Mall Library” identifies the North Strand library and architect C.J. McCarthy. It was taken and uploaded on 22 February 2007. Its page links to the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 2.0 licence (CC BY-NC-SA 2.0):
https://www.flickr.com/photos/dublincitypubliclibraries/398685745

The licence permits sharing and adaptation subject to attribution, non-commercial use and ShareAlike conditions:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/2.0/

This is a useful library exterior lead, but the library is not one of the current 195 catalogue records and the licence is non-commercial. It must not be attached to a neighbouring townhouse or assumed suitable for commercial print use.

**Decision for all library images: not added.** Keep them as a separate catalogue-expansion/rights-contact lead only. No new library object is being created in this batch.

## Conclusion

Batch 041 confirmed a strong official descriptive record for the Charleville Mall terrace and uncovered several library photographs, including a 2020 Commons lead and historical Dublin City Libraries material. However, the historic bombing photographs show Nos. 3–5, not the target Nos. 11–15. Library images depict a separate unlisted building and some carry All rights reserved or non-commercial terms. No image references were added; the audit standard and catalogue boundaries were preserved.
