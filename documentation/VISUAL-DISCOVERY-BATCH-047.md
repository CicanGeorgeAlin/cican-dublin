## 2. Exact-object match — DUB-STR-0014

**Target:** DUB-STR-0014 — Edward VII cast-iron pillar post box, Burgh Quay.  
**NIAH registration:** 50020294.  
**Location:** Burgh Quay footpath at the junction with Hawkins Street, Dublin 2.  
**NIAH Irish Grid:** 316120, 234394.

The official NIAH record contains photograph `50020294_1.jpg`. The image was visually inspected and depicts the green cast-iron pillar box with the Edward VII royal cipher at the same Burgh Quay/Hawkins Street location. This is an exact-object match, not merely a street-context image.

The official NIAH record describes the object as a c.1905 cast-iron pillar box with a shallow domed cap, fluted rim, POST OFFICE frieze, ER VII royal cipher and crown, and Handyside, Derby & London maker's mark. Its record places it on the Burgh Quay footpath at the Hawkins Street junction.

**Archive decision:** register the image as a third-party OBJECT_PHOTOGRAPH for educational/private website display with clear NIAH attribution. Do not re-label it as founder photography.
