## 2. New NLI catalogue records registered

### A. The Five Lamps and Aldborough House — 1965

- NLI record: https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000047841
- Call number: WIL 16[11].
- Physical description: one 6 × 6 cm negative.
- Subjects include ironwork, lampposts, Portland Row and Amiens Street.
- Potential historic street-context reference for DUB-STR-0028, The Five Lamps.

The catalogue title identifies both the Five Lamps and Aldborough House in the frame. The original image has not been independently inspected in this pass, so it is registered as a catalogue source lead, not as a verified image asset.

### B. Ornate sea-horse lamp post, College Green — 1966

- NLI record: https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000049775
- Call number: WIL 74[1].
- Physical description: one 23 × 17 cm photographic print on card.
- Subjects include Trinity College, lampposts, ironwork and College Green.
- Potential relationship to the two former gas-lamp standards with carved seahorses noted east of the Henry Grattan Monument in DUB-MON-0002.

The exact physical lamp and its position must be checked against the image and the NIAH location before any exact-object assignment.

### C. Trinity College and Grattan statue, viewed from Dame Street — 1965

- NLI record: https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000047801
- Call number: WIL 15[12].
- Physical description: one 6 × 6 cm negative.
- NLI subject metadata names Henry Grattan and Trinity College.
- Potential historic context image for DUB-MON-0002.

The NLI record says the statue was erected in 1874. This date must be reconciled against the archive's other heritage references before it is adopted as the canonical date. The image itself has not been independently inspected in this pass.
