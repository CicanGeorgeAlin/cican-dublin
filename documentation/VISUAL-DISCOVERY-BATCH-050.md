## 2. Exact existing targets investigated

### DUB-STR-0003 — Victorian cast-iron pillar post box, Gardiner Street Upper

- Address: Gardiner Street Upper, Dublin 1.
- Official NIAH record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010760/gardiner-street-upper-dublin-1-dublin
- Registration: 50010760.
- NIAH description identifies a c.1880 cylindrical cast-iron Handyside pillar box with Queen Victoria VR insignia and maker's mark.
- Official page exposes a gallery of five images; direct image links include `50010760_5.jpg`, `50010760_2.jpg` and `50010760_1.jpg`.
- The research client received HTTP 403 for direct image retrieval, so the image files could not be inspected or copied. Exact object match is supported by the official record metadata, but the image frames themselves remain unverified.

### DUB-STR-0004 — Marlborough Street Edwardian post box

- Address: west side of Marlborough Street, north of the Pro-Cathedral, Dublin 1.
- Official NIAH record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010238/marlborough-street-dublin-1-co-dublin
- Registration: 50010238.
- NIAH identifies a c.1905 cast-iron pillar box with RE VII POST OFFICE insignia and McDowall Steven & Co London & Glasgow maker lettering.
- The official record contains a photo gallery. The page was blocked (HTTP 403) in this research pass, so no image was downloaded or visually inspected.

The NIAH catalogue descriptions are strong exact-object matches to the two existing CICAN records. The image-gallery files are leads awaiting retrieval and inspection, not assets already added to the live site.

## 3. Rights and usage

This does not make the images public domain or automatically grant commercial republication rights. The relevant terms are: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/terms-and-conditions/
