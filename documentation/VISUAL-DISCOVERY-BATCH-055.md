## Search results and rejection decisions

### Old Detective Office, Exchange Court

The official NIAH record describes a specific attached four-storey c.1830 building at the south end of Exchange Court, with a commemorative plaque and a distinctive classical doorcase. It identifies the building's connection to the Constabulary Office in Dublin Castle's Lower Yard.

Searches for an openly licensed Wikimedia Commons photograph of the exact building did not produce a verifiable file. General Dublin Castle imagery or historical photographs of the 1920 events are not acceptable substitutes for a photograph of this exact building. No image attached.

Official NIAH record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50910280/old-detective-office-1-exchange-court-dublin-2-dublin

### 23 Marlborough Street

The official NIAH record identifies a specific late-eighteenth-century, two-bay, four-storey terraced house, c.1780, with an altered ground-floor shopfront. The Commons Marlborough Street category contains several street-level photographs, but the screened file `20130807 dublin117.JPG` is described only as a general view of Marlborough Street and is categorised with Seán O'Casey's Pub. It does not establish that the specific NIAH building at No. 23 is depicted. Rejected as an exact-object asset.

Official NIAH record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010218/ladbrokes-marlborough-street-dublin-1-dublin  
Commons category: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Marlborough_Street,_Dublin  
Screened Commons file: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:20130807_dublin117.JPG

### 77 and 82 Marlborough Street; National Model Schools

The Commons category for Marlborough Street provides some relevant area photographs and subcategories, but the accessible file listings do not establish an exact match to No. 77, No. 82, or the specific main school building (NIAH 50010222). The existence of images of St Mary's Pro-Cathedral, the Wishing Hand, Telephone House or general street views does not prove depiction of these five targets. None was attached.

Commons category: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Marlborough_Street,_Dublin
