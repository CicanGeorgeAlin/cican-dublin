## Result

Successfully located and visually inspected official NIAH photographs for **DUB-STR-0011 — Edward VII cast-iron pillar post box, North Circular Road**, outside No. 530, Dublin 1 (NIAH Reg. 50060629).

The official NIAH page exposes a photographic slider. The following previews were opened and inspected:

- `50060629_1.jpg` — full front view of the green pillar box and black plinth.
- `50060629_3.jpg` — wider street-context view.
- `50060629_4.jpg` — close-up of the cap and letter aperture.
- `50060629_8.jpg` — close-up of the Edward VII royal cipher and crown.

The full front view and cipher detail are exact-object matches. They correspond with the NIAH description of a c.1905 Handyside, Derby & London cast-iron pillar box with intertwined E VII R royal insignia outside No. 530.
