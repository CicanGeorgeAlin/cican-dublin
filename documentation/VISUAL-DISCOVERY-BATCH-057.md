## Results

### 1. DUB-STR-0015 — Early Free State double post box, Dame Street
NIAH registration 50020195. The official record locates the c.1930 cast-iron double post box on the north footpath of Dame Street, south of the Central Bank. Its description records an oval plan, paired letter slots, raised POST OFFICE lettering, P&T insignia and curved side doors.

The NIAH gallery and previews were opened and visually inspected:
- `50020195_1.jpg`: front view; paired letter apertures, P&T insignia, green body and black plinth.
- `50020195_2.jpg`: side view.
- `50020195_4.jpg`: P&T insignia close-up.
- `50020195_5.jpg`: letter slot and collection panel detail.
- `50020195_6.jpg`: upper front detail.

Two exact-object image references were registered: the full front view and P&T insignia close-up.

### 2. DUB-STR-0027 — Art Deco double-headed concrete electric lamp, College Street
NIAH registration 50020523. The official record describes a c.1935 reinforced-concrete standard with an octagonal shaft, sweeping double-headed brackets and copper-roofed frosted-glass lanterns, at the east end of the north side of College Street.

The NIAH gallery and previews were opened and visually inspected:
- `50020523_1.jpg`: full standard with twin lanterns.
- `50020523_2.jpg`: twin arched brackets and lantern detail.
- `50020523_3.jpg`: single lantern detail.
- `50020523_4.jpg` and `50020523_5.jpg`: street-context views.

Two exact-object image references were registered: the full standard and the twin-lantern detail.
