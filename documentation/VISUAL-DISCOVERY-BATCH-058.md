## Result

**DUB-STR-0018 — Victorian Handyside pillar post box, Mount Street Crescent**  
NIAH Registration 50100593.

The official NIAH record places the post box at the east end of Mount Street Crescent, at the junction with Warrington Place. It describes a c.1900 cast-iron pillar box with cylindrical shaft, shallow domed cap, raised “Post Office” lettering, hinged door with the VR royal cipher, and an Andrew Handyside, Derby & London maker's mark.

The official NIAH gallery was opened and its preview images visually inspected:
- `50100593_1.jpg` — full view of the green pillar box and black plinth.
- `50100593_3.jpg` — letter aperture and collection plate detail.
- `50100593_4.jpg` — raised VR royal cipher on the door.
- `50100593_7.jpg` — street-context view showing the location.

Two exact-object reference images were registered: the full front view and the VR cipher detail.
