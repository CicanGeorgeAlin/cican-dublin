## Verified object research
Official Buildings of Ireland records were located for all five targets:
- NIAH 50010458 places the c.1930 oval-plan twin-aperture cast-iron box on the east side of Jervis Street near Mary Street. Its P&T panel and twin letter slots are distinctive identifiers. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010458/jervis-street-dublin-1-co-dublin
- NIAH 50010555 identifies the early Free State oval twin post box at the north end of O'Connell Street, at Cathal Brugha Street, directly outside the Gresham Hotel. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010555/oconnell-street-upper-dublin-1-co-dublin
- NIAH 50910076 identifies the c.1930 oval twin post box at the South Great George's Street / Fade Street junction. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50910076/south-great-georges-street-dublin-2-co-dublin
- NIAH 50020308 identifies the c.1930 Handyside cast-iron pillar box on Townsend Street, south of the Screen Cinema, with the maker's insignia on its plinth. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50020308/townsend-street-hawkins-street-dublin-2-dublin
- NIAH 50110429 identifies a Tonge & Taggart cast-iron pillar box at South Richmond Street / Lennox Street, installed c.1985. The record notes its foundry mark on the rear face and that it replaced an earlier box near the junction. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50110429/south-richmond-street-lennox-street-dublin-2-dublin
