## Official descriptions reconfirmed
- St Andrew Street: c.1940 oval double cast-iron post box with P&T insignia and twin letter slots, in front of the main post office. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50910194/st-andrew-street-dublin-2-co-dublin
- Westmoreland Street: c.1915 McDowall Steven cast-iron pillar box with George V GR cipher and maker's mark, on the west footpath. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50020222/westmoreland-street-dublin-2-dublin
- Ely Place: c.1880 anonymous Victorian pillar box, notably without royal insignia, at the Ely Place / Baggot Street Lower junction. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50930014/ely-place-dublin-2-dublin
- Fitzwilliam Square South: c.1890 A. Handyside & Co. cast-iron pillar box with VR cipher at the Pembroke Street Upper junction. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50930229/fitzwilliam-square-dublin-2-dublin

## Image findings and exclusions
- Westmoreland Street: an Irish Times image by Peter Thursfield depicts a green pillar box on Westmoreland Street. It is a promising location-specific lead, but the search result alone did not establish that it is the exact NIAH 50020222 box or provide a reuse licence. Not registered. https://www.irishtimes.com/life-and-style/homes-and-property/interiors/design-moment-green-post-box-c1922-1.3004436
- Fitzwilliam Square South: a scanned page in the 2000 book *Letter Boxes of Ireland* labels a historic photograph as the Fitzwilliam Square South / Pembroke Street Upper box, but the caption also says it was “now replaced by IPB 29/2”. It may therefore show a predecessor, not the current NIAH target. Rights for the scanned page/photo are not established. Not registered. https://www.irishphil.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Letter-Boxes-of-Ireland-Ed-1-2000-B-Warren.pdf
- National Library of Ireland search results for Westmoreland Street largely surfaced general street views and the 1911 royal procession. They do not establish a view of the target pillar box. NLI reproduction rights are controlled by NLI; no image was reused.
- Searches for the exact St Andrew Street and Ely Place objects did not yield a visually inspected, exact-object photograph with reuse rights established.
- A visually similar post box on Dawson Street and examples from other Dublin locations were rejected as wrong objects/locations.
