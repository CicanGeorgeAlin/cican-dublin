## Official record findings
- South Richmond Street: the current NIAH entry describes a freestanding cast-iron pillar post box installed c.1985, with a Tonge & Taggart Ltd Dublin foundry mark. It specifically says this appears to have replaced an earlier box near the junction. This distinction matters: historic images of the predecessor cannot be used as a photograph of the current registered object. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50110429/south-richmond-street-lennox-street-dublin-2-dublin
- 23 Hatch Street Lower: c.1810 former townhouse; the NIAH appraisal specifically identifies a cast-iron coal-hole cover in the street pavement cast by South City Foundry. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50110521/23-hatch-street-lower-dublin-2-dublin
- Harrington Hall, 69 Harcourt Street: c.1800 townhouse with cast-iron boot scrapers at the entrance and a cast-iron coal-hole cover in the pavement. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50920250/harrington-hall-69-harcourt-street-dublin-2-dublin
- 67 Harcourt Street: 1862 townhouse with Romanesque-Revival doorcase, cast-iron boot scraper and pavement coal-hole cover. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50920252/67-harcourt-street-dublin-2-dublin
- Ely Place: c.1880 anonymous Victorian cast-iron pillar box by McDowell Steven & Co., located at Ely Place / Baggot Street Lower. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50930014/ely-place-dublin-2-dublin

## Image findings and exclusions
- Harrington Hall: a NIAH image result shows the correct building frontage, but not a sufficiently clear close-up of the boot scraper or pavement coal-hole cover. It is not registered as a reference image for this detail-specific record. A hotel listing image also shows the frontage but has no confirmed reuse licence. Not used.
- 23 Hatch Street Lower: a 2019 Irish Times property article has an image captioned as the office accommodation at No. 23, but it does not establish the coal-hole cover detail and reuse rights are not confirmed. Not used.
- A Dublin coal-hole article image shows a Tonge & Taggart / South City Foundry cover, but the available image context does not establish that it is the cover at No. 23 Hatch Street Lower or either Harcourt Street address. It is not assigned to any of these records.
- A Geograph photograph of an Edward VII post box on Merrion Square South is a different object and was rejected.
- Search results for the current South Richmond Street post box and Ely Place box did not yield a visually inspected exact-object image with reuse rights established.
