## Official record findings
- 23 Hatch Street Lower: c.1810 former townhouse; the official appraisal specifically identifies a pavement coal-hole cover cast by South City Foundry. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50110521/23-hatch-street-lower-dublin-2-dublin
- Harrington Hall, 69 Harcourt Street: former townhouse, now hotel; official record describes cast-iron boot scrapers on the granite entrance platform and a cast-iron coal-hole cover in the pavement. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50920250/harrington-hall-69-harcourt-street-dublin-2-dublin
- 67 Harcourt Street: built 1862; notable Romanesque-Revival doorcase with chevron and rope mouldings, cast-iron boot scraper, and pavement coal-hole cover. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50920252/67-harcourt-street-dublin-2-dublin
- 37 Harcourt Street: c.1800 former townhouse, partially rebuilt in 1915; the NIAH record identifies a cast-iron coal-hole cover to the footpath. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50920231/37-harcourt-street-dublin-2-dublin
- The Moorings, 13 Charleville Mall: c.1830 terrace house facing the Royal Canal; the record identifies a cast-iron boot scraper at the entrance and a cast-iron coal-hole cover in the pavement. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010072/the-moorings-13-charleville-mall-dublin-1-dublin

## Image findings and exclusions
- NIAH pages provide authoritative descriptions for the exact features but the search evidence reviewed did not establish a separately reusable close-up photograph of the boot scrapers or coal-hole covers at the five targets.
- A 2019 Irish Times property article for 23 Hatch Street Lower provides a building/office context image, not a verified close-up of the South City Foundry pavement cover. Reuse rights were not established. Not registered.
- Harrington Hall hotel listing imagery shows building context, not a sufficiently clear detail image of the recorded ironwork; no reuse licence was established. Not registered.
- A general Dublin coal-hole cover photograph with a Tonge & Taggart / South City Foundry association could not be tied to any one of these five exact pavement locations. Not registered.
- The 13 Charleville Mall record confirms the specific boot scraper and coal-hole features in the official appraisal, but no exact detail photograph with confirmed reuse rights was identified in this search.
- No exact, visually inspected and rights-cleared detail image was accepted for 67 or 37 Harcourt Street.
