The five official NIAH records place these houses in the early-to-mid nineteenth-century terrace on Charleville Mall, facing the Royal Canal. The records identify a range of small cast-iron details including coal-hole covers, boot scrapers and entrance features. These are separate objects/features and should be photographed individually where the catalogue record calls for them.

- No exact-object detail photograph with verified reuse rights was identified for these five records in this batch.
- No image was registered; no source record was added.
