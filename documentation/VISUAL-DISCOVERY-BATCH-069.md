## Official record findings
- 2 Buckingham Street Lower: c.1795 terraced three-bay, four-storey house over exposed basement. NIAH describes a cast-iron boot scraper bridging the basement area, a pair of cast-iron coal-hole covers set in granite slabs, and identifies the footpath cover as made by Henshaw of Dublin. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50011162/2-buckingham-street-lower-dublin-dublin
- 1A Sherrard Street Upper: c.1890 Victorian house with granite entrance platform, wrought-iron railings and a cast-iron coal-hole cover set in a granite pavement slab. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010750/1a-sherrard-street-upper-dublin-1-co-dublin
- 17–18 Nassau Street: c.1750 former house, now retail/offices, with a cast-iron basement light and coal-hole cover in granite paving. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50100085/17-18-nassau-street-dublin-2-co-dublin
- 585 North Circular Road: c.1820 former house, now flats; NIAH records a cast-iron boot scraper at the entrance and a cast-iron coal-hole cover in the front pavement. https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50060408/585-north-circular-road-dublin-1-dublin

## Image findings and exclusions
- Wikimedia Commons' Dublin coal-hole category contains a small number of images. The Geograph photograph “Coal hole cover, Dublin” by Rossographer is accurately geolocated at 53.339072, -6.252637 and licensed CC BY-SA 2.0, but its coordinates do not match any of the four target addresses. It is not assigned to a target. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Coal_hole_cover,_Dublin_-_geograph.org.uk_-_7639726.jpg
- A public-domain Commons photograph depicts a coal-hole cover on Mountjoy Square, not one of these target addresses. It is not assigned. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mountjoy_Square,_Dublin,_Ireland_(ornate_coal_hole)_002.jpg
- A recently uploaded Tonge & Taggart cover photograph is explicitly located on Merrion Square South (53.338806, -6.250947), not at 2 Buckingham Street Lower or 585 North Circular Road. Despite its CC BY-SA 4.0 licence, it is not a valid location match and was not registered. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Coal-hole_cover_Tonge_%26_Taggart_Dublin.jpg
- Other Commons coal-hole examples from Merrion Square and Kilkenny are similarly not evidence of the ironwork at the catalogue target addresses.
- No exact-address, visually inspected image with matching depicted-object coordinates and verified reuse terms was found for the four target records.
