## Copyright and reuse decision
The Buildings of Ireland Terms and Conditions state that website content is copyright of the Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage. Educational or private use is permitted with NIAH credit; commercial use requires prior written agreement from NIAH. This is distinct from the NIAH national dataset licence (CC BY 4.0): the dataset licence does not, by itself, grant a licence to reuse individual website photographs.

Terms: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/terms-and-conditions/

Because the CICAN DUBLIN public archive and possible future publications require clearly documented image-reuse rights, the NIAH gallery photographs were not copied into the archive or represented as openly licensed. They remain cited research leads pending confirmation of permission for the intended online/publication use.

The Geograph CC BY-SA 2.0 image is openly reusable with attribution and share-alike compliance, but it does not depict the exact catalogue object closely enough to qualify as an object photograph. It was not attached to the record.
