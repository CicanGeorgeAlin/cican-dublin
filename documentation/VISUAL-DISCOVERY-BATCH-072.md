## Image and evidence leads

### DUB-STR-0013 — South Great George's Street
- Official NIAH record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50910076/south-great-georges-street-dublin-2-co-dublin
- An Environmental Impact Assessment report appendix surfaced an image within a table explicitly labelled NIAH 50910076 and describing the twin post box at the South Great George's Street / Fade Street junction. The extracted report content also includes a visual element showing the green twin box.
- Report source: https://www.pleanala.ie/publicaccess/EIAR-NIS/316272/Environmental%20Impact%20Assessment%20Report%20%28EIAR%29/Volume%204/Part%204%20of%204/Chapter%2016%20-%20Architectural%20Heritage/Appendix-A16.1-16.4.pdf
- This is a high-value exact-object lead because the report table ties the photograph to the NIAH registration and location. The report's photograph reuse licence/rights were not established. Do not copy or register it until permission is confirmed.

### DUB-STR-0021 — Westmoreland Street
- Official NIAH record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50020222/westmoreland-street-dublin-2-dublin
- An Irish Times image search surfaced a close-up titled “Green Post Box on Westmoreland Street”. The box visibly has a crown and Post & Telegraphs features consistent with the NIAH record's George V-era box.
- Article: https://www.irishtimes.com/life-and-style/homes-and-property/interiors/design-moment-green-post-box-c1922-1.3004436
- The article does not provide enough location detail to prove it is the exact west-side box at the NIAH coordinate. The photograph is also a copyrighted publication image; no reuse licence was identified. Retain as a lead only.

### DUB-STR-0025 — Fitzwilliam Square South
- Official NIAH record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50930229/fitzwilliam-square-dublin-2-dublin
- The NIAH record confirms a c.1890 A. Handyside & Co. pillar box with VR insignia at the junction with Pembroke Street Upper.
- A scanned extract from “Letter Boxes of Ireland” surfaced a historical photograph captioned “Fitzwilliam Square South / Pembroke St. Upr.”, but the caption notes the pictured box was later replaced. The photograph's reuse rights were not established. It cannot be treated as a current photograph of the catalogue object.

### Remaining targets
Searches for exact, openly reusable photographs of the Townsend Street, South Richmond Street, St Andrew Street and Ely Place post boxes did not identify a sufficiently matched image with a verified reuse licence in this batch. Their NIAH records remain reliable object-identification sources, not image licences.

## Rights and verification policy
A photograph appearing in a planning report, newspaper, scan or web search result is not automatically reusable. A location caption alone is not sufficient to attach a photograph to an address-level object. No image in this batch met both exact-object verification and documented reuse-rights requirements.
