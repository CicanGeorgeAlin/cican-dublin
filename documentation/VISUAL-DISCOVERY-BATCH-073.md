## Findings

### DUB-STR-0038 — Pearse Street Art Deco lamp standard
Official NIAH record:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50020527/pearse-street-dublin-2-dublin

NIAH confirms a c.1935 reinforced-concrete, double-headed electric lamp standard on the west end of the south side of Pearse Street. It describes octagonal tapered stepped bases and shafts, arched brackets, copper-roofed lanterns and frosted glass.

The official NIAH gallery contains associated photographs:
- https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/building-images/niah/images/survey_specific/800/50020527_6.jpg
- https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/building-images/niah/images/survey_specific/800/50020527_2.jpg

The reviewed gallery images are associated with the correct NIAH record and show street lighting / lamp-standard fabric, but the image set does not provide a fully clear, unobstructed full-object record of the listed double-headed standard. NIAH website image reuse terms were not established for this use. Do not copy or register these images without permission.

Geograph searches for Pearse Street surfaced nearby building, fire-engine and street-detail photographs, but none depicts the exact lamp standard at the NIAH location. Rejected as mismatched/context-only.

### DUB-INF-0002 — Huband Bridge
Official NIAH record:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50100594/huband-bridge-warrington-place-percy-place-dublin-2-dublin

A strong exact-object historical photograph was found:
- Wikimedia Commons file page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Huband_Bridge_(6337098348).jpg
- Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/6e/Huband_Bridge_%286337098348%29.jpg
- NLI Flickr Commons source: https://www.flickr.com/photos/nlireland/6337098348
- NLI catalogue record: https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000049362
- NLI reference: WIL 24[12]; photographer Elinor Wiltshire; photograph dated late autumn/early winter 1966.
- Commons file page records camera position 53.336395, -6.241832 and labels the file “No known copyright restrictions”.

The photograph is clearly identified as Huband Bridge and the Commons file page places the camera at the bridge. It is an exact-object historic reference lead. However, the National Library of Ireland catalogue records that reproduction rights are owned by NLI. The Commons “No known copyright restrictions” statement and the NLI catalogue rights note need to be reconciled with NLI before reuse in CICAN DUBLIN or print publication. It has therefore not been registered as an approved reusable image.

Additional NLI catalogue records describe other 1966–1969 Huband Bridge photographs, including records WIL 63[7] and WIL 63[8]. Their reproduction rights are also stated as owned by NLI:
- https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000049361
- https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000049362

A 2019 NIAH architectural heritage publication also includes a colour photograph of Huband Bridge, but its image reuse terms were not established:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/app/uploads/2019/10/Dublin-South-City.pdf

### DUB-BLD-0042 — Marino Institute of Education / former St Patrick's Monastery
Official NIAH record:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50130265/marino-institute-of-education-griffith-avenue-marino-dublin-3-dublin

NIAH confirms the former monastery was built in 1928–29 and describes the Neo-Georgian U-plan building, Doric portico, pediment, Christian Brothers badge, copper cross finial, chapel and stained glass.

Marino Institute of Education's own website provides campus photographs and an institutional history:
https://www.mie.ie/en/research/conferences_and_events/past_events/atee_annual_conference_2025_dublin/about_us/about_marino_institute_of_education/

The available website photographs are not independently proven to depict the exact former St Patrick's Monastery building as catalogued, and no open reuse licence for the relevant images was established. They remain visual leads only. Do not register a general campus image as the specific former monastery without confirming the building identity and image permission.

## Rights and matching decision
A photograph must meet both tests before registration:
1. It must depict the exact catalogue object, not merely the street, campus or surrounding district.
2. Its reuse rights must be sufficiently clear for the CICAN DUBLIN website and any intended publication.

The Huband Bridge image is a high-confidence exact-object lead, but the NLI rights record requires a permission clarification. NIAH gallery images are useful official identification leads, not automatically open-licensed photographs.
