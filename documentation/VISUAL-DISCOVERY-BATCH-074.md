### DUB-BLD-0049 — Warrenpoint gate lodge, 84 Clontarf Road
- NIAH: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030185/warrenpoint-84-clontarf-road-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin
- Registration: 50030185.

NIAH identifies this as a detached two-bay, single-storey gate lodge built circa 1840 and now a private house. Distinctive features include its entrance loggia, hipped slate roof, carved cusped fascia, paired round-headed casements, red-brick gate piers, segmental arch and masonry sphinx finials.

The NIAH record provides an official photographic gallery, but no separate open licence for reuse in this archive was established. Searches for an independently reusable, exact-object Commons/Geograph photograph did not produce a confirmed match. No image registered. The private-property restriction remains important: field photography must be from a lawful public viewpoint only.

### DUB-BLD-0050 — 86 Clontarf Road
- NIAH: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030182/86-clontarf-road-clontarf-dublin-3-co-dublin
- Registration: 50030182.

NIAH dates the house to 1847 and records its raised basement, Flemish-bond brick frontage, granite platband and stringcourse, round-headed entrance with petal-motif fanlight, console-bracketed door surround, cast-iron entrance balustrade and boundary railings. It forms part of the Warrenpoint seafront terrace grouping.

The official NIAH page is a useful identity and photographic lead, but permission/licence for reusing its gallery images was not established. No independently licensed exact-object image was confirmed in this pass. No image registered. Photograph only from a lawful public viewpoint; the building is a private house.

### DUB-BLD-0052 — Clontarf handball alley
- NIAH: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030337/strandville-road-clontarf-dublin-3-co-dublin
- Registration: 50030337.
- CoisCéim production record: https://www.coisceim.com/productions/in-the-magic-hour/

NIAH identifies a freestanding three-sided handball alley, built circa 1935, with sloping side walls, yellow-brick outer walls, yellow-brick pilasters and smooth-rendered internal walls. It is associated with the adjacent Garda station and is described as disused.

CoisCéim confirms that its 2021 production “In the Magic Hour” was performed at Clontarf Garda Station Handball Alley. This is useful confirmation of the venue identity and that the structure was used for an arts event, but the page's production photographs are not established as openly reusable reference photographs of the architecture. They were not copied or registered. Do not enter Garda property or restricted grounds for founder photography.

### DUB-BLD-0056 — Victoria Terrace, 163–173 Clontarf Road
- NIAH: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030144/163-173-clontarf-road-clontarf-dublin-3-co-dublin
- Registration: 50030144.
- Dictionary of Irish Architects: https://www.dia.ie/architects/view/3291

NIAH describes an eleven-house terrace built circa 1865 and attributes its development to George Tickell and design to J.J. Lyons. The official NIAH page includes a photographic gallery and confirms the terrace's form, brickwork, canted bays and entrance details. The Dictionary of Irish Architects independently records Tickell's 1863 instruction to J.J. Lyons to prepare plans for approximately twelve houses at Clontarf Sheds.

The NIAH photographs are not treated as openly reusable without a clear licence or permission. No separately licensed exact-object photograph was confirmed in this pass. No image registered.
