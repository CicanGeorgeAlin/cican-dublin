## Other records researched

### DUB-STR-0061 — Seafield Road East and West 1930s Art Deco concrete lamp standards

- NIAH 50030341: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030341/seafield-road-east-seafield-road-west-dublin-3-dublin
- Official register confirms a continuous group of freestanding c.1935 octagonal cast-concrete lamp standards on both sides of Seafield Road East and West, with exposed aggregate and sweeping Art Deco-influenced necks.
- NIAH's record includes a gallery, but the displayed institutional images were not established as openly reusable in this pass.
- Commons/Geograph searches did not establish a separately inspectable, exact-object photograph with verified reuse terms.
- No image added. The catalogue's warning that the NIAH point is a road-group reference, not the exact coordinate of each surviving lamp, remains important for founder fieldwork.

### DUB-BLD-0088 / DUB-STR-0077 — 47 Hardwicke Street and its wrought-iron bootscraper

- Commons file: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hardwicke_Street_with_St._George%27s_Church_at_end,_Dublin.jpg
- This historic street view is attributed to the National Library of Ireland on The Commons, circa 1912, and is marked “No known copyright restrictions”.
- Its camera position and view describe the Hardwicke Street / North Frederick Street junction and the street towards St George's Church. It does not prove that No. 47 or its specific entrance bootscraper is individually visible.
- The photograph was therefore rejected as an exact-object image. It remains a useful historical streetscape research lead, not an asset attached to either record.

A second Commons photograph, “Hardwicke Street, Dublin.jpg”, by MOs810 (21 April 2023, CC BY 4.0), depicts a wayside shrine and not the target townhouse. It was rejected as a mismatch.
