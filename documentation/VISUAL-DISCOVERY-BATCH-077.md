## Records reviewed

### DUB-BLD-0091 and DUB-STR-0085 — 1 Harcourt Terrace and its coal-hole cover

- NIAH 50110463: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50110463/1-harcourt-terrace-dublin-2-co-dublin
- NIAH identifies a corner-sited c.1830 Regency house with an Ionic entrance porch, elliptical-plan granite steps, cast-iron coal-hole cover in granite pavior, granite plinth walls and iron railings.
- Targeted searches found hotel/accommodation listings and architectural/planning material, but no inspectable, exact-object image with a clearly reusable licence.
- A Commons/Geograph result titled “Harcourt Terrace” was checked and rejected: it depicts St Ann's in Nottingham, England, not Dublin.
- No image registered. The host building and the coal-hole cover remain separate photography targets; the parent-building coordinate is not an exact coal-hole pin.

### DUB-BLD-0092 and DUB-STR-0086–0087 — 2 Pembroke Street Upper and its entrance/pavement details

- NIAH 50930232: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50930232/2-pembroke-street-upper-dublin-2-dublin
- NIAH describes the c.1825 four-storey Regency townhouse, integral carriage-arch to Fitzwilliam Court, cast-iron balconettes, ornate cobwebbed fanlight, granite platform and steps, bootscraper, railings and pavement coal-hole cover.
- Dublin City Council planning records for application 5449/22 confirm refurbishment works at the exact address, including façade repairs and work to railings/balconettes. The planning material is useful for understanding the property but did not provide a verified, reusable photograph of the object or the two micro-features.
- No exact, rights-cleared photograph of the building, bootscraper or coal-hole cover was found in this pass. No image registered.
- The building-level NIAH coordinate must not be treated as a precise location for the bootscraper or coal-hole cover.

### DUB-BLD-0093 and DUB-STR-0089–0091 — 84 Harcourt Street and its entrance/pavement details

- NIAH 50920237: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50920237/84-harcourt-street-dublin-2-dublin
- The official register describes a c.1830 townhouse with channelled granite ground floor, Scamozzian Ionic entrance columns, cast-iron bootscraper, anthemion-finial railings and a pavement coal-hole cover to the west.
- Targeted searches did not surface a clearly identified photograph of No. 84 or its individual ironwork/pavement features with inspectable reuse terms.
- Generic Harcourt Street or Harcourt Terrace imagery was not treated as an exact match. No image registered.
- The NIAH point is a building reference only; exact positions of the bootscraper, coal-hole cover and railings require field verification.
