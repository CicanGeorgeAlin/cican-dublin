## Findings

### DUB-BLD-0043 — Clontarf Stationmaster's House

Official NIAH record:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030283/clontarf-railway-station-96-howth-road-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin

- NIAH identifies the former stationmaster's house at 96 Howth Road as an attached three-bay, two-storey building dating c.1880, with red brick in English bond and yellow/black polychrome brick detailing.
- Opened and visually inspected individual NIAH gallery images:
  - `50030283_13.jpg` — street-context view clearly showing the former stationmaster's house in its setting.
  - `50030283_2.jpg` — close view of the gabled brick elevation and yellow-brick detailing.
  - `50030283_1.jpg` — frontal view of the entrance projection and polychrome brickwork.
  - `50030283_6.jpg` — roof and chimney detail.
- These are exact-object NIAH image leads; the individual images correspond to the building described in registration 50030283.
- NIAH's current Terms and Conditions state that website content is copyright of the Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage. Educational/private use is permitted with NIAH credit; commercial use requires prior written agreement.
- Because CICAN DUBLIN is intended for public archive and book publication, no commercial reuse permission was established in this batch. Images are therefore not copied into the project or registered as approved archive references.
- A separate Eiretrains history page was found, but it could not be opened reliably in this session and its photo reuse terms were not established. It remains a research lead only.

### DUB-BLD-0072 — 54 Vernon Avenue and DUB-INS-0008 date plaque

Official NIAH record:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030345/54-vernon-avenue-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin

- NIAH identifies a detached three-bay single-storey house dated 1913, with box-bay windows, buttresses, crenellated parapet and a date plaque over the entrance.
- Opened and visually inspected NIAH gallery image `50030345_1.jpg`. It shows the exact white rendered villa and its street-facing elevation. The image supports the building identity but the date plaque is too small in this view to verify its lettering or condition.
- The other gallery image candidate `50030345_3.jpg` repeatedly timed out and could not be visually inspected. It is not treated as verified evidence of the plaque.
- The same NIAH terms apply: educational/private use with credit; commercial reuse only with prior written agreement.
- No permission for commercial reproduction was established. No image was copied or registered.
- The separately catalogued plaque `DUB-INS-0008` remains uncovered: the gallery view does not provide a legible close-up of the inscription.

## Rights and evidence decisions

- NIAH's terms page was opened directly and reviewed. It does not provide a blanket open licence for gallery photographs.
- The exact building identity of the two NIAH gallery subjects was visually verified, but permission for commercial publication was not.
- An exact-object visual match does not, by itself, authorise copying or publication.
- No image was imported from Eiretrains because the page was not reliably accessible and its rights basis could not be established.
- No generic street image, nearby railway station photograph or unreadable plaque detail was registered.
