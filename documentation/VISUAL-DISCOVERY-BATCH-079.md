## Target reviewed

### DUB-BLD-0082 — 238 Clonliffe Road, Dublin 3

Official NIAH record:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50120194/238-clonliffe-road-dublin-3-dublin

The official NIAH record describes the c.1860 corner-sited house and records a granite street-name plaque, wrought-iron railings, cast-iron bootscrape, cast-iron gate and sash windows. The individual micro-details remain separately catalogued as DUB-INS-0007, DUB-STR-0058 and DUB-STR-0059.

A newly located Swords to City Centre Core Bus Corridor Environmental Impact Assessment appendix (Architectural Heritage Appendices A16.1–A16.3, PDF page 17) contains a photographic panel for the protected-structure group at Clonliffe Terrace, 234, 236 and 238 Clonliffe Road. The page identifies the group as DCC Record of Protected Structures 1904–1906 and NIAH 50120194–50120196. The embedded image is a wider street/context photograph, not a close-up of No. 238 or its plaque, bootscrape, gate or railings.

Source:
https://swordsscheme.ie/wp-content/uploads/sites/5/2023/05/A16.1-A16.3.pdf

### Image matching decision

- The PDF photograph is a useful new visual research lead for the Clonliffe Terrace group.
- It does not isolate or unambiguously identify No. 238 within the group, nor show any of the separately catalogued micro-details at sufficient scale.
- The PDF does not itself establish an open licence or permission for commercial reuse of the embedded photograph.
- Accordingly, it is not registered as a reference image and is not copied into the archive.
- The NIAH description confirms the architectural features but does not grant blanket permission to reuse its gallery imagery.
