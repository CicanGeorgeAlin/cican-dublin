## DUB-BLD-0035 — 619 North Circular Road, Dublin 1

Official NIAH record 50011089:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50011089/619-north-circular-road-dublin-co-dublin

The current NIAH entry identifies a c.1830 terraced two-bay, three-storey house over exposed basement. It records a Doric doorcase, original spoked timber fanlight, cast-iron bootscraper, original wrought-iron railings and cast-iron corner posts on a granite plinth wall.

NIAH gallery images were opened and visually inspected:
- `50011089_5.jpg`: wider street-context view of the terrace and target building.
- `50011089_3.jpg`: close view of a sash window and iron railings; it is a feature detail, not a full façade or bootscraper photograph.

These images confirm useful official visual leads, but NIAH content is copyrighted by the Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage. Its terms allow educational/private use with credit and require prior written NIAH agreement for commercial use. No such permission is recorded for CICAN DUBLIN publication. Neither image is imported.

## DUB-BLD-0036 — 50 Middle Abbey Street, Dublin 1

Official NIAH record 50010393:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010393/dublin-1-apartments-50-middle-abbey-street-dublin-1-co-dublin

The NIAH record describes an early Georgian c.1750 terraced house, with a pedimented doorcase, early spoked timber fanlight, granite entrance platform and two granite steps. It specifically records a lamp standard on either side of the entrance, each bearing the maker's name “Harte” at its base.

NIAH gallery images opened and visually inspected:
- `50010393_13.jpg`: broad street-context view.
- `50010393_1.jpg`: frontal view of the building and entrance.
- `50010393_7.jpg`: entrance, doorcase and both flanking lamp standards.
- `50010393_8.jpg`: close view of the doorcase and fanlight.

The image set provides strong exact-building and entrance-detail identification leads. The maker's mark itself is not legible in the opened views. No NIAH images are imported because commercial reuse permission has not been established.

## DUB-BLD-0037 — 18 Pembroke Street Upper, Dublin 2

Official NIAH record 50930293:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50930293/18-pembroke-street-upper-dublin-2-dublin

The NIAH record describes a c.1825 two-bay, four-storey townhouse over basement. It records a granite entrance platform and four steps, iron railings with decorative cast-iron corner posts, a lamp standard mounted over the granite plinth, and a coal-hole cover in the pavement.

A second photographic source was located in the Bray to City Centre Core Bus Corridor Scheme Environmental Impact Assessment Report, Appendix A16.2, page 283:
https://brayscheme.ie/wp-content/uploads/sites/11/2023/08/Appendix-A16.1-16.4-Architectural-Heritage.pdf

The PDF's photographic survey separately identifies two coal-hole records at 18 Pembroke Street Upper (CBC0013BTH189 and CBC0013BTH188), both dated c.1825. The page contains two close photographs of circular pavement covers in granite paving. The photographs are relevant exact-address evidence for the street-level coal-hole feature, but their embedded image rights and permission for CICAN DUBLIN reuse have not been established. They are not imported. The archive currently has one combined building record and does not split these two survey observations into separately mapped objects; do not infer that the two images document one and the same cover.

The same report identifies 18–19 Pembroke Street Upper as a pair of protected structures built c.1825. The report is a useful visual/heritage lead, not an open image licence.
