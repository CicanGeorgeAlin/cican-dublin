## Official NIAH evidence

NIAH registration 50010071:
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010071/12-charleville-mall-dublin-1-dublin

The official record dates the terraced two-bay, two-storey house over raised basement to c.1830. It records the cast-iron bootscraper on the granite-flagged entrance platform, the granite stepped approach, wrought-iron railings and cast-iron corner posts with urn finials. It separately records a cast-iron coal-hole cover in a granite setting in the concrete footpath.

Individual NIAH gallery files were opened and visually inspected (five distinct images):
- `50010071_6.jpg`: wide street-context view of the Charleville Mall terrace.
- `50010071_1.jpg`: frontal view of the target house, entrance and street-facing railings.
- `50010071_4.jpg`: closer entrance view showing the doorcase, granite steps and platform area.
- `50010071_5.jpg`: close view of a circular cast-iron coal-hole cover set in the pavement.
- `50010071_3.jpg`: close view of a sash window and its iron guard.

The coal-hole image is a strong visual lead for the cover type and pavement setting recorded at this address. The entrance image provides context for the bootscraper, but the scraper itself is not shown at sufficient detail to verify its exact design or condition. The NIAH written description independently confirms that the bootscraper exists.
