## Official NIAH evidence

https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030124/hillview-terrace-204-clontarf-road-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin

NIAH dates the terraced two-bay, two-storey house over basement to 1846. Its description records an M-profile roof, rendered parapet and cornice, smooth-rendered walls with stringcourses, moulded window surrounds, round-headed entrance with moulded surround and pilasters, panelled timber door, granite-capped plinth walls, cast-iron railings and pedestrian gate. The record also notes remnants of an early coach house to the rear lane. The appraisal attributes construction to Joseph Mannin on land leased from J.E.V. Vernon and identifies No. 204 as one of a terrace of five.
