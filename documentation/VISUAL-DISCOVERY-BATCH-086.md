## Target reviewed

### DUB-BLD-0043 — Clontarf Stationmaster’s House, 96 Howth Road, Clontarf, Dublin 3

- **NIAH registration:** 50030283.
- **Official record:** https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030283/clontarf-railway-station-96-howth-road-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin
- **NIAH identifies:** attached three-bay, two-storey former stationmaster’s house, built circa 1880; now a private house. The description records red English-bond brick, yellow-brick quoins, black/yellow brick stringcourses, yellow-brick plinth, slate roof, brick chimneystacks, sash windows, granite sills, and a half-glazed timber entrance door. It adjoins the former station building beside the railway.
- **Historic context:** the NIAH appraisal states that the station site belonged to the Dublin & Drogheda Railway, opened in 1844, and that the station closed in 1956.

## Rights and reuse decision

NIAH’s published terms state that website content is copyrighted by the Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage; educational/private use is permitted with NIAH credited, while commercial use requires prior written agreement.

Terms: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/terms-and-conditions/

Because CICAN DUBLIN is intended for public-facing publication and may be used commercially, the gallery images were **not** copied into the visual inventory. No open Creative Commons licence was established for these NIAH images. Written permission should be obtained before any commercial reuse.

## Similar-looking railway image rejected

A Wikimedia Commons photograph titled “CLONTARF ROAD TRAIN STATION (PHOTOGRAPHED SEPTEMBER 2016)” by William Murphy was inspected:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:CLONTARF_ROAD_TRAIN_STATION_%28PHOTOGRAPHED_SEPTEMBER_2016%29_-_panoramio_-_William_Murphy.jpg

Although the file is CC BY-SA 3.0, its own description identifies the modern Clontarf Road railway station, opened in 1997, and discusses the old Clontarf Station remnants. It is not a photograph of the 96 Howth Road stationmaster’s house. It was rejected and must not be attached to DUB-BLD-0043.

The separate NIAH record for the former station building at 94 Howth Road (registration 50030277) is also a different catalogue object; images of that building must not be substituted for the stationmaster’s house at No. 96.
