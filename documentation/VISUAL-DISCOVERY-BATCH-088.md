## Target reviewed

### DUB-BLD-0074 — Woodside Villa, 70–72 Vernon Avenue, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Official NIAH record (registration 50030099):  
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030099/70-72-vernon-avenue-clontarf-dublin-3-co-dublin

The NIAH record identifies a pair of semi-detached houses built circa 1870. It describes the shared roof and parapet, canted bay windows, differing rendered finishes, doorcases, granite entrance settings, and the wrought-iron gate and railings. It records No. 70 as the residence of William Barton Esq. in 1908. These details provide a reliable visual checklist for future founder photography.
