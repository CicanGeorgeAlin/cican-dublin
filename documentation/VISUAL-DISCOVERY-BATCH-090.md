# Visual Discovery Batch 090 — Clontarf Baths

**Research date:** 29 October 2026  
**Scope:** Existing uncovered catalogue record DUB-BLD-0060  
**Outcome:** Exact NIAH photograph visually inspected; Wikimedia Commons candidate reclassified as a misleading title / non-exact visual; no image imported

## Target

### DUB-BLD-0060 — Clontarf Baths, Clontarf Promenade, Clontarf Road, Dublin 3

Official NIAH registration 50030148:  
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030148/clontarf-baths-clontarf-road-clontarf-dublin-3-dublin

The NIAH record describes a rectangular swimming pool built in 1881, chamfered south corners, single-storey structures along the north shore, concrete seafront retaining walls, and metal railings and gates enclosing the north boundary. It places the baths east of the bandstand. The record states that the baths have been disused since 1996, although historic fabric remains and the pool's overall form is still evident.

## Image 1 — Official NIAH gallery photograph

Direct image:  
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/building-images/niah/images/survey_specific/800/50030148_1.jpg

The actual image was opened and visually inspected. It depicts the pool basin / former pool footprint, surviving perimeter wall and seafront context. It is a genuine exact-object reference image and supports the identification of the surviving pool outline and wall.

**Decision:** Do not import. The NIAH image's reuse terms and commercial / print permissions were not established in this research pass. Official publication on the NIAH website is not treated as an open commercial licence. The image remains an identification lead only.

The file page and actual 960-pixel preview were opened and inspected. The photograph shows a modern entrance frontage and perimeter railings, not a legible view of the historic swimming-pool basin, its chamfered plan or the surviving 1881 pool walls. The file's wording refers to “The Baths bar”; the title alone is not proof that it depicts the historic pool object in the catalogue.

**Decision:** Reject for DUB-BLD-0060. The licence is explicit, but the image does not adequately establish the listed historic swimming-pool fabric. A permissive licence cannot cure a subject-identification mismatch. Do not attach it to the record.

## Visual matching standard reaffirmed

The historic pool and a business or modern facility using the “Baths” name must not be conflated. For this archive, the image must show identifiable surviving fabric of the 1881 pool or its historic perimeter, not merely a nearby or similarly named venue.

## Founder field photography checklist

The catalogue's planned shots remain appropriate:
1. Wide view showing the surviving pool outline and seafront relationship.
2. Chamfered south corners and pool geometry.
3. Concrete retaining walls and surviving outer wall.
4. North-shore single-storey structures from lawful public viewpoints.
5. Metal railings, gates and boundary details.
6. Present condition, vegetation, repairs and safety barriers.

The NIAH describes the site as disused since 1996; this does not establish its present access arrangements or current condition. Photograph only from lawful public viewpoints. Do not cross barriers or enter a restricted or unsafe site.
