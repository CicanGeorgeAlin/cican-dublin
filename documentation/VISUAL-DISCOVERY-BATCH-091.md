# Visual Discovery Batch 091 — 23 Marlborough Street

**Research date:** 29 October 2026  
**Scope:** Existing uncovered catalogue record DUB-BLD-0006  
**Outcome:** Three official NIAH gallery photographs opened and visually inspected; exact building identity confirmed; no image imported because reuse rights were not established.

## Target

### DUB-BLD-0006 — 23 Marlborough Street, Dublin 1

Official NIAH registration 50010218:  
https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010218/ladbrokes-marlborough-street-dublin-1-dublin

The official NIAH record identifies a terraced two-bay, four-storey house built circa 1780, with a shopfront inserted at ground floor. It describes red brick laid in Flemish bond, gauged brick flat-arched window openings, granite sills, replacement timber sash windows, exposed sash boxes and a rebuilt parapet. The NIAH appraisal notes that the upper-floor façade composition survives despite the altered shopfront.

## Identity and rights decision

**Exact-object identity:** Confirmed to a high degree. The three images are delivered by the official NIAH gallery associated with registration 50010218, and the visible façade/window fabric agrees with the record's description.

**Reuse rights:** Not established. The images are published on the official NIAH website, but public accessibility does not itself grant permission for republication, commercial website use or print publication. The linked NIAH terms/accessibility destination could not be retrieved successfully in this research pass (HTTP 403). No open licence or written permission was verified.

**Decision:** Do not copy, hotlink as an archive image, register or republish these photographs as approved visual assets until the applicable terms or written permission are confirmed. They remain official research and identification references only.

## Other candidate search

Searches for an independently licensed Commons or Geograph photograph specifically identifying 23 Marlborough Street did not produce a candidate that could be verified as both the exact property and suitable for reuse. General street views and images of nearby numbers are not substitutes.

## Field photography checklist

The existing archive plan remains appropriate:
1. Square-on full elevation from a lawful public viewpoint, with number/property boundaries identifiable.
2. Upper-floor red-brick façade, Flemish bond and gauged brick arches.
3. First- and second-floor sash windows, exposed sash boxes and granite sills.
4. Top-floor window proportions and brick reveals.
5. Ground-floor shopfront and Victorian cornice, recorded as present-day altered fabric.
6. Roof/parapet and north party-wall chimney features where visible from a lawful viewpoint.

Do not enter private premises or obstruct the public footpath. Avoid photographing identifiable passers-by where practicable.
