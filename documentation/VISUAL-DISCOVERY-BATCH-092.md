## Target

### DUB-BLD-0007 — 77 Marlborough Street, Dublin 1

- NIAH registration: 50010235
- Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50010235/donal-stanley-clerical-tailoring-77-marlborough-street-dublin-1-dublin
- Coordinates in NIAH: Irish Grid 315982, 234867
- Catalogue address: 77 Marlborough Street, Dublin 1

The NIAH record identifies an end-of-terrace, two-bay, three-storey house built circa 1750, later used as retail premises. It describes red-brick Flemish-bond walling, a slate roof behind a parapet, square-headed windows and an unusual half-octagonal chimney-like feature on the north/front elevation. The feature stands on a moulded limestone pedestal with a rusticated Doric pilaster and fluted console bracket. The appraisal notes it may be a remnant of an adjoining building demolished in recent years.

**Rights decision:** Do not import these files into the public visual-assets registry. The NIAH terms page states that website content is copyrighted by the Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage; educational/private use is permitted with credit, while commercial use requires prior written agreement from NIAH. No such written permission has been obtained for CICAN DUBLIN's intended publication and possible commercial/print use.

## Founder field-photography checklist

If photographed from a lawful public viewpoint:
1. Square-on full frontage including the parapet and shopfront / entrance zone.
2. Oblique view showing the building's end-of-terrace position.
3. Close-up of the half-octagonal chimney-like feature, limestone pedestal, Doric pilaster and fluted console.
4. Upper brickwork, gauged voussoirs, granite sills and window details.
5. Any surviving entrance surround and fanlight, without assuming concealed or altered elements remain intact.
6. Record present condition and any conservation works separately from the historic NIAH description.

The NIAH states that most surveyed structures are privately owned and not open to the public. Photograph from public space only; do not enter private premises or building works without permission.
