# Visual Discovery Batch 093 — Vernon Lodge Pedestrian Entrance

**Research date:** 29 October 2026  
**Scope:** Existing uncovered catalogue record DUB-BLD-0040 — Vernon Lodge, Clontarf  
**Outcome:** Four official NIAH photographs opened and visually inspected; exact-object identity confirmed; no image imported because commercial republication permission is not established.

## Target

### DUB-BLD-0040 — Vernon Lodge, 41A Castle Avenue, Clontarf, Dublin 3

- NIAH registration: 50030164
- Official record: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/buildings-search/building/50030164/vernon-lodge-41a-castle-avenue-clontarf-dublin-3-co-dublin
- NIAH Irish Grid: 319351, 236272
- Description: Freestanding carved ashlar limestone pedestrian entrance to Clontarf Castle, dated 1885; now disused.

The NIAH describes a Tudor-arch door opening with decorative timber panelled door, carved limestone surround with roll-mouldings, gable and crenellations, carved date stone, motto, coat of arms and crest, and octagonal piers with monolithic shafts and richly carved capitals. It is set into a snecked limestone boundary wall at the junction of Castle Avenue and Kincora Avenue. The arms, crest and motto “Vernon Semper Viret” are associated with the Vernon family.

## Wikimedia Commons cross-check — similarly named but different structure

Two Commons photographs were also opened and inspected:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dublin_-_Clontarf_Castle_gate_lodge_-_20220911164349.jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dublin_-_Clontarf_Castle_gate_lodge_-_20220911164331.jpg

Both are credited to Tdr112 and licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. The Commons metadata gives camera location 53.363434, -6.205410. The Vernon Lodge NIAH location is 53.363408, -6.207910 (derived from its published Irish Grid coordinate), approximately 165 metres west. The Commons images are catalogued as “Clontarf Castle gate lodge” and depict a different gate-lodge structure. They are not the Vernon Lodge pedestrian entrance at 41A Castle Avenue.

**Decision:** Reject both Commons images for DUB-BLD-0040. Their open licence does not overcome the location and subject mismatch. They must not be attached to Vernon Lodge.

## Founder field-photography checklist

From lawful public viewpoints only:
1. Complete front elevation, square-on, including the whole boundary-wall opening.
2. Three-quarter view showing the entrance projecting from / set into the boundary wall.
3. Close-up of the Tudor arch, roll-mouldings, carved date stone and crenellations.
4. Heraldic panel: coat of arms, crest and motto lettering, captured straight-on and in even light.
5. Octagonal piers, monolithic shafts and carved capitals.
6. Timber panelled door, “Vernon Lodge” sign and any surviving warning plaque.
7. Record current condition, weathering, repairs and any missing or damaged carving without assuming historical condition.

The NIAH notes that most surveyed structures are privately owned and not open to the public. Stay on the public footpath and do not enter private grounds.
