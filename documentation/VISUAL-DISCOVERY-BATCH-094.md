## Evidence and visual inspection

The current NIAH record identifies a freestanding cast-iron pillar box installed circa 1905, located on the north side of Lower Abbey Street. Its description records a shallow domed cap, moulded corona, cylindrical shaft and plinth, raised “POST OFFICE” lettering, the Edward VII royal insignia and a Handyside maker's mark. Published Irish Grid coordinates are 316132, 234580.

The live NIAH gallery was accessible in this investigation. Four image files were opened and visually inspected:

- https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/building-images/niah/images/survey_specific/800/50010291_9.jpg — wide street-context view; the box is visible in situ.
- https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/building-images/niah/images/survey_specific/800/50010291_4.jpg — close view of the cap, moulding, lettering and letter aperture.
- https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/building-images/niah/images/survey_specific/800/50010291_1.jpg — near-full-height view of the exact green pillar box, including the Edward VII cipher.
- https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/building-images/niah/images/survey_specific/800/50010291_5.jpg — close-up of the royal cipher and crown.

These images visually correspond to the exact catalogued object and are useful research references. They are **not cleared for CICAN's commercial/print publication**: the NIAH Terms and Conditions state that website content is copyright of the Department and commercial use requires prior written agreement. No permission was obtained in this batch. Terms: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/terms-and-conditions/

A Geograph image titled “Postbox, Dublin” (photo 5253628) was also screened. Its page explicitly locates the Edward VII pillar box on Dame Street, not Lower Abbey Street, so it is a different object and was rejected. https://www.geograph.org.uk/photo/5253628

Other generic Dublin Edward VII post-box results were not accepted as exact matches without object-level location evidence.
