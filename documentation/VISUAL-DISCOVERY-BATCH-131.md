**Date:** 11 November 2026

## Summary

Two separately identifiable landscape features in St Anne’s Park were added after comparison against the existing catalogue:

1. **DUB-STR-0096 — St Anne’s Park shell house with “The House that Jack built” pier**
   - NIAH registration: 50030040
   - NIAH Irish Grid reference: 321484, 237275
   - NIAH describes shell and pebble decoration, bone and skull material in niches, a central decorated pier, lettering and a dog figure.
2. **DUB-STR-0097 — St Anne’s Park Naniken River grotto / hermitage**
   - NIAH registration: 50030035
   - NIAH Irish Grid reference: 321841, 237270
   - NIAH describes a pointed-arch opening, calp-limestone rubble frontage, yellow-brick groin vault and riverside setting.

Dublin City Council’s dedicated Follies guide independently identifies the Shell House / Dogs Graveyard and Rustic Hermits Cave – Cave among the twelve garden follies at St Anne’s Park.
