**Date:** 28 November 2026  
**Project:** CICAN Dublin Archive

**Scope:** Next 100 eligible records from the refreshed four-authority Dublin missing-registration queue  
**Status:** Published to the master catalogue and public NIAH register; national CSV re-audit pending.

## Method

The 100 records were selected from the workflow-generated official NIAH four-authority missing-registration queue after excluding existing archive registrations and rows without a valid registration, official NIAH record URL, name or survey description. Every selected row has a unique REG_NO, COUNTY=DUBLIN, recognised Dublin local authority, official WEBSITE_LINK and survey metadata. Official address, use, date, rating, special-interest, appraisal and coordinate fields were carried through without inventing missing values.

All selected rows belong to South Dublin County Council. This is bulk official-dataset source verification. Individual pages were not separately opened one by one in this cycle; official URLs are retained in each record. Records are not field verified. NIAH CSV coordinates are SOURCE_DERIVED with unspecified positional accuracy, not founder GPS. No NIAH-linked images are reused.
