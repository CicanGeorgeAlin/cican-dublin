# Visual Discovery Batch 132 — St Anne’s Park towers and bridge

**Date:** 11 November 2026  
**Project:** CICAN DUBLIN  
**Founder:** CICAN GEORGE ALIN

## New records

- **DUB-BLD-0130 — Roman Tower** (NIAH 50030021). Built 1837, originally on the roof of St Anne’s House and moved to its present hilltop position in 1873. The NIAH records its use as a viewing tower over Dublin Bay.
- **DUB-BLD-0131 — Annie Lee Tower and Bridge** (NIAH 50030023). Dated 1838; the NIAH links it to the birth of Annie Lee. A plaque was inserted in 1926.
- **DUB-BLD-0132 — Clock Tower and walled-garden entrance** (NIAH 50030037). Built 1854; a four-stage bell tower with an integral entrance arch, clock, carved stonework and wrought-iron gate.
