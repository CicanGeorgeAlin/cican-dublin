# Visual Discovery Batch 133 — St Anne’s Park temple, arch and fountain

**Date:** 11 November 2026  
**Project:** CICAN DUBLIN  
**Founder:** CICAN GEORGE ALIN

## New records

- **DUB-BLD-0133 — Herculanean Temple** (NIAH 50030024): c.1850 classical temple folly with a six-column Ionic portico, carved limestone architrave and frieze, granite plinth wall and wrought-iron railings.
- **DUB-BLD-0134 — Rustic archway and flanking viewing arches** (NIAH 50030030): c.1860 U-plan folly with a central segmental arch, flanking arches and granite steps. The side arches were intended to frame views towards Howth and the Dublin Mountains.
- **DUB-BLD-0135 — Yew Circle and former fountain** (NIAH 50030031): c.1860 circular fountain pit surrounded by mature yew trees. The NIAH records the former marble basin, central statue and early pump mechanism; survival of those components is not assumed.

## Verification limits

NIAH Irish Grid references are retained, but no WGS84 coordinates or founder GPS are assigned. Present condition, access, visibility and survival of original fountain components require field verification. Dublin City Council reports decorative tiles discovered during temple conservation, but current visibility has not been established.

No online photograph was attached without a confirmed reuse licence. Each record has a founder photography plan and awaits an original photograph before line-art production.
