# Visual Discovery Batch 134 — St Anne’s Park estate structures

**Date:** 11 November 2026  
**Project:** CICAN DUBLIN  
**Founder:** CICAN GEORGE ALIN

## New catalogue records

1. **DUB-BLD-0136 — Earlier walled-garden remains** (NIAH 50030026). c.1820. Pre-dates the Guinness acquisition of Thornhill Estate in 1835; includes rubble-stone walls, yellow-brick interior fabric, buttresses and carved stone door details.
2. **DUB-INF-0136 — Rockwork Bridge** (NIAH 50030028). c.1860. A single-arch pedestrian bridge made from roughly hewn limestone slabs arranged to resemble a natural rock formation.
3. **DUB-STR-0136 — Relocated iron gate screen and granite piers** (NIAH 50030039). Erected c.1850 and moved c.1980. Wrought- and cast-iron gate, urn-topped openwork piers, decorative railings and carved granite plinths.
4. **DUB-BLD-0137 — Double-arch bridge incorporating a hermitage** (NIAH 50030034). c.1860. Carries the road over the Naniken River and a footpath; includes pointed arches and a small hermitage.

## Verification limits

NIAH Irish Grid references are retained, but no WGS84 coordinates or founder GPS are assigned. Present condition, exact field positions, and access to individual structures require on-site verification. The gate screen is explicitly recorded as relocated around 1980; it is not represented as remaining in its original location. The earlier walled garden is recorded as surviving remains, not a complete intact enclosure.

No online photograph was added without confirming a suitable reuse licence. Each record has a photography brief and awaits founder-captured original photography before line-art production.
