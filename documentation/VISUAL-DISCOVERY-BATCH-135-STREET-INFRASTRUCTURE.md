## Six new records

1. **DUB-STR-0146 — Lady Grattan Fountain, St Stephen’s Green** (NIAH 50100257). Presented in 1880; red marble shaft, bronze spouts, granite basin and plinth. Irish Grid 316031, 233514.
2. **DUB-STR-0147 — Inchicore Railway Works water hydrant** (NIAH 50080411). c.1890 cast-iron Glenfield & Kennedy hydrant, with lion-head spout and maker’s inscription. Irish Grid 311030, 233263. Site access is not assumed; permission required.
3. **DUB-STR-0148 — James’s Street drinking fountain** (NIAH 50080307). Erected 1790, designed by Francis Sandys; fluted Portland-stone obelisk, sundials, carved masks and later cast-iron wall fountains. Irish Grid 313923, 233898.
4. **DUB-STR-0149 — Stanley Street tram rails and point** (NIAH 50070207). c.1900 cast-iron tram tracks set among granite cobbles, part of Dublin Corporation’s former waste-disposal transport system, which ceased in 1925. Irish Grid 314540, 234742.
5. **DUB-STR-0150 — Rutland Fountain, Collins Barracks** (NIAH 50070158). Dated 1785, presented by the Duke of Rutland; moved to current site in 1889. Inscription and granite/limestone structure recorded. Irish Grid 314117, 234534.
6. **DUB-STR-0151 — Gray Street / Reginald Street former MacFarlane fountain canopy** (NIAH 50080656). Former c.1900 drinking fountain, converted to a Sacred Heart monument in 1929; decorative ironwork and octagonal former fountain base survive. Irish Grid 314651, 233580.

## Coordinate and fieldwork controls

The published NIAH Irish Grid references were transformed from TM65 / Irish Grid (EPSG:29902) to WGS84 using EPSG operation 1641. Original grid references are retained. All WGS84 points are **source-derived, not founder GPS fixes**. Current physical condition, exact micro-position and safe access remain field-verification tasks. Inchicore Railway Works access is not assumed; photography there requires permission.

## Visual discovery

Six visual records and founder photography briefs were created. No third-party images were imported. All six await founder-captured originals; A4 line art must be created only from the matching founder photograph.
