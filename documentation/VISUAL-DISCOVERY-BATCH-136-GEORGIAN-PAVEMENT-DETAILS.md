# Visual Discovery Batch 136 — Georgian pavement details and street lettering

**Date:** 11 November 2026  
**Project:** CICAN DUBLIN  
**Founder:** CICAN GEORGE ALIN

## Research scope

This batch focuses on small details that are easily overlooked: cast-iron coal-hole covers embedded in granite pavements and carved stone street-name lettering.

## Four separately catalogued objects

1. **DUB-STR-0152 — 74 Eccles Street coal-hole cover** (NIAH 50070481). NIAH records a cast-iron coal-hole cover set in the granite pavement to the front of the Georgian house. Published Irish Grid 315580, 235537.
2. **DUB-STR-0153 — 77 Eccles Street coal-hole cover** (NIAH 50070484). NIAH records a cast-iron coal-hole cover in the granite pavement in front of the house. Published Irish Grid 315597, 235530.
3. **DUB-STR-0154 — 3 Mountjoy Square North coal-hole cover** (NIAH 50010820). NIAH records a cast-iron pavement coal-hole cover. The host building already exists as DUB-BLD-0025; this detail record is linked to that parent, not a duplicate building.
4. **DUB-STR-0155 — St Andrew Street carved limestone name plaque** (NIAH 50910150). The NIAH record describes a carved limestone plaque reading “St. Andrew’s Street”. The south end of the street was historically called Hog Hill.

## Coordinate precision and verification status

The published Irish Grid references were transformed from TM65 / Irish Grid (EPSG:29902) to WGS84 using EPSG operation 1641. These references identify the host buildings. They do **not** identify the exact centre of each coal-hole cover or plaque. Therefore, map coordinates are explicitly marked **SOURCE_DERIVED_APPROXIMATE**, not founder GPS. Exact feature positions, present survival and condition require field photography and verification.

The four objects are marked PARTIALLY_VERIFIED because the official survey documents the features but does not establish their current condition or exact present-day micro-position.

## Visual asset status

Four visual records and detailed founder photography briefs were registered. No third-party images were imported. All four await founder-captured original photographs; A4 line art must be created only from the corresponding original photograph.
