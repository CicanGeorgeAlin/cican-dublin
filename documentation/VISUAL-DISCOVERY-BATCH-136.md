# Visual Discovery Batch 136 — St Anne’s House archaeology and Rockwork Feature

**Date:** 11 November 2026  
**Project:** CICAN DUBLIN  
**Founder:** CICAN GEORGE ALIN

## New records

1. **DUB-ARC-0001 — St Anne’s House (formerly Thornhill), excavated mansion remains, portico and entrance.** Category: ARCHAEOLOGY. Verified from Dublin City Council's preliminary archaeological excavation report dated 20 November 2024, licence 22E0688 ext. The report documents 2024 excavation of 450 m² around the former mansion's southern façade, including the portico, porch, picture gallery and part of the main hall. It records in-situ walls and surfaces, window recesses, portico foundations, front-step details, encaustic floor tiles, a carriage-road surface and heating infrastructure. The report also records earlier investigation of the cellars and other rooms.
2. **DUB-LND-0001 — St Anne’s Park Rockwork Feature.** Category: LANDSCAPE. Status: RESEARCH_LEAD. Dublin City Council lists this as one of the park's twelve follies, and Oldstone Conservation separately names it in its conservation project. The contractor describes the smaller rockwork and rustic follies as mainly consisting of large stone boulders. The suggested Lough Corrib origin is not treated as established provenance. Exact feature location and current condition remain to be field-verified.

## Existing record identity improvement

**DUB-BLD-0138 / NIAH 50030032** is now titled **Pompeiian Temple / Boathouse (former tea house)**. Oldstone Conservation identifies the Boathouse as the Pompeiian Temple and states it was formerly used as a tea house; Dublin City Council's official follies list names the Boat House. This is an alias update to the existing NIAH structure, not a duplicate record.
