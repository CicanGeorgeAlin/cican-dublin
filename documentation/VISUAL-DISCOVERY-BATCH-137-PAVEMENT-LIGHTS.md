1. **DUB-STR-0156 — 18 Suffolk Street pavement light** (NIAH 50910139). NIAH records a cast-iron pavement light at street level serving the basement. Published Irish Grid 315948, 233949.
2. **DUB-STR-0157 — 21 Suffolk Street pavement light** (NIAH 50910142). NIAH records the basement as enclosed by a cast-iron pavement light. Published Irish Grid 315932, 233959.
3. **DUB-STR-0158 — 110 Marlborough Street cellar light** (NIAH 50010284). Glazed cast-iron pavement light set in granite slabs. Published Irish Grid 316077, 234519.
4. **DUB-STR-0159 — The Long Hall, 51 South Great George’s Street basement grille** (NIAH 50910032). NIAH records a grille serving the basement in the pavement to the front of the building. Published Irish Grid 315593, 233776.

## Coordinate precision

The published Irish Grid references were transformed from TM65 / Irish Grid (EPSG:29902) to WGS84 using EPSG operation 1641. These grid references locate the host buildings, not the exact centre of each pavement feature. Coordinates are explicitly marked **SOURCE_DERIVED_APPROXIMATE**, not founder GPS. Exact feature positions, current survival and condition require field photography and verification.
