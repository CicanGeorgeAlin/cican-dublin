# Visual Discovery Batch 138 — Benchmarks and hidden street details

**Date:** 11 November 2026  
**Project:** CICAN DUBLIN  
**Founder:** CICAN GEORGE ALIN

## Five new detail records

1. **DUB-STR-0160 — 1 Sherrard Street Lower benchmark and date panel** (NIAH 50010730). NIAH records an Ordnance Survey benchmark on the corner plinth wall and a Portland limestone panel inscribed “Lower Sherrard St. 1827”. This is distinct from the previously catalogued benchmark at 1 Sherrard Street Upper (DUB-STR-0048). Irish Grid 316002, 235735.
2. **DUB-STR-0161 — Cherrywood Railway Viaduct benchmark** (NIAH 60260097). A benchmark survives on a granite ashlar pier of the five-arch viaduct built 1852–1853 and opened in 1854. Irish Grid 324410, 222818.
3. **DUB-STR-0162 — 25 Pleasants Street ventilation pipe** (NIAH 50110355). NIAH records a full-height cast-iron ventilation pipe on the c.1845 house. Irish Grid 315430, 233021.
4. **DUB-STR-0163 — 45 Pearse Street pavement iron grille** (NIAH 50020369). NIAH records an iron grille set in granite paving and kerbing. Its original function is not specified, so the archive does not label it as a drain or vent. Irish Grid 316684, 234063.
5. **DUB-STR-0164 — The Lombard, 1 Lombard Street East basement pavement light** (NIAH 50020367). NIAH records cubed glass with granite pavier surrounds on the south side. Irish Grid 316671, 234068.

## Coordinate precision

The NIAH Irish Grid references were transformed from TM65 / Irish Grid (EPSG:29902) to WGS84 using EPSG operation 1641. They identify the host building or structure, not the exact micro-position of the benchmark, pipe, grille or pavement light. Coordinates are marked **SOURCE_DERIVED_APPROXIMATE**, not founder GPS. Exact positions, present survival and current condition require field verification.

## Visual asset status

Five visual records and detailed founder photography briefs were registered. No third-party images were imported. All five await founder-captured original photographs; A4 line art must be created only from the corresponding original photograph.
