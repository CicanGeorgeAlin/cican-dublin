# CICAN DUBLIN ARCHIVE — Fieldwork protocol

## Purpose
This document governs how a research discovery becomes a founder-led photography assignment and, later, a verified visual archive entry.

## Status vocabulary
- `RESEARCH_LEAD`: a possible discovery; identity or significance is not yet established.
- `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`: some facts are supported, but important fields remain unresolved.
- `VERIFIED`: identity, location and material historical claims have adequate cited evidence.
- `TO_PHOTOGRAPH`: verified and ready for a founder field visit.
- `PHOTOGRAPHED`: only after the founder confirms the original photograph exists.
- `LINE_ART_CREATED`: only after the illustration is produced and checked against the original.
- `PUBLISHED`: only after a live publication URL is confirmed.

## Minimum research record
Every object must have a unique ID, name, category, district, address or clearly described location, historical description, source references, verification status and next action. Coordinates are optional and must remain null until verified.

## Field photography
For each assignment, provide a map link, access notes, public/private access distinction, safety or photography restrictions, full-object views, contextual views and close-ups of inscriptions or construction details. Never advise entering restricted property or crossing barriers.

## Image preservation
Keep the founder's original photograph unchanged in the originals collection. Store line art separately. The line art must not add details absent from the source photograph.

## Duplicate control
Search existing records by name, address, coordinates (when verified), source-register identifier and parent structure before assigning a new ID. Distinct physical details may have child records linked to a parent building or monument.

## Evidence
Cite authoritative records and record the exact page or register identifier where possible. Conflicting dates or attributions must be documented as a discrepancy, not silently resolved.

## Publication
A record is not ready for a colouring-book page until the photograph, illustration, caption, rights/access checks and print quality have passed review.
