How the archive is built

Curiosity, backed
by evidence.

A beautiful image can invite discovery. Evidence gives it meaning. We keep those two responsibilities connected, but never confused.

01 · DISCOVER

Find the detail

Identify places and objects through field observation, maps, archives and heritage inventories.

02 · VERIFY

Follow the evidence

Check dates, descriptions, location and provenance. Separate confirmed facts from open questions.

03 · DOCUMENT

Preserve the view

Bring together rights-cleared historical references and original founder photography.

04 · SHARE

Make it accessible

Publish searchable records, source notes, walking links and faithful visual editions.
