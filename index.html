05 / Evidence standard

Every claim has a trail.

The first catalogue batch is based on National Inventory of Architectural Heritage records. Register identifiers are retained so each discovery can be checked against the source. Some locations, especially inside Trinity College, require access checks before a visit.

Irish Grid references are preserved separately; they are not misrepresented as GPS latitude and longitude. GPS fields remain blank until reliably converted or field-verified.
