04 / The production method

Research becomes preservation.

One coordinated workflow connects historical evidence, your field photography, faithful line art and published books.

01 — RESEARCH

Discover & verify

02 — FIELDWORK

Your photography

03 — ARTWORK

Original to line art

Preserve the original photo and create accurate A4 black-and-white artwork.

04 — PUBLICATION

Archive & publish

Connect the record to the website and its future Lulu book edition.

05 / Our standard

Evidence before appearance.

This archive is built on real places, physical objects and traceable historical evidence. A lead is not a confirmed discovery. A planned photograph is not a completed photograph. Every stage is recorded honestly.
