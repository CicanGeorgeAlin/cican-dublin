Cactus Provisoire — Alexander Calder

Fellows Square, Trinity College Dublin, Dublin 2

Welded and riveted painted sheet-steel sculpture by Alexander Calder, cast in 1967 and installed at Trinity in 1978. It stands in a cobbled circular setting with an inscribed limestone plaque.

Nationally rated public artwork recorded by NIAH.
