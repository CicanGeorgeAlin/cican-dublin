Talbot House, 9 Talbot Street

9 Talbot Street, Dublin 1

Symmetrical eleven-bay, three-storey building over an exposed basement, built in 1842 with a central seven-bay breakfront and two-bay wings added in 1859. NIAH describes granite ashlar at ground level, ruled-and-lined rendered upper walls, rusticated quoins, decorative frieze with rosettes and dentils, granite modillions, and iron rainwater goods.

A substantial mid-nineteenth-century institutional building with unusually rich masonry and decorative details, documenting the architectural development of Talbot Street.
