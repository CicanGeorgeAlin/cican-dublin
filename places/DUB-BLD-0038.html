7 Marino Crescent — Georgian doorway and leaded fanlight

7 Marino Crescent, Dublin 3

Terraced three-storey house over raised basement, built in 1792 as one of the twenty-five houses forming Marino Crescent. NIAH records an elliptical-headed doorway with a carved stone doorcase, panelled pilasters with foliate brackets, a frieze and a leaded petal fanlight. The entrance is approached by granite steps and a platform with wrought-iron and decorative cast-iron railings; a decorative cast-iron gate and matching railings stand on a carved limestone plinth wall.

A particularly detailed surviving Georgian entrance within one of Dublin's few Georgian crescents. Its carved doorcase, leaded fanlight and iron boundary work offer several distinct subjects for close-up archival photography. NIAH records the crescent as built by Charles ffolliot in the 1790s and notes the local 'Spite Crescent' story as a reputed explanation, not a proven fact.
