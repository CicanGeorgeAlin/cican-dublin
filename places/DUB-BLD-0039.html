Marino College — 1935–36 technical institute and library

14–20 Marino Mart, Dublin 3

Detached former technical institute and public library built in 1935–36, with a two-storey main composition and single-storey bow-fronted pavilions at the east and west ends; the central block was raised by an additional storey circa 1975. NIAH records brown brick laid in Flemish bond, herringbone-pattern brickwork over a raised ashlar granite plinth, carved granite mullions and continuous granite sill courses. Carved limestone plaques by L.T. Campbell depict Industry and Commerce. The main entrance has a carved ashlar granite surround, glazed sidelights and overlight, and three granite steps.

NIAH describes Marino Technical Institute as a flagship building that became a model for later technical schools in Ireland. Its reinforced-concrete construction, herringbone brickwork, carved granite and sculptural plaques preserve an important chapter in twentieth-century public education and technical training.
