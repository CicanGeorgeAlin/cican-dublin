Clontarf and Scots Presbyterian Church — Gothic Revival church

Howth Road / Clontarf Road, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Freestanding Gothic Revival Presbyterian church built in 1888–90, with a four-bay nave, south transept, chancel and a four-stage southwest tower added in 1897. NIAH records rusticated granite walling, red sandstone quoins and detailing, carved stone stringcourses, traceried and stained-glass windows, pointed-arch doorways and a hammer-beam roof. A 1919 Harry Clarke stained-glass window commemorates those who died in the First World War.

A landmark designed by architect Thomas Drew and a richly detailed example of late nineteenth-century Presbyterian church architecture. The 1919 Harry Clarke memorial window gives the interior an additional artistic and commemorative significance.
