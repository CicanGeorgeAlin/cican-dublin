Clontarf Promenade — Modernist concrete shelter

Clontarf Promenade, Clontarf Road, Dublin 3

Freestanding single-storey openwork reinforced-concrete shelter on the seafront promenade, built circa 1955–1958. NIAH describes canted corners to the front elevation, a bowed rear elevation, a flat roof with oversailing eaves, pebbledash platband, full-height openings and integral concrete benches to the front and rear, with a curved internal bench.

One of a series of precast-concrete shelters built by Dublin Corporation along Clontarf Promenade. NIAH identifies it as a strong example of Modernist seafront architecture, using a horizontal form and open geometry to provide shelter and views.
