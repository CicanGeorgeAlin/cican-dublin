Clontarf handball alley — former Garda station recreation structure

Clontarf Road at Strandville Avenue East, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Freestanding three-sided handball alley built circa 1935, with sloping side walls. NIAH describes yellow brick laid in Flemish bond to the outer walls, yellow-brick pilasters to the south elevation, smooth-rendered internal walls and remains of steel railings along the wall tops. The register describes it as currently disused.

NIAH identifies the alley as probably having been built as an amenity for officers of the adjacent Garda Síochána station. It is a well-preserved example of a standardised early twentieth-century handball-alley form and is associated with the cultural nation-building of the early Irish state.
