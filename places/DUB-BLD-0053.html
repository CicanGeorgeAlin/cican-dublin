Seaview Terrace — 77–81 Clontarf Road

77–81 Clontarf Road, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Terrace of five single-storey houses over raised basements, comprising one two-bay house and four three-bay houses, built circa 1845. NIAH records pitched M-profile roofs, parapets with cut-granite capping, brown-brick front elevations, granite stringcourses, rendered basements, historic timber sash windows at some openings, and round-headed doorways with cobweb fanlights, corniced and friezed surrounds, foliate console brackets and timber-panelled doors. Entrance steps and platforms have cast-iron balustrades; front boundaries include cast-iron pedestrian gates and rendered walls.

NIAH describes the terrace as retaining its early suburban form and character, with important surviving entrance, door, window and garden-boundary fabric. Its raised reception rooms and front gardens reflect the mid-nineteenth-century development of Clontarf Road as a seafront residential area.
