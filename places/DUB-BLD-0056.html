Victoria Terrace, 163–173 Clontarf Road

163–173 Clontarf Road, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Terrace of eleven houses built circa 1865, composed of projecting two-bay end houses over raised basements, intervening two-bay houses and a three-bay central house. NIAH records canted bay windows, M-profile slate roofs, red-brick Flemish-bond façades, granite plinths and sills, round-headed entrances, fanlights, timber-panelled doors, cast-iron handrails and wrought-iron gates.

Developed by George Tickell to designs by architect J.J. Lyons. NIAH describes the terrace as a carefully composed seafront streetscape, with taller projecting end houses and a central breakfront creating a focal point. Historic maps show an earlier post office and police station occupied the site.
