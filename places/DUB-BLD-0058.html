St Anthony's Hall — former Clontarf Town Hall

61 Clontarf Road, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Detached gable-fronted building erected in 1896 as Clontarf Town Hall, refaced and converted to a Catholic church in 1926, and later used as a parish hall. NIAH records a pitched slate roof, carved granite verge stones, kneelers and finial, rusticated granite front walling, red-brick side elevations, buttresses and cut-stone detailing.

Designed by township surveyor William G. Perrott and built by Robert Farquharson on land granted by Colonel Edward Vernon. Its changing uses include town hall, church, parish hall and, for a period in the 1920s, cinema. NIAH notes a reported association with meetings of Easter Rising rebels, but that claim should remain attributed to the register rather than presented as independently established fact.
