Edward VII cast-iron postbox — Castle Avenue

Castle Avenue, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Wall-mounted cast-iron postbox dating to circa 1905, set in a pier in front of the Clontarf telephone exchange. It carries the royal monogram of Edward VII and raised 'Post Office' lettering; the foundry mark at its base is illegible.

A surviving piece of early twentieth-century communications infrastructure that remains in active postal use according to the NIAH record. Its royal insignia and cast lettering make an ordinary street utility an unusually legible historic object.
