Rathmore — former pair of houses, 8–9 Castle Avenue

8–9 Castle Avenue, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Former semi-detached pair of three-bay, two-storey houses built circa 1915, now used as one house. NIAH records a gabled full-height box-bay window, shared recessed entrance, slate roof, red-brick Flemish-bond elevations, half-timbered gables, timber sash windows, granite steps and wrought-iron gates.

The building retains the form and character of two early twentieth-century houses despite their later amalgamation. Its brickwork, decorative gables, bay windows and surviving front boundary contribute to the early suburban character of Castle Avenue, one of Clontarf's earliest laid-out streets.
