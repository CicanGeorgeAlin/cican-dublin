Brian Boróimhe's Well — 1850 commemorative drinking fountain

Castle Avenue, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Former well and drinking-fountain feature with a decorative metal plaque dated 1850, set within a smooth-rendered wall. The plaque carries a shamrock motif and raised inscription: 'Erected over Brian Boróimhe's Well by subscription AD 1850'. NIAH records that it was placed over the remains of an earlier drinking fountain.

The memorial feature records a nineteenth-century public subscription and local association with Brian Boru and the Battle of Clontarf. NIAH states that the well is at or close to an ancient well; the precise archaeological relationship should not be asserted beyond that wording without further archaeological evidence.
