54 Vernon Avenue — 1913 detached villa

54 Vernon Avenue, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Detached three-bay single-storey house dated 1913, with box-bay windows, pier buttresses and a recessed entrance. NIAH records a flat roof with rendered round chimneystacks and crenellated parapet, smooth-rendered walls, a date plaque, pointed-arch doorway and crenellated front boundary piers.

One of four decorative villas built in succession by John Ryan, builder and plasterer, and his architect son Fergus Ryan on land leased from Col. Edward Vernon. NIAH identifies Nos. 50 and 54 as the most intact of the four. Vernon Avenue developed from a route to the Clontarf Sheds fishing village, where locally caught herring was cured.
