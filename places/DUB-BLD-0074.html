Woodside Villa — 70–72 Vernon Avenue

70–72 Vernon Avenue, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Pair of semi-detached houses, three bays and two bays respectively, built circa 1870 over basements. NIAH records flat-roofed canted bays, shared pitched slate roof, rendered parapet with moulded cornice, varied rendered wall finishes, decorative doorcases, granite entrance platforms and wrought-iron railings with cast-iron palmettes.

The pair illustrates the nineteenth-century suburban development of Vernon Avenue, historically part of the route to the Clontarf Sheds fishing village. No. 70 was known as Woodside Villa and was recorded as the residence of William Barton Esq. in 1908. The paired bays and ironwork retain streetscape and craft interest.
