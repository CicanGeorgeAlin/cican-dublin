Hillview Terrace / Rutland Terrace — 205–206 Clontarf Road

205–206 Clontarf Road, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Attached pair of two-bay, two-storey houses over basements, built in 1846 and circa 1860. The pair has full-height bow windows, recessed entrance bays, hipped roofs concealed behind parapets, moulded cornices, timber sliding-sash windows, round-headed doorways, decorative fanlights, granite entrance steps and cast-iron railings. Remnants of early coach houses survive to the rear lane.

No. 205 originally formed the end of a symmetrical five-house Hillview Terrace built by Joseph Mannin on land leased from J.E.V. Vernon in 1846. NIAH identifies No. 206 as a later neighbouring house, likely part of Rutland Terrace, despite its similar appearance. These buildings are among the earlier surviving seafront developments and occupy an area historically known as the Clontarf Sheds, associated with fish-drying sheds.
