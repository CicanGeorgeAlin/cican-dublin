18 Earlsfort Terrace — late-Victorian townhouse with surviving entrance ironwork

18 Earlsfort Terrace, Dublin 2

Terraced two-bay, three-storey former house over basement, built circa 1880 as one of a pair and now in office use. NIAH describes a carved brick and terracotta façade, timber oriel and stained-glass fanlight, granite steps, cast-iron bootscrape, cast-iron balustrade, matching gate, basement panel railings and a coal-hole cover set in a granite slab to the front pavement.

A fine late-Victorian townhouse whose carved brick, terracotta and cast-iron features preserve multiple examples of decorative craft in a highly detailed streetscape.
