Magazine Fort — eighteenth-century military powder magazine

St Thomas Hill, Phoenix Park, Dublin 8

Freestanding, broadly square-plan military magazine fort with four demi-bastions, watch-towers, an eastern extension and ranges of barrack and magazine buildings. The National Inventory of Architectural Heritage dates the complex to 1730–1810 and describes its principal construction phases around 1735, c.1760 and 1801. It was designed as a powder magazine for Dublin and retains original magazine chambers and evidence of successive military uses. The Office of Public Works led a major conservation programme; the fort reopened for guided public tours in 2026.

A nationally rated military complex associated with the defence and supply infrastructure of Dublin and the 1916 Rising. Its conservation and 2026 reopening make it an especially important current heritage site.
