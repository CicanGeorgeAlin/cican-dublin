Ashtown Castle — restored fortified tower house

Phoenix Park Visitor Centre, Chesterfield Avenue, Phoenix Park, Dublin 8

Detached three-storey fortified tower house with a square-plan stair tower to the south-east, built around 1605. The building has uncoursed limestone rubble walls, a pitched slate saddle-back roof behind parapets, a dressed-stone chimney, small square-headed windows and defensive loops. It was incorporated into a larger Georgian residence around 1760; that later lodge was subsequently demolished. The tower was restored and now forms part of the Phoenix Park Visitor Centre heritage experience.

A rare surviving fortified house of the early seventeenth century close to Dublin city, preserving evidence of the earlier Ashtown demesne and later park administration.
