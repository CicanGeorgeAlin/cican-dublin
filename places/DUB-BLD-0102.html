Castleknock Gate — Decimus Burton's granite and iron entrance screen

Castleknock Gate, Castleknock Road and Chesterfield Avenue, Phoenix Park, Dublin 8

Cast-iron and granite gate screen at a principal western entrance to Phoenix Park. The National Inventory describes double-leaf geometric spear-headed cast-iron vehicular gates, matching railings over a granite dwarf wall, single-leaf pedestrian gates, six square-section ashlar granite piers with corniced caps and plinths, and brass lamps on foliate cast-iron standards. The screen forms a designed composition with the adjacent Castleknock Gate Lodge (a separate NIAH record, 50060003).

A fine surviving component of Decimus Burton's nineteenth-century remodelling of Phoenix Park, illustrating the coordinated design of gates, ironwork, stone piers, lighting and gate lodges.
