CIÉ Goods Depot — former North Wall Railway Station

48–57 North Wall Quay, Dublin 1

Detached symmetrical thirteen-bay two-storey brick-and-stone former North Wall Railway Station, arranged around a central three-bay block and taller five-bay wings, with a single-storey curved eastern wing. The NIAH records hipped slate roofs, granite ashlar parapets, moulded granite plinths and quoins, red-brick elevations and substantial original interior fabric including timber-truss roofs, glazed screens and boarded or tiled floors. The building served the North Wall railway terminus and was later used for freight and railway administration.

A major surviving component of Dublin's North Wall railway and port complex, associated with cross-channel mail and passenger traffic, wartime troop movements and later freight operations.
