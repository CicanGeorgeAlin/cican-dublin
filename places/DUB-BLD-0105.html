North Wall Quay — Wool Store

North Wall Quay, beside the former North Wall Railway Station, Dublin 1

Attached eleven-bay three-storey former warehouse built of random-rubble calp limestone, with roughly squared limestone quoins, gauged brown-brick round-arched openings and a central pedimented loading bay with an oculus. The NIAH identifies it locally as the Wool Store and records its later incorporation into the North Wall Railway Station complex, where the railway company used the ground floor as stables. The NIAH survey described the building as disused but secured.

A rare surviving mid-nineteenth-century port warehouse whose loading bay, oculus, limestone walling and railway-era adaptation illustrate the changing uses of the North Wall waterfront.
