Richford Motors — former Dublin and Glasgow Steam Packet Company building

73 North Wall Quay, Dublin 1

Detached three-storey brick commercial building erected for the Dublin and Glasgow Steam Packet Company, with a central pediment, moulded limestone raking cornice, red-brick English-garden-wall bond and yellow-brick and sandstone details. The NIAH records a later showroom use and a new structure built behind the retained historic façade around 2000. Original fanlight, iron grilles and polychromatic brick details contribute to the surviving street frontage.

A rare pre-twentieth-century commercial and maritime company frontage on this section of North Wall Quay, retaining a distinctive nineteenth-century decorative façade despite later redevelopment.
