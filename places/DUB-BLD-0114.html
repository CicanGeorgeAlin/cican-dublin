Grand Canal Quay — triple-gable former malt house

Grand Canal Quay, Dublin 2

Attached triple-gable-fronted former malt house built around 1890, comprising three single-storey blocks with attics. The NIAH records pitched roofs, moulded yellow-brick eaves cornice, carved granite coping, brown-brick front elevation with granite quoins and plinth, rubble-limestone walling, pattress plates, triple lancet windows with carved block-and-start surrounds and granite sills, infilled openings and segmental-headed doorways.

Part of the industrial group around Grand Canal Docks. The three gabled fronts, carved granite details and surviving industrial fabric form a distinctive architectural composition, built around the time the neighbouring sugar refinery was converted to a distillery.
