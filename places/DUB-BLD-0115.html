Grand Canal Quay — Guinness former malt house of 1886

Grand Canal Quay, Dublin 2

Attached twenty-one-bay, four-storey former malt house built by the Guinness brewery in 1886, with three attic bays and later continuous glazed attic extension. The NIAH records Flemish-bond yellow brick above snecked limestone ground-floor walls, granite stringcourses and quoins, cast-iron rainwater goods, pattress plates, square- and segmental-headed openings, brick reveals and granite sills. Remodelled in 1995 by T.J. Cullen and Co. The skewed end accommodates the adjacent railway line.

A substantial surviving component of the Guinness industrial landscape at Grand Canal Dock, illustrating the close relationship between brewing, canal transport and rail freight. The brick-over-limestone construction and unusual skewed end are key architectural features.
