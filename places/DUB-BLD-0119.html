R. & H. Hall — monumental reinforced-concrete grain silo

Alexandra Road / Branch Road South, Dublin Port, Dublin 1

A detached nine-bay reinforced-concrete grain silo rising approximately fifteen to twenty storeys, built 1915–1920 and designed by Frederick G. Hicks. NIAH records an attached steel-bin silo added around 1932 and a further reinforced-concrete silo added in 1938. The building has deeply recessed vertical façade panels, a heavy cornice and a central head-house. NIAH notes loading arches with granite wheel-guards and diorite setts.

A nationally rated example of large-scale early twentieth-century industrial architecture and port grain storage. NIAH describes it as exceptionally elaborate in scale and design for an Irish grain silo.
