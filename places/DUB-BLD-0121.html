Odlum's Mills — c.1935 grain tower and steel silo drums

Alexandra Road / Branch Road South, Dublin Port, Dublin 1

Detached multi-bay, multi-storey reinforced-concrete grain silo built around 1935, with a two-bay wing and riveted cylindrical steel grain-silo drums to east and west. NIAH records reinforced-concrete tower walls, steel casement windows, vertical glazed panels and a two-storey loading block with timber doors.

A regional-rated industrial composition combining reinforced-concrete grain storage and riveted steel silo drums, with restrained Art Deco characteristics identified by NIAH.
