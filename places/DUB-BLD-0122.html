Alexandra Road — early steel-frame redbrick industrial building

Alexandra Road / Branch Road South, Dublin Port, Dublin 1

Detached multi-bay, multi-storey industrial building built around 1920, with a narrow western wing. NIAH describes redbrick walls in stretcher bond carried on a riveted iron frame, pitched roofs with replacement steel sheeting, a raised central section with timber louvres and later inserted tripartite timber-framed windows.

A regional-rated example of early twentieth-century industrial architecture and early steel-frame construction within the port landscape. NIAH highlights its exposed iron-frame construction and textured redbrick envelope.
