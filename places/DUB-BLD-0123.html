Port Centre — Dublin Port Company headquarters

East Wall Road / Alexandra Road, Dublin Port, Dublin 3

Detached six-storey office building over basement, supported on a single-height podium, designed by Conor Dwyer of Scott Tallon Architects following an architectural competition in 1954 and built in 1980–1981. The NIAH describes a glazed envelope set behind an external precast-concrete frame, continuous recessed glazing panels, a louvred concrete parapet carrying Dublin Port Company insignia, a glazed entrance screen with circular glass enclosures and revolving doors, and a concrete entrance platform approached by granite-faced steps. The building is bounded by rubble-limestone walls and includes pierced cast-iron double-leaf gates to the north.

A prominent late-twentieth-century architectural landmark at the entrance to Dublin Port. NIAH highlights its technically innovative external concrete frame and full-height glazing, and its role as an architectural marker between the city and the industrial port landscape.
