2 Sir John Rogerson's Quay — former Tedcastle Steamer Company warehouse (Bushell Ltd. Furniture)

2 Sir John Rogerson's Quay, Dublin 2

Attached gable-fronted three-bay, two-storey former warehouse built in 1913–14. NIAH records a pitched slate roof set perpendicular to the street with a glazed clerestory, carved granite coping to the gable, cast-iron rainwater goods, red-brick Flemish-bond walls, granite string courses and red-brick pilasters with granite coping. The front elevation includes recessed brick panels, blocked segmental-headed ground-floor openings, square-headed upper windows with bull-nosed brick surrounds and an oculus at the gable apex. A double-height round-headed opening with raised brick pilasters was added around 1980. NIAH identifies its former name as Tedcastle Steamer Company and describes later occupation by the British & Irish Steam Packet Company.

A regional-rated mercantile warehouse illustrating the early-twentieth-century commercial and maritime character of Sir John Rogerson's Quay. NIAH attributes the design to James Evans for ship-owner Tedcastle McCormack. The façade is separately listed by Dublin City Council as protected structure RPS 7543.
