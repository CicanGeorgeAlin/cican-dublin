Columbia Mills — former H.M. Leask & Company mill, 14–15 Sir John Rogerson's Quay

14–15 Sir John Rogerson's Quay, Dublin 2

Attached gable-fronted five-bay, three-storey former mill built c.1890, now recorded by NIAH as offices. The inventory describes a pitched corrugated-steel roof with roof lights behind a parapet with central pediment and painted masonry coping, lined-and-ruled rendered walls, segmental-headed window openings with rendered surrounds and painted masonry sills, and later replacement windows and ground-floor shutters. NIAH records Thomas Crosby's sailcloth and canvas manufacturers on the site in 1880 and H.M. Leask & Company in 1894; the building is associated with Leask's linseed-crushing trade.

Columbia Mills preserves a rare industrial trace within the former mixed-use quay landscape. Its association with sailcloth manufacture and H.M. Leask & Company connects the building to maritime supply trades and to the industrial streetscape described in James Joyce's Ulysses.
