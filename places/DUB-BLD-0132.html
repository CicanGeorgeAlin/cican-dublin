St Anne’s Park — Clock Tower and walled-garden entrance

Entrance to the walled garden, St Anne’s Park, Raheny, Dublin 5

An attached square-plan four-stage bell tower forming the entrance to the walled garden. The NIAH describes yellow-brick walls, a carved granite cornice and stepped parapet with corner finials, a carved shield, a recessed panel with stone cherubs, and an inset clock with a carved surround. The integral round-headed arch has a carved limestone keystone and wrought-iron gate. The upper stage contains round-headed openings with carved stone Y-tracery and louvred vents.

A functional and ornamental estate clock and bell tower. The NIAH records that the clock would have been visible from the house and gardens, while the bell regulated activities on the estate. It was originally inscribed with Benjamin Lee Guinness’s name and the family motto “Spes Mea in Deo”, as recorded by the NIAH.
