St Anne’s Park — Pompeiian Temple / Boathouse (former tea house)

St Anne’s Park, Raheny, Dublin 5, Ireland

Freestanding single-bay, single-storey temple folly overlooking an artificial pond. The NIAH describes a carved granite temple front with broken pediment, painted masonry columns, paired terminating pilasters and cast-iron railings; a south-facing portico with composite columns, granite platform and steps; rendered elevations with pilasters; square-headed gated openings; cobbled interior floor; and a rubble-stone plinth with segmental- and round-headed arches.

A separate classical folly in the designed landscape of the Guinness estate. Its carved granite frontage, south portico and deliberate relationship with the artificial pond illustrate the mid-nineteenth-century use of classical architectural forms to create picturesque garden settings.
