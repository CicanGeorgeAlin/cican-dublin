25 Hatch Street Lower — early nineteenth-century townhouse and entrance fabric

25 Hatch Street Lower, Dublin 2, Ireland

Terraced two-bay three-storey former house over basement with attic accommodation, built c.1810. NIAH records a quarry-tile and granite entrance platform with wrought-iron boot-scrape, granite steps, wrought-iron railings with cast-iron posts, and a cast-iron coal-hole cover in the footpath.

The townhouse retains architectural and entrance details associated with the early nineteenth-century development of Hatch Street, approved by the Wide Streets Commissioners in 1791.
