James Joyce Centre, 35 North Great George's Street — 1784 townhouse

35 North Great George's Street, Dublin 1, Ireland

End-of-terrace three-bay four-storey house over exposed basement, built in 1784 and now used as a museum. NIAH records red-brick walls, cast-iron air vents below windows, cast-iron balconettes, a doorcase with an original cobweb fanlight and inset lantern, a Victorian bell push, granite entrance platform and steps, wrought-iron railings, and a cast-iron corner lamp standard.

A nationally rated Georgian townhouse with architectural, artistic, cultural and historical significance, associated with the Gardiner Estate and later cultural history of the building.
