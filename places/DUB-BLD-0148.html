45 Home Farm Road — corner shop and Modernist building

45 Home Farm Road at Clare Road, Dublin 9, Ireland

Attached multiple-bay three-storey corner building with canted corner entrance bay, ground-floor shopfront and flat roof behind a concrete parapet. NIAH records Flemish-bond brickwork, geometric acroteria, concrete platbands and cornice, a timber shopfront with polished granite plinth and stained-glass overlights.

A distinctive suburban Modernist commercial building whose canted corner, geometric detailing and surviving shopfront fabric contrast with nearby late nineteenth-century terraces.
