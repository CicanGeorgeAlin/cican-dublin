Three Sisters Craft, 187 Botanic Road

187 Botanic Road, Glasnevin, Dublin 9, Ireland

End-of-terrace three-bay two-storey house and shop dated 1901. NIAH describes a cranked façade, two angled northern bays, a curvilinear sandstone parapet inscribed with the date, domestic-revival gables, red-brick walling and a shopfront framed by brick piers with foliate capitals.

A distinctive early twentieth-century commercial and domestic building within Glasnevin village. Its dated parapet, cranked façade and crafted brick shopfront contribute to the architectural character of the historic village centre.
