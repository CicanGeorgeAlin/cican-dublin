Swords Carnegie Free Library

North Street, Swords, County Dublin, Ireland

Detached three-bay, two-storey Carnegie Free Library built in 1908, with yellow brick and red-brick dressings, a projecting canted central bay, limestone stringcourse and plaque, moulded entrance arch and timber sash windows. NIAH also records original interior fittings including a glazed timber counter or screen.

A purpose-built Carnegie library with a distinctive civic façade and surviving original library fittings, documenting early twentieth-century public education and reading provision in Swords.
