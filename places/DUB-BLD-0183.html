40–82 Kincora Road — International Style semi-detached housing group

40–82 Kincora Road, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Group of eleven pairs of five-bay, two-storey semi-detached houses built around 1930. NIAH describes flat roofs, central balconies to the front bays, alternating entrances, rendered walls with red-brick panels, concrete sills and canopies, and front garden boundaries with metal gates and brick piers. Some properties have later roof and window alterations.

Designed by Joseph Downes of McDonnell & Dixon for the Dublin Commercial Public Utility Society. NIAH identifies the group as an example of the International Style in Irish housing, with its paired villa-like composition and retained boundary treatments contributing to the suburban streetscape.
