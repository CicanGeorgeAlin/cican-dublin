25 The Stiles Road — International Modern house

25 The Stiles Road, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Detached irregular-plan house built 1935–37, comprising single-, two- and three-storey sections with rounded corners and a flat-roofed open-sided porch. NIAH describes flat roofs behind rendered parapets, smooth rendered walls, rounded corner windows and vertical strip windows. The building has a front steel gate and rendered garden wall; some window frames and the entrance door are replacements.

Designed by Vincent O'Hanlon and identified by NIAH as an example of the International Modern style in a suburban residential setting, with subdued Art Deco references in its curved corners, stepped façade and flat roofs.
