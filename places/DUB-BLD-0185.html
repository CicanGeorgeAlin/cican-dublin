Clontarf Hospital — historic Blackheath house

Blackheath Park, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Former detached complex-plan, multiple-bay, three-storey house with dormer attic, built in 1872 and extended in 1879. NIAH describes canted bays, box bays, a gabled entrance breakfront, carved timber and stone details, Portland stone window surrounds, stained-glass stair-hall windows and decorative interior plasterwork and joinery. NIAH records the building as in use as a hospital administration building.

Designed by Thomas Drew for Gibson Black and built in two phases during the 1870s. The house was later occupied by the Orthopaedic Hospital, founded in 1876, and is part of the history of the Incorporated Orthopaedic Hospital of Ireland. The hospital's own history records its move to the Clontarf site in 1941; the NIAH record documents the historic house itself.
