Kilcrea House — c.1800 farmhouse

Kilcrea House, Kilcrea, Donabate, Fingal, County Dublin, Ireland

Detached five-bay, two-storey farmhouse with a parallel range to the southeast and a return to the northeast. The NIAH record notes renovation and refenestration c.1985, with a projecting entrance porch added to the return. Several outbuildings and entrance gateways are associated with the site. The wider heritage audit notes that the townland is documented from the fourteenth century and that the house and mill appear on Rocque's 1760 map; this earlier estate reference is not a revised construction date for the present farmhouse.

NIAH rating Regional, with Architectural and Artistic interest. The farmhouse forms part of a historic agricultural and demesne landscape, with associated outbuildings and gateways.
