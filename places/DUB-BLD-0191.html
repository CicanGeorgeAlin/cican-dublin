Corballis House — c.1830 farmhouse and farmyard group

Corballis House, Corballis, Donabate, Fingal, County Dublin, Ireland

Detached three-bay, two-storey farmhouse with dormer attic and central projecting two-storey bay. NIAH records a rear return, an attached former farm building now converted to a dwelling, another attached farm building, and a farmyard complex to the south and east containing buildings from c.1760 to the present. The object is registered as the farmhouse; associated buildings are described as part of its setting and are not automatically split into separate archive IDs.

NIAH rating Regional, with Architectural interest. The farmhouse and associated farmyard retain evidence of the historic agricultural landscape at Corballis.
