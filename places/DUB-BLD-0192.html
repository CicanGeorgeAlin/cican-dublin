Kilbush House — c.1850 thatched house

Kilbush Lane, Rush, Fingal, County Dublin, Ireland

Detached west-facing seven-bay single-storey thatched house with dormer attic, terminating Kilbush Lane. The NIAH describes a projecting timber entrance porch added c.1980 and a flat-roofed rear extension c.1970. Fingal County Council's 2022 survey records a pitched wheaten-straw roof with flush bobbin ridge, bamboo rodding and a lattice band, rendered chimney stack, pebbledash front, painted random-coursed stone to the rear, timber casement windows and later extensions.

NIAH rates the house Regional for Architectural and Social interest. Fingal's 2022 Thatch Project describes Kilbush House as one of Rush's last five remaining houses with intact early thatched roofs, highlighting its importance to the town's vernacular building heritage.
