Rose Cottage (Roseville) — c.1860 thatched house

Skerries Road (R127), Greatcommon, Lusk, Fingal, County Dublin, Ireland

Detached east-facing five-bay single-storey thatched house with a projecting entrance porch. The NIAH describes a double-pitched roof, gabled to the right and hipped to the left, with corrugated iron to the rear pitch; two brick chimney stacks with clay pots; cast-iron rainwater goods; slate roofs to the front and rear porches; roughcast-rendered walls; and two-over-two timber sash windows. Fingal County Council's 2022 thatch survey additionally records a front windbreak, enclosed front garden with wrought-iron railings, long rear garden and outbuilding, wheaten-straw roof with bamboo rodding and lattice band, and rear roof slope covered with galvanised sheeting at the time of survey.

NIAH rates the house Regional for Architectural and Social interest. Fingal County Council's 2022 survey notes early façade features including sash windows and a windbreak, and describes its ornamental garden setting. The 2022 report is a dated survey record, not confirmation of present-day condition.
