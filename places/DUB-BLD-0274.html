Leixlip Road — detached house (NIAH 11201005)

Leixlip Road, Cooldrinagh, Leixlip, County Dublin, Ireland

The official NIAH record (registration 11201005) describes: Detached eight-bay single-storey house, c.1860, recorded as unoccupied at the time of the NIAH survey. Roughcast rendered walls with pronounced base batter to the road side. Square-headed window openings, boarded up to the entrance front, retaining stone sills and timber sash and casement windows to the road side. Projecting gabled entrance porch with round-headed doorway. Pitched slate roof with perforated cresting, carved barge boards and single chimney stack. Blocked entrance gate in the boundary wall to the east.

A simple domestic building, unusual in its orientation away from the road, and a valuable element of the streetscape on the approach to Leixlip village.
