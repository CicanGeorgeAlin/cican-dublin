2 Cooldrinagh Lane

2 Cooldrinagh Lane, Cooldrinagh Lane, Leixlip, County Dublin, Ireland

The official NIAH record (registration 11201011) describes: Terraced three-bay two-storey house, c.1830. Roughcast rendered walls with timber sash windows having architrave surrounds, and a replacement timber panelled door. Projecting cast-iron arched hood on slender cast-iron columns to the entrance. Pitched slate roof with chimney stack to the rear. Original cast- and wrought-iron railings to the street.

A handsome terraced house which retains its original appearance, including its windows and cast-iron decoration. An important element of the Cooldrinagh Lane area.
