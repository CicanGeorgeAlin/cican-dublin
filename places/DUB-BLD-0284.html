Trinity City Hotel (former Central Fire Station)

Junction of Pearse Street and Tara Street, Dublin 2, County Dublin, Ireland

Corner-sited attached five-bay three-storey over basement former fire station, built 1906, having five-bay elevation to south, square-profile six-stage integrated tower with corbelled platform and parapet to sixth stage, with bull-nosed corner bay to southwest. Now in use as hotel. The tower has a pyramidal lead roof, carved limestone cornices and clock faces. The elevations retain red brick and ashlar limestone detailing, paired sash windows and an arcade of five segmental-headed carriage arches to the south.

Designed by city architect C.J. McCarthy and built by James Donovan & Sons, the building served as the central fire station for the Dublin fire brigade. Its tower, modelled on an Italian campanile, served as a fire lookout and for hanging and drying leather hoses. The four clocks were made by Ganter Brothers in 1906. The NIAH also records the building’s original dormitories, gym, stables and rapid-response features, including telegraph signalling and quick-opening engine-bay doors.
