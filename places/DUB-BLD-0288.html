21 Sycamore Street — former furniture workshop

21 Sycamore Street, Dublin 2, County Dublin, Ireland

Attached two-bay two-storey former workshop, built c.1850 and recorded by NIAH as disused. The hipped roof is set perpendicular to the street and hidden behind a lined-and-ruled rendered parapet with cut-granite coping to the west elevation. The rendered walls have quoins and a string course above a smooth rendered plinth, with uncoursed rubble limestone visible at the plinth and brown brick visible to the north elevation. A round-headed first-floor window has a cut-granite sill and multiple-pane timber-framed window; the ground-floor window and door openings have later metal grilles and a metal door.

The NIAH appraisal notes that Thom’s Directory of 1862 describes this building, together with three adjoining buildings to the south on Dame Street, as being used by a furniture manufacturer. The repeated opening sizes, continuous plinth and lined-and-ruled elevation support its identification as a good example of nineteenth-century industrial architecture in an urban setting.
