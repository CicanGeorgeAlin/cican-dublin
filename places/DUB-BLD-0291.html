Morningstar Hostel — former North Union Workhouse

Morningstar Avenue, Dublin 7, County Dublin, Ireland

Detached irregular-plan multiple-bay three-storey former workhouse, built 1879, with full-height single-bay projections, a three-bay three-storey extension and single-storey block to the front (south), and later extensions to the front and rear. Now in use as a hostel. Hipped slate roof, red-brick chimneystacks, some cast-iron rainwater goods, dressed calp-limestone walls, block-and-start quoins and stepped buttresses. Square-headed windows have red-brick voussoirs and surrounds, concrete sills and replacement uPVC/aluminium windows. A stained-glass window is recorded at ground floor to the front; a chapel is recorded internally.

The NIAH appraisal records that the House of Industry stood here from 1773 and was converted into a workhouse in 1841, with extensions by George Wilkinson in subsequent decades. It describes the building as a physical reminder of the workhouse system, the Great Famine and the destitution of the surrounding decades. Its calp-limestone construction and regular fenestration contribute to the institutional streetscape.
