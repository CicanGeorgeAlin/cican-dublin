43 O'Connell Street Upper — 1925 Portland-stone commercial building

43 O'Connell Street Upper, Dublin 1, County Dublin, Ireland

Terraced two-bay five-storey building over a partially exposed basement, dated 1925, with a shopfront to the ground floor. It is now recorded by the NIAH as a public house, with a two-storey brick building fronting onto Moore Lane. A flat roof is concealed behind a Portland-stone parapet with a shallow pediment inscribed “AD 1925” and a deep moulded parapet cornice. Coursed Portland-stone ashlar walls have three pilasters rising from the shopfront to the parapet, interrupted by a deep moulded cornice with mutules and a frieze over the third floor. Outer pilasters have Ionic capitals with lions' heads and foliate mouldings. The façade retains elaborate stone carving, paired window openings and a polished-granite shopfront plinth and pilasters.

The NIAH appraisal states that the building replaced an earlier house following the destruction of much of the streetscape during the 1916 Rising. It was built as a shoe shop in 1925 by Batchelor and Hicks. The appraisal highlights its quality stone carving and unusually well-preserved shopfront, and its contribution to the early twentieth-century character of O'Connell Street.
