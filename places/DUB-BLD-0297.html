43 Capel Street — c.1810 former house and shopfront

43 Capel Street, Dublin 1, County Dublin, Ireland

Terraced two-bay four-storey former house, built c.1810, with a shopfront to the ground floor. The upper floors are recorded as disused. The roof is concealed behind a rendered parapet with granite coping. Cast-iron rainwater goods include a hopper. The upper floors have rendered walling and diminishing square-headed window openings with slightly projecting painted reveals and painted masonry sills; replacement metal-framed casement windows are recorded. The replacement shopfront has a modern pilaster and fascia and a single modern entrance door. The building fronts directly onto Capel Street.

The NIAH appraisal describes the building as a representative example of nineteenth-century development on Capel Street, with combined commercial and residential use. It contributes to the historic built form of Capel Street Architectural Conservation Area, identified by the NIAH as one of Dublin's oldest commercial streets.
