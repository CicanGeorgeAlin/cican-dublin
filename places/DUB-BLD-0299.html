52 Bayview Avenue — late Georgian terraced house

52 Bayview Avenue, Dublin 3, County Dublin, Ireland

Terraced two-bay two-storey house over a raised basement, built c.1830 as one of four, with a return to the rear. The pitched artificial-slate roof is hidden behind a brick parapet with cut-granite coping. The front elevation has brown brickwork laid in Flemish bond over a cut-granite plinth course and ruled-and-lined rendered basement walling. Square-headed window openings have raised render reveals and masonry sills; replacement uPVC frames are recorded. The round-headed doorway has a moulded render surround and timber doorcase with panelled pilasters, scrolled brackets with acanthus-leaf detail, a cornice and petal fanlight. A flight of seven granite steps leads to the entrance platform. Cast-iron railings and a matching gate enclose the front boundary.

The NIAH appraisal describes the house as an example of the regular proportions and classically restrained detailing of late Georgian urban domestic architecture. The petal fanlight and surviving early fabric add artistic interest. The house forms part of a short terrace with similar parapet heights and fenestration, dating to a period when development beyond Dublin's canals was intensifying.
