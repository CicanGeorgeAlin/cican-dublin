Dublin City Gallery — Charlemont House

22 Parnell Square North, Dublin 1, County Dublin, Ireland

Detached, symmetrical five-bay, three-storey townhouse designed by Sir William Chambers for Lord Charlemont and built between 1763 and 1778. It was remodelled and extended in 1931–1933 by Horace O’Rourke for use as a municipal art gallery, with a further glazed rear wing added around 2006. The limestone and rendered façade features an elaborate Ionic portico, quadrant forecourt wings and obelisks. Significant interior features include a decorated entrance hall, staircase and sculpture gallery.

The NIAH rates Charlemont House National and describes it as a major work of eighteenth-century architecture and a significant public art gallery. The design, craftsmanship, retained historic interiors and later gallery conversion all contribute to its architectural, artistic and cultural importance. Its position within the planned Parnell Square composition is also significant.
