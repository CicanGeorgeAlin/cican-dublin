Saint Paul's Christian Brothers School — 1890 school building

Saint Paul's Christian Brothers School, Brunswick Street North, Dublin 7, County Dublin, Ireland

Detached eight-bay, two-storey school dated 1890, with gabled breakfronts, hipped slate roofs, brown brick laid in Flemish bond, yellow-brick detailing and a granite plinth. The façades include tripartite and segmental-arched window openings, terracotta name and date plaques, and arched entrances. Replacement uPVC windows are recorded in the NIAH description. The building sits within a cluster of associated school structures.

The NIAH appraisal identifies the building as part of a socially important school group designed by the Dublin partnership Ashlin & Coleman. Its gabled composition, contrasting brickwork, terracotta details and high-level school windows illustrate late nineteenth-century educational architecture and the building’s original institutional purpose.
