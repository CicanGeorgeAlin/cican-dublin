Mendicity Institution gate screen — Usher's Island

9 Usher's Island, Dublin 8, County Dublin, Ireland

A mid-nineteenth-century gate screen originally forming the entrance to the Mendicity Institution. A pair of carved square-profile granite piers flank double-leaf cast-iron gates, with matching railings set on limestone plinth walls capped in granite. Pedimented pedestrian entrances form part of the boundary; the NIAH record notes that the east entrance is blocked and the west entrance has a steel gate.

The NIAH appraisal identifies the gate screen as the only surviving remnant of the Mendicity Institution, a poverty-relief charity established in 1818. It is associated with the former Moira House site and the 1916 Rising, when Volunteers led by Seán Heuston occupied the building. The masonry and ironwork are noted for their craftsmanship.
