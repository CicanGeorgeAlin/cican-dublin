Dublin City Council, Wood Quay, Fishamble Street, Dublin 8, Ireland

Attached pair of square-plan multi-storey office towers over basements with chamfered corners, built in 1983 as the first phase of Dublin Corporation’s Civic Offices. The towers have cut-granite cladding, a granite-tiled skirt roof and recessed horizontal strip windows. Recessed ground-floor door openings have glazed doors, and a later glazed atrium connects the towers.

The NIAH appraisal records that the Wood Quay site was allocated for Dublin Corporation’s headquarters in the 1940s, but construction was delayed by the discovery of remains of the Viking city. The towers were designed by Sam Stephenson; the original proposal included two further towers to the north-west, while a later phase was designed by Scott Tallon Walker in the late 1990s. The building remains a prominent and controversial landmark in the city.
