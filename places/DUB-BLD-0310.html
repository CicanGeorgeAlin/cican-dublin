5 Parnell Square East, Dublin 1, DUBLIN

5 Parnell Square East, Dublin 1, Ireland

Terraced two-bay four-storey house over exposed basement, built c.1755 as a pair with No. 6, with a three-storey rear addition. The roof is concealed behind a rebuilt red-brick parapet with granite coping. The front combines Flemish-bond red brick, a granite plinth and rendered basement walls. A plaque commemorates Senator Oliver St. John Gogarty. The entrance has a painted stone doorcase with rusticated pilasters and an open-bed pediment; cast-iron lamp posts and bootscrapers flank the approach, which is enclosed by iron railings. The NIAH record also describes surviving decorative interior plasterwork and historic room arrangements.

The NIAH appraisal describes the house as part of the historic Parnell Square Georgian streetscape. The site was leased in 1754 by Henry Darley, master mason to Dr Bartholomew Mosse, and the house was the birthplace and home of Senator Oliver St. John Gogarty. It retains significant Georgian fabric, including its doorcase, brickwork and historically sympathetic sash windows, as well as decorative interior details.
