2 Lennox Place, Dublin 8, DUBLIN

2 Lennox Place, Dublin 8, Ireland

Attached three-bay two-storey house over a raised basement, built around 1800 as a pair with the house to the north, with a return to the rear east elevation. It has a pitched slate roof concealed behind a brick parapet with cut-granite coping, brick chimneystacks with clay pots and cast-iron rainwater goods. Red brick laid in Flemish bond rises above a masonry plinth, with rendered basement walls. Square-headed windows have rendered reveals, masonry sills and two-over-two pane timber sliding sash windows. The round-headed entrance has a timber doorcase with panelled pilasters and cornice, a plain fanlight and glazed panelled door; granite steps, a surviving bootscrape and cast-iron railings complete the approach.

The NIAH appraisal identifies the double-fronted composition, centrally placed doorcase and regular fenestration as characteristic of late Georgian architecture. The simple cast-iron railings add decorative interest. It is one of a small group of buildings from Portobello's earliest development phase, associated with construction of the Grand Canal harbour in 1801.
