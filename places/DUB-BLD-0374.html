Liffey Descent

7 The Mall, Main Street, Lucan, Lucan And Pettycanon, County Dublin, Ireland

Terraced three-bay two-storey over basement house, c.1815. Roughcast rendered walls. Timber sash windows. Round-headed doorway with replacement timber panelled door having radial fanlight. Pitched slate roof with brick and rendered chimney stacks. Lean-to extension to rere. Roughcast rendered boundary wall with stone coping to street.

A modest house retaining many original features including rendered entrance fa�ade, which is an integral part of this Georgian terrace of great charm and subtle variety.
