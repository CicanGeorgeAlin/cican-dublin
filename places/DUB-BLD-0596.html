ARCHITECTURE · Clondalkin, County Dublin

Moran's

Orchard Lane, Main Street, Clondalkin, County Dublin, Ireland

Detached two-storey irregular plan public house, c.1850, on an island site. Roughcast and smooth rendered walls. Timber casement windows, some with architrave surrounds to first floor. Recessed timber panelled door. Roof with smooth rendered chimney stacks hidden by parapet.

One of the most conspicuously sited buildings in central Clondalkin, this substantial public house dominates its surroundings and, being visible from many approaches, acts as a focal landmark within the village.
