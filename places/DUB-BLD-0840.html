Knockdromin — gate lodge

BEAU, County Dublin, Ireland

Detached four-bay single-storey gate lodge, c.1900, with gable-fronted projecting entrance porch.ROOF: Hipped slate roof with terracotta ridge tiles and overhanging eaves; nap rendered chimney stacks. WALLS: Rough rendered with nap rendered plinth course. OPENINGS: Square headed splayed rendered reveals; granite cills and probably original timber side hung casements; segmental headed splayed rendered door; opening with rendered hood moulding and timber tongue and groove door

NIAH rating: Regional. Special interest categories: ARCHITECTURAL.
