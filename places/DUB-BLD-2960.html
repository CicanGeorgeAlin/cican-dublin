ATHGOE NORTH — farm house (NIAH 11212024)

ATHGOE NORTH, County Dublin, Ireland

Detached five-bay single-storey farmhouse, c.1860. Roughcast rendered walls. Timber casement windows. Glazed timber door in gable-ended projecting porch. Pitched slate roof with smooth rendered chimney stacks. Cylindrical squared stone gate piers with iron bar half-moon gate to north side of house. Modern outbuildings to north and east.

A simple, unpretentious house, retaining its original proportions, which is part of a group of farm buildings. The cylindrical gate piers and iron gate, both peculiar to this area, and the unusual porch, enhance the vernacular quality of this group.
