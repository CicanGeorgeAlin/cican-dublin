North Wall Quay — western granite quay wall

North Wall Quay, between the Convention Centre and New Wapping Street, Dublin 1

A stretch of granite ashlar quay wall erected around 1800 along the north embankment of the River Liffey. The NIAH records coursed squared granite masonry with granite coping, paired iron mooring hooks set into the coping and three shallow recesses containing iron ladders. A later public-realm refurbishment replaced much of the associated machinery and street furniture, while the hooks and ladders retain evidence of the quay's maritime working use.

Surviving early-nineteenth-century harbour engineering fabric, with mooring fittings and access ladders that preserve a tangible connection to the working Liffey waterfront.
