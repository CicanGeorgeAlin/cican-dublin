Sheriff Street Lifting Bridge — Spencer Dock bascule bridge

Sheriff Street Upper over Spencer Dock, Dublin 1

Single-span bascule bridge installed over Spencer Dock between 1939 and 1941. The NIAH records that it is now fixed and retains a pair of reclaimed cut-limestone panels with rope-twist detailing and the date “1873”, carrying the emblem of the Midland Great Western Railway of Ireland. These panels recall the earlier Spencer Swivel Bridge designed by James Price and opened in 1873, the first mechanical crossing over Spencer Dock. The 1941 bridge was reported to have cost £18,000 and was described at the time as a structure unique of its kind in Ireland.

An important surviving docklands engineering structure that combines a twentieth-century bascule bridge with reused nineteenth-century railway heraldic panels, preserving evidence of two successive mechanical crossings at Spencer Dock.
