Grand Canal Dockyard — former southern graving dock and boat slipway

East side of Grand Canal Basin, north end of South Docks Road, Dublin 4

Former southernmost graving dock built in 1796 as part of Grand Canal Dock. The NIAH records that it was infilled and converted to a boat slipway around 1850. Surviving elements include cut-limestone retaining walls, a rubble-limestone enclosing wall to the south, a former southern gate opening, and a former eastern gate opening with brown-brick surrounds and castellated limestone capping, now infilled with concrete block. The surface includes later concrete paving and a slip ramp.

A distinct dockyard structure whose altered use preserves evidence of the dock's ship-repair history and later industrial adaptation. It should not be conflated with the two former graving docks immediately to its north.
