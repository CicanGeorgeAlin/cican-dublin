McKenny Bridge — former Conyngham Bridge

Junction of Mount Street Lower and Northumberland Road, Dublin 2

Single-arch granite canal bridge built around 1790 to carry the road over the Grand Canal. The NIAH records coursed-granite parapets, stringcourses and copings, projecting piers, a segmental arch with voussoirs and hood-moulding on the east elevation, and flat concrete lintelling with a rebuilt parapet on the widened west side. A carved limestone plaque on the east elevation reads “McKenny Bridge”; a memorial commemorating the 1916 Battle of Mount Street stands to the south-west. The bridge was originally named Conyngham Bridge and later renamed for Thomas McKenny, chairman of the Grand Canal Company.

An important surviving structure from the first decades of the Grand Canal, showing later widening and a layered naming history. Its carved bridge-name plaque and nearby 1916 memorial are separate details to document carefully.
