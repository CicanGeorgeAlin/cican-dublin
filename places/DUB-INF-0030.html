Grand Canal Quay Railway Bridge — Dublin and Kingstown Railway

Grand Canal Quay, Dublin 2

Single-arch, low-profile railway bridge constructed around 1835 to carry the railway over Grand Canal Quay. The NIAH records a segmental arch with dressed-granite voussoirs, cut-granite impost and stringcourses, dressed-limestone parapets and ashlar-limestone spandrels, flared squared snecked-rubble-limestone abutments, and skewed granite masonry continuing through the arch barrel.

Part of Ireland's first railway line, the Dublin and Kingstown Railway. The skewed arch and combination of granite and limestone preserve evidence of early railway engineering alongside the Grand Canal industrial district.
