Lucan-Newlands Road — single-arch railway bridge, Cappagh

Lucan-Newlands Road, Cappagh, County Dublin, Ireland

Single-arch road bridge over the railway, with a three-centred arch and dressed voussoir stones. Coursed squared limestone parapets at road level are raised with coursed squared granite; limestone rubble retaining walls extend to either side.

NIAH appraises the bridge as a finely executed railway crossing whose contrasting raised parapets and stonework enhance the road above.
