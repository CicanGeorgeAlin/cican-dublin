Mack's Bridge — Newbridge Demesne

Mack's Bridge, Newbridge Demesne, Donabate, Fingal, County Dublin, Ireland

Bridge identified as Mack's Bridge in Newbridge Demesne and cross-referenced to NIAH registration 11336004 in Fingal County Council's Donabate–Portrane Heritage Assets Audit. The 2019 Broadmeadow Way EIAR constraints schedule classifies it as a bridge and supplies an explicit ITM locator. A detailed structural description, materials, construction date and current condition were not retrieved in this batch; these are deliberately left unspecified.

Individually identified as an NIAH-registered bridge in the local-authority heritage audit. The NIAH individual record's rating and appraisal were not independently retrieved in this pass.
