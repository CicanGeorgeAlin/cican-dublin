Kilcrea — metal railway bridge between stone piers

Kilcrea railway bridge, Kilcrea, Donabate, Fingal, County Dublin, Ireland

Metal railway bridge spanning between stone piers, described by the official NIAH County Dublin search record as dating to c.1860 with alterations c.1960. The Donabate Local Area Plan separately lists RPS 502 as a railway bridge on Corballis Road, Kilcrea, described as a stone rail bridge over a road. The two entries are a strong possible match, but the cross-reference has not been directly confirmed against the current RPS map and individual NIAH record map.

NIAH rating Regional, with Architectural and Technical interest. It is a distinct bridge record and must not be merged with NIAH 11336014 at Turvey Avenue, a separate single-arch ashlar limestone road bridge over the railway.
