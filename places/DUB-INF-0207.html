Bachelor’s Walk

Bachelor’s Walk, Dublin 1, County Dublin, Ireland

Granite ashlar quay, built c.1800, one of several continuous quays lining north side of River Liffey. Ashlar granite quay walls with saddle-back finish. Modern timber boardwalk on steel framework to river side. Bounded at north side by tarmac pavement.

Historical appraisal

The NIAH appraisal places Bachelor’s Walk within the long history of maritime development along the River Liffey. The quay extends from Ha’penny Bridge to O’Connell Bridge and is distinguished among the city quays by its name. It is recognised as evidence of Dublin’s economic and maritime development as Ireland’s principal port and as an example of nineteenth-century engineering.
