NIAH 11204021 — foot bridge — Lucan

Lucan, Lucan And Pettycanon, County Dublin, Ireland

Single-arch footbridge, c.1800. Segmental arch of course limestone with low parapets, now partly overgrown.

This modest bridge is set in peaceful surroundings and adds simple charm to the overall character of the area.
