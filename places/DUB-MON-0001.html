O'Connell Monument

O'Connell Street Lower, Dublin 1

Bronze monument to Daniel O'Connell, conceived by John Henry Foley in 1866 and completed by Thomas Brock in 1883. It includes a high-relief frieze and multiple figures around a granite plinth.

Major national monument at the southern entrance to O'Connell Street.
