Edmund Burke Statue

Trinity College Dublin, north of main entrance, College Green, Dublin 2

Standing electro-typed copper-bronze figure by J. H. Foley, erected in 1868 on a stepped granite plinth with incised lettering.

Commemorates Edmund Burke (1729–1797), Dublin-born political thinker and Trinity alumnus.
