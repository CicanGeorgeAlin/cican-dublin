Jim Larkin Monument

O'Connell Street Lower, outside the former Clerys building, Dublin 1

Freestanding bronze figure of James ('Big Jim') Larkin with outstretched arms, designed by Oisín Kelly and erected in 1980 on a square-plan tooled granite pedestal. NIAH records gilt lettering and inset bronze plaques on the south, east and west faces.

Commemorates labour leader Jim Larkin and recalls his address to an illegal assembly from a hotel balcony during the 1913 Lockout.
