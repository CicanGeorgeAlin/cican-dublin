Wellington Monument (Wellington Testimonial)

South-eastern Phoenix Park, near Islandbridge Gate, Dublin 8

Freestanding granite obelisk, 62 metres tall, designed by Sir Robert Smirke as a testimonial to Arthur Wellesley, Duke of Wellington. The monument stands on a three-stepped monumental pedestal and a square-plan stylobate. Four bronze plaques cast from cannons captured at Waterloo are set into its faces; three carry bas-reliefs and the eastern face bears a Latin and English inscription. The monument is also known as the Wellington Testimonial.

A nationally rated monumental work and one of the defining nineteenth-century features of Phoenix Park. Its bronze plaques, relief sculpture and inscriptions make it a multi-part visual and epigraphic subject.
