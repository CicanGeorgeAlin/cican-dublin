The Phoenix Monument — Portland-stone Corinthian column and phoenix

Central roundabout, Chesterfield Avenue, Phoenix Park, Dublin 8

Freestanding Portland-stone Corinthian column erected in 1747 and surmounted by a carved stone phoenix rising from the ashes. The full-height fluted shaft has a Corinthian capital and stands on a plinth with inscribed marble plaques to the east and west and carved coats of arms to the north and south. Its stepped granite base is enclosed within a circular platform of stone setts. The monument stands at the central junction of Chesterfield Avenue, Acres Road and Odd Lamp Road.

One of the earliest surviving features of Phoenix Park and a focal point on its principal avenue. The column, phoenix sculpture, plaques, heraldry and surrounding stone-sett platform each merit separate visual documentation.
