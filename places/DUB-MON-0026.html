Hamilton Monument, Strand Street, Skerries

Strand Street, Skerries, County Dublin, Ireland

Freestanding limestone monument erected in memory of James Hans Hamilton, MP, on a rock-faced base. NIAH records an engraved marble inset tablet on each side, a tiered plinth and cast-iron railings on a chamfered plinth.

A Victorian civic memorial with multiple inscribed faces and associated ornamental ironwork, providing a compact record of local commemorative practice.
