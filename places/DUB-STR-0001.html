GPO historic cast-iron lantern bases and wheel guards

In front of the General Post Office, O'Connell Street Lower, Dublin 1

Pair of cast-iron lantern-post bases and six domed wheel-guards recorded by NIAH. Lantern bases are described as likely early nineteenth-century; wheel-guard date is less certain.

Rare surviving early nineteenth-century street furniture associated with the GPO.
