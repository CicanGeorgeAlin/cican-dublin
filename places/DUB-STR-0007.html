Edwardian cast-iron pillar post box, Lower Abbey Street

North side of Lower Abbey Street, Dublin 1

Freestanding cast-iron pillar post box with round plan, shallow domed cap, moulded corona, neck moulding, cylindrical shaft and moulded plinth base. Raised POST OFFICE lettering and royal insignia with crown motif reading ER VII; raised maker's lettering identifies Handyside. NIAH records it on the north side of Abbey Street Lower.

A surviving Edwardian post box and working piece of cast-iron street furniture associated with the final decade of British rule in Ireland.
