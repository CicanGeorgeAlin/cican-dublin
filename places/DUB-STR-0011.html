Edward VII cast-iron pillar post box, North Circular Road

South side of North Circular Road outside No. 530, Dublin 1

Cast-iron pillar box with Edward VII royal insignia, a fluted frieze below the cap, a letter aperture flanked by raised POST OFFICE lettering, and an E VII R monogram with crown motif on the door. The base bears the maker's mark Handyside Derby & London. NIAH records green paint and a black base.

A surviving Edwardian postal artefact that remained in use in the NIAH survey record, retaining royal insignia and a named manufacturer.
