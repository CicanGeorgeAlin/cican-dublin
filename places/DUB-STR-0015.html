Early Free State double post box, Dame Street

North footpath of Dame Street, south of the Central Bank, Dublin 2

Freestanding cast-iron double post box with oval plan, shallow domed cap, fluted cornice, moulded neck and plinth. Two letter slots are framed by raised POST OFFICE lettering; a P&T insignia occupies the central front panel, with curved doors on the side elevations.

A well-preserved example of the early Free State double pillar-box design. The paired apertures indicate high postal demand, while the Celtic-style P&T lettering reflects state identity in public infrastructure.
