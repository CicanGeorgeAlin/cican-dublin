P&T double-opening post box with Áth Cliath lettering, Nassau Street

Nassau Street, Dublin 2

Freestanding oval-plan cast-iron pillar box with double letter opening, shallow domed cap and moulded corona. It carries raised Post Office and Next Collection lettering, a P&T motif and enamel Áth Cliath (Dublin) lettering above the aperture. No maker's mark was visible in the NIAH record.

A post-Independence double-opening pillar box with bilingual place lettering. NIAH records that it was temporarily painted red for the 1916 centenary, making its surface history another fieldwork detail to investigate.
