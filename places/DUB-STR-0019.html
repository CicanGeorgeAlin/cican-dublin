Tonge & Taggart cast-iron pillar post box, South Richmond Street

South Richmond Street at Lennox Street, Dublin 2

Freestanding cast-iron pillar post box with shallow domed cap, moulded neck and cornice. The south-facing door carries a Post logo and collection-notice panel; the rear north-facing panel bears the Tonge & Taggart Ltd Dublin foundry mark.

A late twentieth-century continuation of Dublin's cast-iron post-box tradition. Its local Dublin foundry mark is especially notable; the NIAH appraisal notes that An Post replaced the P&T postal-service name in 1984 and that this traditional-form box followed soon after.
