Edward VII cast-iron pillar post box, Merrion Square

Merrion Square near the junction with Merrion Street Upper, Dublin 2

Freestanding cylindrical cast-iron pillar post box with shallow domed cap, fluted cornice, moulded neck and plinth. The letter aperture is set in a raised frame above a curved door bearing Edward VII's royal cipher and crown; the plinth carries the Handyside, Derby & London maker's mark.

A well-preserved Edwardian pillar box associated with the final decades of British rule. Its royal cipher, maker's mark and continued postal use give it both material and functional heritage value.
