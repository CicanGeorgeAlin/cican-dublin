Howth Road — Victorian wall-mounted cast-iron postbox

Howth Road, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Wall-mounted cast-iron postbox cast circa 1890, set into an uncoursed limestone wall south of Howth Road. The indexed NIAH register description records the raised royal cipher of Queen Victoria and crown motif on the head, with 'Post Office' in cast lettering on the letter flap. The foundry mark at the base is recorded as illegible.

A surviving Victorian postal artifact. Its royal cipher provides visible evidence of the period in which the box was manufactured, while its position in a limestone wall documents a different suburban postbox form from freestanding pillar boxes.
