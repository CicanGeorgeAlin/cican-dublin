Mount Prospect Avenue — Carron cast-iron P&T pillar postbox

Mount Prospect Avenue, Clontarf, Dublin 3

Freestanding cast-iron pillar postbox erected circa 1940 and set in a lawned verge in a residential area. NIAH describes a shallow domed cap with fluted rim, moulded neck and plinth, raised 'Post Office' insignia on the front door and Carron Company Stirlingshire maker's insignia on the front of the plinth. The appraisal identifies P&T insignia as dating the box to the early years of the Irish state.

An example of post-Independence postal infrastructure with a maker's mark linking it to Carron Ltd of Stirlingshire. Its Gaelic P&T lettering and cast-iron detailing record both national identity and industrial manufacture.
