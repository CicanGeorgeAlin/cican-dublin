St Anne’s Park — shell house with ‘The House that Jack built’ pier

Within the walled garden at St Anne’s Park, Raheny, Dublin 5

A square-plan rubble-stone shell house within the walled garden. The NIAH records groin-vaulted stone arches, rounded wall corners, interior niches decorated with pebbles and shells, and niches incorporating animal bone and skull material. A central freestanding two-stage pier carries projecting basins and pebble-and-shell decoration; lettering below the basins reads “The House that Jack built”, with a dog figure on the lower stage.

An unusually personal ornamental garden structure within the former St Anne’s estate. Its shellwork, bone decoration, lettering and figurative detail make it a particularly rich visual record of nineteenth-century garden craft and estate leisure.
