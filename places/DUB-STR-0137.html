Howth Road, Dublin 3 — c.1825 granite GPO / Howth milestone

North side of Howth Road, Dublin 3, Ireland

Dressed granite milestone with chamfered sides, recorded by the National Inventory of Architectural Heritage as one of a series along the north-side footpath of Howth Road. Its inset cast-iron plate reads “P.O/Dublin 3/Howth 6”; a benchmark is visible on the south-west face.

A surviving example of the early nineteenth-century distance-marking system associated with the improved mail route between Dublin’s General Post Office and the port and mail station at Howth. The NIAH identifies the series with Thomas Telford’s road-improvement programme.
