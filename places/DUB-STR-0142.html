Malahide Road, Artane — c.1820 granite GPO / Malahide milestone and surround

Malahide Road, Artane, Dublin 3, Ireland

Granite milestone with a tapered body and pointed head. An inset angle-headed cast-iron plate carries raised contrasting lettering reading “GPO Dublin 3 / Malahide 6”. The marker stands within a roughcast cement-rendered segmental-headed surround with rectangular piers and concrete coping rising from dwarf walls along Malahide Road.

An important survivor of the early post-road waymarking system in Dublin. The distinctive raised surround makes this marker unusually prominent and illustrates how milestones were incorporated into roadside structures to guide mail and other travellers.
