James’s Street — Francis Sandys 1790 drinking fountain and sundial obelisk

James’s Street, Dublin 8, Ireland

Freestanding drinking fountain comprising a fluted Portland-stone obelisk with projecting block and oval sundials on each face, ball finial, painted masonry plinth with carved human masks, cast-iron wall fountains on the north, south and west elevations, and a plaque on the east. The fountain is surrounded by cast-iron bollards and was restored in 1995.

Designed by Francis Sandys, this is an early example of decorative public water infrastructure. The carved masks originally discharged water into a basin; the cast-iron fountains by T. Kennedy of Kilmarnock were later additions. Its sundials and carved stonework combine utility, craftsmanship and public-space history.
