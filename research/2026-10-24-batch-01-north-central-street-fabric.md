# Research batch 01 — North-central Dublin street fabric

**Research date:** 24 October 2026  
**Scope:** O’Connell Street north end and Commons Street / former Custom House docks edge  
**Outcome:** Two source-supported archive records added; one wider historic street-surface research programme registered for follow-up.

## Added records

### DUB-STR-0005 — Four Victorian cast-iron bollards, north end of O’Connell Street
- NIAH registration 50010556; circa 1880.
- Four ornate cast-iron bollards on the central mall, with scrollwork, pierced openings and domed foliate caps.
- Published grid reference: 315817, 234965.
- NIAH suggests a possible original air-vent function; this is not certain.
- Documentary record verified; current condition and founder photography outstanding.

### DUB-STR-0006 — Historic Custom House Docks boundary wall on Commons Street
- NIAH registration 50060568; circa 1830, with later alterations.
- Limestone and granite wall associated with the former Custom House docks complex.
- NIAH also records an adjacent historic cobbled street surface and granite kerbs.
- Published grid reference: 316869, 234776.
- The French-prisoner story is folklore, not confirmed provenance.
- Documentary record verified; current condition and founder photography outstanding.

## Major research lead: historic street surfaces
Dublin City Council’s Heritage Plan review summarises a city-wide historic street-surfaces inventory and a 2009 conservation study. It reports 27 streets with complete granite-flagged pavements, approximately 118 streets with surviving kerbstones, 37 streets with complete setted surfaces (some modern reinstatements), and a 2010 resurvey identifying 78 streets/lanes with setted surfaces.

These are historical survey figures, not a current count and not additive unique-street totals. The 2010 survey captured material, joint type, bond pattern, uniformity/intactness, authenticity and condition. Next step: locate the underlying schedules and appendices, extract named streets and compare them with current mapping and field evidence. Do not create guessed street records from aggregate counts.
