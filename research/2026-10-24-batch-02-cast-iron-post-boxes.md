# Research batch 02 — Dublin 1 cast-iron post boxes

**Research date:** 24 October 2026  
**Scope:** Individually recorded cast-iron post boxes in Dublin 1.  
**Outcome:** Six new archive records and six official NIAH source entries added.

## Discoveries

### DUB-STR-0007 — Lower Abbey Street Edwardian pillar box
- NIAH 50010291; circa 1905.
- Cast iron, ER VII insignia, POST OFFICE lettering and Handyside maker's mark.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316132, 234580.

### DUB-STR-0008 — Jervis Street early Free State twin-slot box
- NIAH 50010458; circa 1930.
- Two apertures, enamelled notices, Gaelic P & T lettering; east side of Jervis Street near Mary Street.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 315482, 234563.

### DUB-STR-0009 — O'Connell Street Lower P&T double box
- NIAH 50010515; circa 1940.
- Oval cast-iron double box, P&T insignia and paired letter apertures.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 315972, 234488.

### DUB-STR-0010 — O'Connell Street Upper twin box
- NIAH 50010555; circa 1930.
- Twin apertures and Gaelic P & T lettering; north end of O'Connell Street at Cathal Brugha Street.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 315851, 234897.
- Full NIAH record opened and checked during this batch.

### DUB-STR-0011 — North Circular Road Edward VII pillar box
- NIAH 50060629; circa 1905.
- E VII R insignia, fluted frieze and Handyside Derby & London maker's mark; outside No. 530.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316297, 235613.

### DUB-STR-0012 — Mountjoy Square West Victorian pillar box
- NIAH 50010954; circa 1890.
- VR insignia and Handyside & Co maker's lettering; southwest corner of Mountjoy Square.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316022, 235339.

## Coordinate handling

Each NIAH Irish Grid reference was converted to WGS 84 using TM65 / Irish Grid (EPSG:29902), transformation operation 1641. Decimal coordinates and coordinate-based Google Maps walking URLs were added. The transformation's published accuracy does not establish the accuracy of the original mapped point; all six remain SOURCE_DERIVED pending founder field verification.

## Fieldwork priorities

- Verify that each post box remains at its recorded location.
- Photograph the full object, royal or P&T insignia, maker marks, letter apertures, cap, neck, plinth and current condition.
- Record any discrepancies between the NIAH point and the actual object position.
- Do not mark a coordinate FIELD_VERIFIED until checked on site.

## Integrity note

The new records are source-based catalogue entries. The NIAH pages for several registrations returned access-denied responses to direct page opening, so their indexed official register data was used; the O'Connell Street Upper record was opened in full. Current presence, exact object-level pin position and condition remain fieldwork tasks.
