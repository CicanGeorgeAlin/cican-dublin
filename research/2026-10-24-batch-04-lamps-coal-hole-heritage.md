# Research batch 04 — Dublin 1 and Dublin 2 lamp standards and coal-hole heritage

**Research date:** 24 October 2026  
**Scope:** NIAH-recorded lamp standards and Georgian/Victorian buildings retaining historic coal-hole covers, boot scrapers, railings and granite entrance fabric.  
**Outcome:** Eight new archive records and eight official NIAH source entries added.

## New records

### DUB-STR-0027 — College Street Art Deco concrete electric lamp standard
NIAH 50020523, c.1935. A rare reinforced-concrete double-headed electric standard with copper-roofed frosted-glass lanterns. NIAH places it at the east end of the north side of College Street. Grid reference 316098, 234221.

### DUB-STR-0028 — The Five Lamps
NIAH 50010060, c.1880. A cast-iron five-lamp standard and former fountain with lion-head water spouts, filled-in basins and crown-motif lanterns. Converted to electricity c.1900; located on a traffic island at Amiens Street/North Strand. Grid reference 316793, 235262.

### DUB-BLD-0011 — 23 Hatch Street Lower
NIAH 50110521, c.1810. Georgian house with granite pavior and a cast-iron coal-hole cover attributed by NIAH to the South City Foundry, located nearby on Bishop Street. Grid reference 316162, 232885.

### DUB-BLD-0012 — Harrington Hall, 69 Harcourt Street
NIAH 50920250, c.1800. Townhouse frontage with cast-iron boot scrapers, railings, coal-hole cover and lamp standards on granite steps. Grid reference 315762, 233015.

### DUB-BLD-0013 — 67 Harcourt Street
NIAH 50920252, 1862. Victorian townhouse with Romanesque Revival doorway, granite platform, cast-iron boot scraper, railings and coal-hole cover. Grid reference 315763, 232999.

### DUB-BLD-0014 — 37 Harcourt Street
NIAH 50920231, c.1800, partially rebuilt 1915. Retains Georgian frontage, granite platform, cast-iron railings and coal-hole cover. Grid reference 315728, 233009.

### DUB-BLD-0015 — 82 Amiens Street
NIAH 50010086, c.1832. House with original cast-iron coal-hole cover in a granite slab, cast-iron bootscraper, granite platform and railings. Grid reference 316791, 235200.

### DUB-BLD-0016 — The Moorings, 13 Charleville Mall
NIAH 50010072, c.1830. Canal-facing house with granite entrance platform, cast-iron bootscraper, wrought-iron railings, urn-finial corner posts and coal-hole cover in a granite setting. Grid reference 316855, 235487.

## Important coordinate qualification

For the two lamp standards, the published NIAH point locates the object/site. For the six buildings, the coordinate locates the building, not the exact coal-hole cover or boot-scraper centre. These records therefore guide the founder to the property frontage; exact child-feature pins must be captured on site before separate micro-detail records are created. All coordinates remain SOURCE_DERIVED.

## Fieldwork priorities

- Photograph the College Street electric lamp standard, especially the copper roofs, frosted glass and exposed-aggregate concrete.
- Photograph The Five Lamps from safe public viewpoints; do not enter the traffic island.
- At the six building frontages, document coal-hole covers, maker marks, boot scrapers, railings, granite steps and entrance platforms.
- For the coal-hole cover at 23 Hatch Street Lower, look for evidence supporting the South City Foundry attribution.
- Record exact cover and boot-scraper GPS only during fieldwork; do not reuse the building coordinate as a separate object pin.
