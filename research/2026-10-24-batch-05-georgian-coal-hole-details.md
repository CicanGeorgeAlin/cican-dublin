# Research batch 05 — Dublin 1 and Dublin 2 Georgian coal-hole and entrance details

**Research date:** 24 October 2026  
**Scope:** NIAH-recorded Georgian and Victorian buildings retaining coal-hole covers, bootscrapers, granite kerbing, basement railings and entrance ironwork.  
**Outcome:** Fourteen new archive records and fourteen official NIAH source entries added.

## New records

### DUB-BLD-0017 — 11 Charleville Mall — Georgian frontage and coal-hole cover
- NIAH 50010070; Circa 1830.
- Terraced two-bay two-storey house over raised basement, built c.1830 as one of a terrace of eight facing the Royal Canal. NIAH records a granite-flagged platform, cast-iron bootscraper, granite steps, wrought-iron railings, cast-iron corner posts with urn finials, and a cast-iron coal-hole cover in a granite setting within the footpath.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316865, 235478.

### DUB-BLD-0018 — 12 Charleville Mall — Georgian house with coal-hole cover
- NIAH 50010071; Circa 1830.
- Terraced two-bay two-storey house over raised basement, built c.1830. NIAH records six-over-six timber sliding sash windows, a granite-flagged platform, cast-iron bootscraper, granite stepped approach, wrought-iron railings, cast-iron corner posts with urn finials, and a cast-iron coal-hole cover in a granite setting to the concrete footpath.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316860, 235483.

### DUB-BLD-0019 — 14 Charleville Mall — Georgian entrance and coal-hole cover
- NIAH 50010073; Circa 1830.
- Terraced two-bay two-storey house over raised basement, built c.1830. NIAH records a granite-flagged platform, cast-iron bootscraper, stepped granite approach, wrought-iron railings on a moulded granite plinth, cast-iron corner posts with urn finials, and a cast-iron coal-hole cover in a granite setting to the footpath.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316850, 235491.

### DUB-BLD-0020 — 15 Charleville Mall — paired bootscrapers and coal-hole cover
- NIAH 50010074; Circa 1830.
- Terraced two-bay two-storey house over raised basement, built c.1830. NIAH records a granite-flagged approach with a pair of cast-iron bootscrapers, granite steps shared with the neighbouring house, wrought-iron railings, cast-iron corner posts with urn finials, and a cast-iron coal-hole cover in a granite setting in the concrete footpath.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316846, 235495.

### DUB-BLD-0021 — 2 Buckingham Street Lower — Henshaw coal-hole covers
- NIAH 50011162; Circa 1795.
- Terraced three-bay four-storey house over exposed basement, built c.1795. NIAH records a tiled threshold and platform with a cast-iron bootscraper, a pair of cast-iron coal-hole covers set in granite slabs, granite kerbing, and a moulded granite plinth with wrought-iron railings. The appraisal attributes the coal-hole cover to Henshaw of Dublin.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316649, 235060.

### DUB-BLD-0022 — 1A Sherrard Street Upper — Victorian frontage and coal-hole cover
- NIAH 50010750; Circa 1890.
- Terraced four-bay three-storey single-pile house over exposed basement, built c.1890. NIAH records a granite platform and steps, original wrought-iron railings, cast-iron corner posts, and a cast-iron coal-hole cover set in a granite slab with granite kerbing.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 315860, 235653.

### DUB-BLD-0023 — 60 Amiens Street — original coal-hole cover in granite slab
- NIAH 50010104; Circa 1810.
- Terraced two-bay three-storey house over raised basement, built c.1810. NIAH records a granite entrance platform and three granite steps bridging the basement area, together with an original cast-iron coal-hole cover set in a granite slab in the front pavement.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316704, 235131.

### DUB-BLD-0024 — 17–18 Nassau Street — Georgian house with coal-hole cover and basement light
- NIAH 50100085; Circa 1750.
- Attached three-bay four-storey former house over concealed basement, built c.1750 as part of a five-bay block. NIAH records a cast-iron basement light and a cast-iron coal-hole cover set in granite paving at the junction of Nassau Street and South Frederick Street.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316200, 233856.

### DUB-BLD-0025 — 3 Mountjoy Square North — Georgian house with historic political use and coal-hole cover
- NIAH 50010820; Circa 1797.
- Terraced three-bay four-storey house over raised basement, built c.1797 as one of a pair. NIAH records a granite platform and five granite steps, cast-iron bootscraper, original wrought-iron railings, cast-iron corner posts, basement gate and cast-iron coal-hole cover in the pavement. The house was used for meetings of the banned First Dáil in 1919.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 315980, 235494.

### DUB-BLD-0026 — 585 North Circular Road — coal-hole cover and basement ironwork
- NIAH 50060408; Circa 1820.
- Terraced two-bay three-storey former house over basement, built c.1820. NIAH records a concrete entrance platform with cast-iron bootscraper, a basement well enclosed by a painted granite plinth and wrought-iron railings with cast-iron newel posts and urn finials, and a cast-iron coal-hole cover set in a granite flagstone to the front pavement.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316388, 235615.


## Notable discoveries

- **2 Buckingham Street Lower:** a pair of cast-iron coal-hole covers in granite slabs; the NIAH appraisal attributes the cover to Henshaw of Dublin.
- **83 Amiens Street:** identified in NIAH research as having an original coal-hole cover inscribed “Henshaw Dublin”; retained as a future lead because this address has not yet been added in this batch.
- **Charleville Mall:** a sequence of Georgian houses with coal-hole covers, granite entrance platforms, cast-iron bootscrapers, wrought-iron railings and urn-finial corner posts, facing the Royal Canal.
- **3 Mountjoy Square North:** the NIAH record combines a coal-hole cover and entrance details with the historic association of meetings of the banned First Dáil in 1919.
- **17–18 Nassau Street:** a cast-iron basement light and coal-hole cover survive together in granite paving.

## Coordinate qualification

The coordinates are NIAH building/site points converted from published Irish Grid references. They take the founder to the property frontage; they do **not** pinpoint the individual coal-hole cover or bootscraper. Separate micro-detail records and precise object-level coordinates must wait until the founder photographs and GPS-checks each feature in the field.

## Fieldwork priorities

- Photograph each property frontage from the public footpath.
- Photograph coal-hole covers, any maker's marks, granite settings and kerbing.
- Record bootscrapers, railings, corner posts, urn finials and entrance platforms.
- For 2 Buckingham Street Lower, look for Henshaw of Dublin marks on both covers.
- For 17–18 Nassau Street, distinguish the basement light from the coal-hole cover.
- Never enter private property or school premises without permission.
