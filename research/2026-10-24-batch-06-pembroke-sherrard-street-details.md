# Research batch 06 — Pembroke Street and Sherrard Street pavement details

**Research date:** 24 October 2026  
**Scope:** NIAH-recorded buildings in Dublin 1 and Dublin 2 retaining coal-hole covers, bootscrapers, original paving and entrance ironwork.  
**Outcome:** Five new archive records and five official NIAH source entries added.

## New records

### DUB-BLD-0031 — 1 Pembroke Street Upper — Georgian embassy frontage with coal-hole cover
- NIAH 50930231; Circa 1825.
- Terraced three-bay four-storey over-basement former townhouse, built c.1825 and now used as an embassy. NIAH records a granite entrance platform, cast-iron bootscraper, five granite steps, cast-iron railings over a granite plinth enclosing basement wells, and a coal-hole cover to the pavement.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316386, 233025.

### DUB-BLD-0032 — 14 Pembroke Street Lower — Victorian townhouse with coal-hole cover
- NIAH 50930035; Circa 1850.
- Attached two-bay four-storey former townhouse over basement, built c.1850 and now used as offices. NIAH records cast-iron balconettes, a granite entrance platform and four granite steps, iron railings with finials on a granite plinth enclosing the basement, and a coal-hole cover to the pavement.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316453, 233219.

### DUB-BLD-0033 — 13 Pembroke Street Upper — Georgian townhouse with coal-hole cover
- NIAH 50930242; Circa 1825.
- Terraced two-bay four-storey over-basement former townhouse, built c.1825 and now used as offices. NIAH records a granite entrance platform, four granite steps, iron railings with decorative cast-iron corner posts enclosing the basement area, and a coal-hole cover to the pavement.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316318, 232970.

### DUB-BLD-0034 — 4 Sherrard Street Lower — original granite paving and coal-hole cover
- NIAH 50010727; Circa 1830.
- Terraced two-bay three-storey house over raised basement, built c.1830. NIAH records an original sandstone-paved platform, cast-iron bootscraper, four granite steps, decorative spear-headed wrought- and cast-iron railings on a moulded granite plinth, original granite paving and a cast-iron coal-hole cover to the street.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316008, 235753.

### DUB-BLD-0035 — 619 North Circular Road — Georgian frontage and cast-iron entrance ironwork
- NIAH 50011089; Circa 1830.
- Terraced two-bay three-storey house over exposed basement, built c.1830 and now used as offices. NIAH records a concrete entrance platform with cast-iron bootscraper, original wrought-iron railings and cast-iron corner posts on a granite plinth. The house forms part of a terrace following the curve of the North Circular Road.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316567, 235543.


## Research notes

- Pembroke Street Upper and Lower retain several coal-hole covers alongside granite platforms, steps, railings and cast-iron balconettes.
- 4 Sherrard Street Lower is especially notable for original granite paving and an associated coal-hole cover.
- 619 North Circular Road adds another example of original cast-iron entrance fittings and wrought-iron frontage work.

## Coordinate qualification

The coordinates are NIAH building/site points converted from published Irish Grid references. They take the founder to the property frontage; they do not pinpoint individual coal-hole covers or bootscrapers. Separate micro-detail records and precise object-level coordinates must wait until the founder photographs and GPS-checks each feature in the field.

## Fieldwork priorities

- Photograph each frontage from the public footpath.
- Record coal-hole covers, maker's marks, granite settings and paving.
- Photograph bootscrapers, railings, cast-iron corner posts and balconettes.
- For embassy and office frontages, respect security and private access restrictions.
- Never create a separate micro-detail pin by reusing the building's coordinate.
