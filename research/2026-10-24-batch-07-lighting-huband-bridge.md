# Research batch 07 — Dublin street lighting and Huband Bridge

**Research date:** 24 October 2026  
**Scope:** NIAH-recorded lamp standards and associated historic street infrastructure in Dublin 1 and Dublin 2.  
**Outcome:** Four new archive records and four official NIAH source entries added.

## New records

### DUB-BLD-0036 — 50 Middle Abbey Street — Georgian house with Harte-marked lamp standards
- NIAH 50010393; Circa 1750.
- Terraced two-bay four-storey house over exposed basement, built c.1750. NIAH records a granite entrance platform and two granite steps, with a lamp standard to either side of the entrance area. The bases bear the manufacturer's name Harte. The façade appears to have been rebuilt during the early twentieth century, possibly following damage during the 1916 Rising.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 315744, 234459.

### DUB-BLD-0037 — 18 Pembroke Street Upper — Georgian frontage with lamp standard and coal-hole cover
- NIAH 50930293; Circa 1825.
- Attached two-bay four-storey over-basement former townhouse, built c.1825 and now used as offices. NIAH records a granite entrance platform and four granite steps, iron railings with decorative cast-iron corner posts, a lamp standard mounted over the granite plinth, and a coal-hole cover to the pavement.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316299, 232993.

### DUB-STR-0038 — Art Deco double-headed concrete electric lamp standard, Pearse Street
- NIAH 50020527; Circa 1935.
- Freestanding reinforced-concrete electric lamp standard with octagonal-profile tapered stepped bases and shafts. Double-headed arched stepped brackets carry suspended octagonal-profile lamps with copper roofs and frosted glass. NIAH identifies it as one of the Dublin Corporation standards installed across the city centre between 1936 and 1939.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 316136, 234204.

### DUB-INF-0002 — Huband Bridge — 1791 Grand Canal bridge and parapet lamp standard
- NIAH 50100594; 1791.
- Single-arch humpbacked bridge spanning the Grand Canal, built in 1791. The bridge has an ashlar granite arch and parapet walls over dressed limestone piers, with cast-iron balustrades between panelled limestone piers. A central limestone plaque bears the name Huband Bridge and the date 1791; a cast-iron lamp standard stands over the north parapet coping. A canal lock lies nearby.
- Published Irish Grid reference: 317136, 233192.


## Research observations

- 50 Middle Abbey Street preserves two entrance lamp standards with the maker's name Harte cast into their bases.
- 18 Pembroke Street Upper combines a parapet/entrance lamp standard with a coal-hole cover and decorative cast-iron railings.
- Pearse Street has a second documented example of Dublin Corporation's rare c.1935 Art Deco concrete electric lighting standards, complementing the College Street example.
- Huband Bridge is a 1791 Grand Canal crossing with an inscribed date plaque, cast-iron balustrades and a lamp standard on the north parapet.

## Coordinate qualification

The coordinates are NIAH building or structure points converted from published Irish Grid references. For building records, the point locates the property rather than the exact entrance lamp or coal-hole cover. The Huband Bridge and street-lamp records are structure-level points. All remain SOURCE_DERIVED until checked on site.
