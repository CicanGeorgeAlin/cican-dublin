# CICAN DUBLIN — Partially Verified Foundation Records: NIAH Bulk Check

Date: 27 October 2026
Scope: All catalogue records currently marked PARTIALLY_VERIFIED (28 records).

## Purpose
The 28 records below rely on indexed NIAH content or a limited official-register extract; direct opening of some individual NIAH pages returned HTTP 403 during the current research pass. The official NIAH Data Download page describes a Dublin CSV containing registration-number-indexed location, classification, rating, description and appraisal fields, plus links to records and images. The national CSV distribution is listed as updated 9 July 2026. The official NIAH ArcGIS Feature Service also exposes a JSON-queryable buildings layer.

Official routes:
- NIAH CSV download: https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/niah-data-download/
- NIAH live Feature Service: https://services-eu1.arcgis.com/HyjXgkV6KGMSF3jt/ArcGIS/rest/services/NIAHBuildingsOpenData/FeatureServer
- NIAH Historic Environment Viewer: https://www.archaeology.ie/advice-and-support/locate-a-monument-or-wreck/historic-environment-viewer/

## Required verification procedure
1. Obtain the latest Dublin CSV or query the NIAHBuildings layer by the registration numbers below.
2. Match registration number, location, classification, rating, date, full Description and Appraisal against the catalogue record.
3. Compare official ITM, National Grid and latitude/longitude fields; do not substitute an approximate or different coordinate system.
4. Check the live NIAH record link and image links, where available. Do not assume NIAH photographs are freely reusable; check image-specific rights separately.
5. Record exact source fields and access date in the source registry. Upgrade a record from PARTIALLY_VERIFIED only when its identifying and descriptive claims are supported by the full official record or another independent authoritative source.
6. Keep micro-details as PARENT_BUILDING_REFERENCE until the exact feature position is independently surveyed.
